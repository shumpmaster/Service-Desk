# Ruling needed: How the automatic job's small pull requests show on the desk

Milestone: MD2

WHY: Most of your Chief of Staff's work, and every question it files, now goes through a small pull request that the automatic job merges, usually within minutes, sometimes hours later. While one is open, the desk shows it as a merge waiting on you and stops reading "Quiet", though the job merges it; a filed question is asked in the session and reaches your Queue once merged. Once merged, these pile up on the Timeline as work tied to no milestone.
OPTIONS:
  A. No change — While one is open, it shows as a merge waiting on you, and "Quiet" waits until the job merges it. Once merged, it stays on the Timeline: most sit under "Not tied to a milestone", and one that names a milestone sits under it as "not yet in the plan". Nothing to build.
  B. Off the Timeline once merged — While one is open, as A. Once merged, the job's pull requests no longer show as work on the Timeline; the questions and answers they carry still show in the Queue and in "Done today". A small change to how the desk reads merged pull requests: no extra requests, though each merged pull request's description is sent twice.
  C. B, and not counted as waiting on you while open — The desk would also read how each open pull request's checks are going, every minute, and which files it changes, whenever they change: reads it does not make today. That adds to what counts against GitHub's hourly limit and means redoing the desk's daily budget sums, and it still cannot see checks from apps other than GitHub's own. It partly takes the "Desk reads unmerged asks" route you passed over when you chose "Agents merge asks" ("That's more desk work, and it ties the desk to how pull requests are named"); this would tie the desk to the job's rules instead. And for up to 30 minutes it could hide a pull request you must merge yourself, such as a defaulted ruling, or an answer missing its Proxy line or with a Milestone line that differs from its question's, since the desk can't read those answers before they merge; after 30 minutes it shows as waiting on you again.
RECOMMENDATION: B — it clears the Timeline with no extra reads. The false "waiting on you" stays, but only while a pull request is open, usually minutes; stopping it (C) means much more reading by the desk and could still hide something you must merge.
RISK CLASS: R0   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: the desk's trial view only: its Queue's Merges group, who has the ball, "Quiet" and the Timeline; the views you accepted and every record stay as they are
DEFAULT: B   TIMEOUT: 48
