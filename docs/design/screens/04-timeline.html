Screen 4 of 7: Timeline

One line through each phase's work, in the order it happened, modelled on your Structured screenshot. Milestones head their part of the line. Reviews, failures, your decisions and stalls sit on it where they happened, and repeat passes are labelled as numbered iterations. Tap a phase at the top to move between phases, and tap any step for a plain-English summary. There are no clock times or durations: the line shows only order.

What's on this screen, and why

  • Structured's week strip becomes the six phases. The coral circle is the phase on screen. The dots under each phase show its milestones: green when done, coral for the one under way, an empty ring when not started. Tap any phase to see its line.
  • Structured's tall capsule marks a milestone, in plain English, with its own colour carried down the dashed line. Round pills are steps, coloured by who acted:
    • coral: you;
    • blue: an agent;
    • green: a check that passed;
    • red: a check that failed;
    • purple: a change to merge;
    • amber: a blocker.
    The circle on the right says whether each step is done, failed, waiting on you, or still to come.
  • Structured's notes in the gaps carry the story: the day changes, "Iteration 2 · after your re-specify" when work went back round, and "Nothing moved for 2 h 32 min" where it stalled.
  • Confirmation on the timeline. In Design, the last step is waiting on you: PR #61, your saved screenshots. Tap it, then "I merged it". The step ticks off where it sits, and the next step lights up. That's the feedback you asked for, shown in place.
  • Real history, plain words. Every step comes from Service Desk's records from 1 to 10 October, and each popup says what happened, why, and what it means for you. Upcoming steps are greyed, and Operate and Review honestly say nothing is planned yet.
