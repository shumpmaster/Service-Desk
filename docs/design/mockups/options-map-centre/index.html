The map's window in the centre

Your comment on v19, on the map's window:

"What are your thoughts on always pulling the highlighted section to the center of the slider so you can see the before and after?"

I think it's a good idea. Today the window sits wherever the events in view fall, so near the newest events it hugs the right edge and you see only what came before. Centred, what came before fills the left side and what comes after fills the right. The cost: each side is squeezed by its own count, so where the window sits no longer says how far along the milestone you are. Here M6 has 17 events before the newest ones, so the left side gets tight, and its sprint names can crowd.

Each phone opens on the timeline project with M6 selected. Scroll inside it: at the top you're at the planned end, further down you reach the stop and the reviews.
