Status for events and steps

You said only phases, milestones and sprints get gauges, and events need different status indicators. Today, in v12, an event shows its kind’s icon and its state as a grey word (Done, Failed, Waiting on you), and a step says “5 of 6 done · 1 blocked” in its heading.

Then three ways to show an event’s status, under S-011, with the sprint wearing the tinted gauge you chose, now a little thinner. A step (the agent’s plan between events) is treated like an event. The colours are the gauges’ soft green, yellow and red.

A · The state word in colour

The kind’s icon stays as it is. The state word beside it takes a soft tint: yellow for waiting on you, red for failed or blocked. Done stays grey, so colour only marks what needs a look.
