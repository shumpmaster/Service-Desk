# Ruling needed: When the desk cannot read the record of when cards were raised, should it hide decision cards and say so, show the ones that have notes, or show them all at once?

Milestone: M4

WHY: Something failed: the Critic has failed the card fix's new spec at both of its reviews, and the second time its first reason was this point. The next review is the third, and a third failure stops the work and brings it back to you. The plan says that when the desk has not read the record of when each card was raised, a decision card shows at once. Your re-specify ruling says a card is held or shown only from the records on main, so every view and the sender agree. The two cannot both hold: a card shown because one read missed that record would be hidden again by the next read, and the desk and the notifications could disagree. The rewritten spec follows your ruling, and its writer marks this as needing your answer before the spec is frozen. Whatever you choose, one thing is the same: while the desk cannot read the list of cards waiting or the notes themselves, it shows no decision card and says so on the queue and Home, with no time limit, which is an exception to your preference that no card is hidden for more than a day, and those hours cannot be counted as misses. The plan was approved at its gate, so changing it needs your ruling. All the options can be undone. If you do not answer within 72 hours, I adopt my recommendation, B.
OPTIONS:
  In every option: while a read of the desk or of the notification sender cannot get the list of cards waiting or the notes, it shows no decision card, counts none on Home, puts none on the board, offers no answer buttons and sends no notice for one, and the queue and Home say which record could not be read; once they are read, each card is held or shown by the plan's rules. There is no other choice that keeps your ruling: without the list of cards waiting the desk does not know a card exists, and without the notes, showing a card would risk the wrong mark and the views disagreeing. There is no time limit on this, so it makes an exception to the plan's promise that no card is held for good and to your preference that none is hidden for more than a day; how often or for how long it happens is not known. The weekly value check cannot count these as misses, because a failed read leaves no record behind, so the plan's meaning of a miss is narrowed to leave them out; the line on the desk at the time is the only sign. This fix does not change how gate cards, questions and pull requests behave. The options differ only in what happens when the record of when cards were raised cannot be read:
  A. Hide every decision card and say so — the same as for the queue and the notes: no decision card is shown, and the line says the record of when cards were raised could not be read. This is what the rewritten spec says now, apart from what every option changes in it: it counts these cards as misses, which the records cannot show, and its replay of past records must allow for them.
     Cost: while that record cannot be read, you see no decision card at all, only the line, with no time limit. Rules out: a card shown only because a read missed that record, then hidden again by the next read; and views that disagree.
  B. Show the cards that have notes, hide the rest and say so — as A, except that a decision card whose Chief of Staff notes with a recommended word are on main is treated as if that record had been read: it is shown with its recommendation, counted on Home, put on the board, offered with answer buttons and notified, since none of that depends on when it was raised. Only the cards without notes are hidden, and the line says that decision cards without notes are not shown.
     Cost: as A, but only for cards without notes; of those, the ones within their first day would be held anyway, so what you lose while that record cannot be read is the cards past their day or with no recorded raise time. B adds more new wording to the spec than A: the rule for a read without that record, the line's wording and the join sheets, all going into the review whose failure stops the work. Rules out: the same as A.
  C. Show them all at once, as the plan says now — a card whose raise time has not been read shows straight away, marked "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" or with its notes' recommended word, and may be hidden again on the next read.
     Cost: a card can show and then vanish, and the views and the notifications can disagree for a while: a notice may open to a card no longer listed, and a card may be notified again when it shows again, which sets aside part of your re-specify ruling; the spec's rule and its join sheets are rewritten for this case. Rules out: a card hidden because that record cannot be read; it does not rule out a card hidden while the queue or the notes cannot be read.
RECOMMENDATION: B — of the two choices that keep your ruling that every view and the sender agree, it hides fewer cards, and the ones you most need, with my recommendation, still show; the desk says why the others are missing. Confidence: medium; it would change to A if you would rather send the simplest wording into the review whose failure stops the work, or to C if seeing every card while the raise times are missing matters more to you than the views agreeing.
RISK CLASS: R0   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: the card fix's plan and spec only; nothing is built yet, and the live desk is unchanged until the fix goes live
