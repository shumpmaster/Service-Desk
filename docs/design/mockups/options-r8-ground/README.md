# Options: colour in the background (round 8, answer 15)

Built over mockup v37 (`../timeline-v37-craft.html`). `source/build.py` writes `phone.html` (v37 with `source/gr.css` and `source/gr.js`; each option switched on by the phone's hash, `.light` or `.dark` fixing the phone's theme, `home` staying on home, `wd` opening the Home-screen widget); `source/page.py` writes `index.html`, using the round 8 options pages' stylesheet (`../options-r8/source/pages.py`).

| Option | What it is |
|---|---|
| A (recommended) | A soft colour field: three large pale fields (blue, lavender, a touch of mint) fixed behind everything; faint deep glows in dark. A. A soft colour field everywhere: it is the slight variation you asked for, the glass finally has something to catch, and since it is the same on every page it never has to be learned or mistaken for a status. |
| B | Each project its own pair of cool hues (The timeline blue and lavender, the widget teal and blue, and so on); home neutral. None is a status colour. |
| C | Light from the top: a blue-to-lavender wash behind the band, fading to the plain ground halfway down. |
| D | A mood: warm while something waits on you, cool when all is moving. The warm wash sits near the yellow of waiting. |

Touches: P-005-rebrand-style (neutral greys and one blue for selected or now): every option loosens the neutral ground and the one-blue rule for the background only; cards, rows, words, status colours and the blue of now are unchanged. Round 8, answer 14, item 4A (glass on what floats) is what the colour shows through. With reduced transparency or more contrast, the ground turns flat again.

Measured on the rendered ground at 393 wide, every 6 points, at its most tinted spot: the words straight on the ground stay 4.5:1 or better in every option. Light: the grey under titles 4.9 (A, B, C) to 5.5 (D), with the two secondary greys one shade darker on a tinted ground (#5a5c60, #555559); the blue of now 4.9 to 5.4; ink 12.4 or more. Dark: the grey 6.0 or more, the blue 7.8 or more, white 17 or more. Cards, rows, sheets and the map band keep their own solid colours, so nothing on them changes. The label grey (#8e8e93, the map's small words) was already under 4.5:1 on the flat ground in light (2.9:1); it is for labels, not words, and is unchanged.

The map band paints the same field, fixed to the screen, so the colour runs on behind it while rows still pass under it unseen. iPhone Safari does not honour a fixed background on an element; the real desk would draw the band's field from the band's own top instead, which lines up because the band sits at the top of the screen.
