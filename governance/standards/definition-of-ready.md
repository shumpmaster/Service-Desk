# Definition of ready — leaving Shape (D-052)

*Copied verbatim from the operating model (OPERATING_MODEL.md, "Definition of ready: leaving
Shape", model commit 00d6360, which carries item 7 as amended by model ruling L-0119) so the Critic's definition-of-ready check has its checklist in its
pack. The model's text governs; if they differ, this copy is a bug to fix.*

One project document in two parts. *Part 1, the brief* (settled at approval): (1) scope — in, out,
where it must work; (2) strategy — the chain from deliverables to value; (3) the 25,000-foot plan —
deliverables two levels deep with dependencies; (4) owners for every deliverable, including the
owner himself and outside parties; (5) limits — spend ceiling, data boundaries, and the project's
tier (T1–T3 by v2.5.1's tier triggers), which is its trust level (D-062); (6) value and stop rule —
this project's own measure, target, date and miss, including the cost to the owner's time; (7) solid
foundation — every load-bearing unknown answered with evidence, or accepted open by the owner: an
unknown may stay open into Define only when the project document names the test that closes it
there and the fallback if it fails, and the owner's ruling accepting it is recorded in
`decisions/` (L-0119); (8) clear and consistent — key terms
defined, no contradictory constraints. *Part 2, the intent* (living): drafted by the Chief of Staff
from conversations, portfolio defaults and past rulings, shown on the approval card. The Critic
checks the list. A limit belongs on it only if the team would otherwise ask mid-build *and* the
answer changes per project; the rest are standing defaults in the portfolio intent.
