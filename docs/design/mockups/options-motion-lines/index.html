Opening a project, and its branch lines

First the motion: how a project opens now, then three ways it could fade in from the left. Then the lines: how they look now, then three styles. Every picture is the real v10 page with only that part changed.

Try them live

The live page has a switcher above the tab bar. Pick a motion and a line style, then tap a project.

Motion

Now (v10)

A pen draws the plan from the top down

The card flies up, a line draws out of the desk's trunk, and then a pen grows the plan downwards while the page scrolls after it to Now. It takes about two and a half seconds.

Frames at 0.06, 0.18, 0.32, 0.48, 0.64, 0.82, 1.05 and 1.35 seconds.

Option 1 · recommended

All at once, from the left

The desk fades out, and the whole plan fades in from 16 points to the left, already centred on Now. No scrolling and no drawing. It takes about 0.4 seconds.

Frames at 0, 0.07, 0.14, 0.21, 0.28, 0.35, 0.44 and 0.70 seconds.

Option 2

A quick ripple from Now

As in 1, but the rows come in one after another: Now first, then the rows above and below it, 22 milliseconds apart. It takes about 0.6 seconds and shows your eye where Now is.

Frames at the same times as option 1.

Option 3

Lines first, then the rows

The line and the header fade in from the left first, and the rows follow together a moment later. The branch shows first, in two steps. It takes about 0.6 seconds.

Frames at the same times as option 1.

All three close the same way, with the plan fading out to the left and the desk fading back in. I also changed the yellow flash on Now, left over from v8, to a soft blue in all three.

Branch lines

Now (v10)

Dashed elbows

The lines are thin and grey, with dashed square-cornered elbows from the trunk to each card. A sharp step joins the desk's line to the project's line under the title. Planned work is dashed.

Option 1 · recommended

Hairline curves

Every line is one 1.5-point hairline with round ends. Each card's branch leaves the trunk in one smooth curve. The desk's line flows into the project's line in a gentle S. Planned work is a fine row of round dots, not dashes.

Option 2

Soft rail

The line becomes a 4-point track in a pale fill, like a slider's track, with the same smooth curves. Done work is a shade darker and planned work paler. The dots sit on the track.

Option 3

Just the line, no connectors

Hairlines as in 1, but with no branches drawn at all. The cards and rows sit beside the line, and their indent alone shows they hang from it. This is the closest to Craft, which draws no lines.

Round 2

You asked whether there are other motions, and said the soft rail is the closest but not quite right. Here are four more motions, and four variations on the soft rail. All of them are in the live page's switcher too.

Motion, round 2

Option 4

Soft focus

The plan fades in from the left out of a light blur, sharpening as it settles, and the desk blurs as it leaves. It takes about 0.5 seconds.

Frames at 0, 0.08, 0.16, 0.24, 0.32, 0.40, 0.52 and 0.80 seconds.

Option 5

Push

The desk slides out to the right while the plan slides in from the left, 40 points, like turning back a page. The direction is clearer than in 1, and it takes about 0.5 seconds.

Frames at 0, 0.08, 0.16, 0.24, 0.32, 0.40, 0.52 and 0.80 seconds.

Option 6

The branch reaches out

On the desk, the tapped project’s branch first draws out from the trunk to its card while everything else fades. Then the plan fades in from the left. The branch is the cue, drawn sideways rather than down. It takes about 0.7 seconds.

Frames at 0, 0.08, 0.16, 0.24, 0.32, 0.40, 0.52 and 0.80 seconds.

Option 7

Settle

As in 1, but the plan also grows very slightly, from 98.5 percent, anchored on the left, so it seems to settle into place. It takes about 0.4 seconds.

Frames at 0, 0.08, 0.16, 0.24, 0.32, 0.40, 0.52 and 0.80 seconds.

Branch lines, round 2: variations on the soft rail

Option 4

Groove

The track is pressed into the page: a 6-point groove with a soft inner shadow and a light lower edge, the opposite of the raised chips. The done part fills the groove like a slider’s fill.

Option 5

Thin rail

A 2.5-point rail, between the hairline and the soft rail. Done work is a clear grey and planned work pale.

Option 6

Fading rail

The soft rail, but each branch fades out as it reaches its card, and the planned rail fades away upwards into the future.

Option 7

Line in the rail

A pale 4-point track everywhere, with a thin darker line running inside it for the work that is done, like progress along a track.
