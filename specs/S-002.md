# Spec S-002 — Every decision card shows the Chief of Staff's recommendation, or says it has none

status: draft   frozen_at: —   workstream: P-003 (the card fix)
work-type: fix   design_id: P-003   lineage: [S-001]
mechanism: One rule in the desk's shared card list (src/lib/) holds a decision card until its Chief of Staff notes are on the main branch, then marks the notes' recommended word, and after a day shows the card anyway marked "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet"; every view and the notification sender read that one list.
problem: The owner answers decision cards on the desk, but the desk marks a button Recommended only from the card's own section 5 and never reads the Chief of Staff's notes, so twice the owner answered `re-specify` where the notes recommended `drop`.
milestones: M4

*Plan: docs/projects/P-003.md (Part 1 approved at its Design gate, decisions/P-003/dor-4.md, "proceed"). Value target: V6 (plan §6). Terms "decision card", "gate card", "notes", "recommended word", "raised" and "on the main branch" are the plan's (§1, §8) and mean the same here.*

## The defect
Owner's ruling record: decisions/questions/P-001-card-recommendation.md ("Proceed with the fix"; "Well then we need to make sure the cards don't show up until then."; "Show it after a day, flagged (Recommended)"; then "A: own addition now (Recommended)"), quoted in the plan's Part 2 and header. The record itself is not in this definer's pack; this spec takes its words from the plan's quotation of it.

What goes wrong today: a decision card (`dor-fail`, `stop` or `failure`) shows on the desk as soon as it is in queue/, and its Recommended mark comes only from its own section 5. The Chief of Staff's recommendation lives in a separate notes file, `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<n>-notes.md`, which the desk never reads (plan §2, citing research/P-003-sizing-findings.md §2). Seen twice: decisions/Q-019/stop-1.md and decisions/Q-017/stop-2.md were answered `re-specify` where the notes recommended `drop` (plan §2, citing the sizing findings §4).

## Acceptance criteria (scenarios; owner-approved)
Each criterion is a plain scenario traced to V6: *decision cards shown to the owner with neither the Chief of Staff's recommended button marked nor the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, 0, from the day M4 is live to 2026-12-31*. A V6 miss is also any decision card held off the queue more than a day after it was raised (plan §6).

### Holding and showing (the rule, D1, and what the owner sees, D2)
- **AC1** [trace: V6 → AC1] — When a decision card is in queue/ and its notes are not on the main branch, and it was raised less than a day ago, then the desk holds it: it is not on the owner's queue, not in Home's count of what needs the owner, not on the board, has no card sheet reachable from those views, and no notification is sent for it.
- **AC2** [trace: V6 → AC2] — When a held decision card's notes reach the main branch with a recommended word, then on the open desk's next read the card shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board; its card sheet says the Chief of Staff recommends that word, and that word's button, and only that one, is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent then.
- **AC3** [trace: V6 → AC3] — When a decision card's card sheet shows, then its own section 5 marks no button, whatever word it names: on a decision card only the notes' recommended word marks a button. (Gate cards keep today's behaviour, AC9.)
- **AC4** [trace: V6 → AC4] — When a decision card was raised a day (24 hours) ago or more by its `card` entry in the dispatch log and still has no notes on the main branch, then it shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board, its card sheet carries the mark "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet", and no button is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent then. If the desk is open as the day passes, the card shows on the desk's next read, without a reload.
- **AC5** [trace: V6 → AC5] — When a card shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark later gets notes with a recommended word on the main branch, then on the desk's next read the mark is gone and the card shows the notes' recommended word with that word's button marked Recommended, as in AC2. No second notification is sent for it.
- **AC6** [trace: V6 → AC6] — When a decision card's notes are on the main branch but have no recommended word (no `Recommendation:` line; no backticked word on that line; or no backticked word on it that is one of the card's words), then the desk treats the card as having no notes: held under AC1 within its first day, shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark under AC4 after it, and never with a button marked.
- **AC7** [trace: V6 → AC7] — When a decision card's raise time is not known to the desk (no `card` entry for it in the part of the dispatch log the desk reads, or the log not yet read), then the card shows at once, as in AC4, with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, or as in AC2 if its notes with a recommended word are already on the main branch. Once the open desk has shown a decision card, it does not withdraw it from the queue, Home's count or the board until the card is answered or leaves queue/, even if a later read of the log finds it raised less than a day ago.
- **AC8** [trace: V6 → AC8] — When notes for a decision card exist only on a branch other than the main branch (for example in an open pull request), then the desk treats the card as having no notes.

### Unchanged
- **AC9** [trace: V6 → AC9] — When a card is a gate card (`dor`, `criteria` or `launch`), an owner question, or a pull-request card, then it shows, counts and notifies exactly as before this fix, with its Recommended mark from its gate case as today, and the desk does not look for notes for it.
- **AC10** [trace: V6 → AC10] — When the desk reads notes, then it reads them through the repository reads it already makes, writes nothing to any repository, and sends nothing to any new outside service; the desk's spend stays $0 (plan §1 Out, §5).

### The views agree
- **AC11** [trace: V6 → AC11] — When the same records are read, then the queue, Home's count, the board, the card sheet (including the hand-written one in src/public/sandbox.js) and the notification sender all agree, card by card, on which decision cards are held, which show with a recommended word (and which word), and which show with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark. Each decision card's notification is sent at most once, when it first shows.

### Running, monitoring, feedback and value review after graduation
*The desk has graduated (docs/PROJECT.md §3, Launch-5); these criteria make M4 part of how it already runs. They are built in Build like the rest.*
- **AC12** [trace: V6 → AC12] — Running: when M4 is live, then it needs no new setting, credential, schedule or step from the owner or any agent; the desk's operating notes (docs/handover/) say in plain words what the hold, the day's fallback and the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark mean, that the Chief of Staff's notes must be on the main branch for a decision card to show with a recommendation, and how to roll M4 back (redeploy the previous production deployment).
- **AC13** [trace: V6 → AC13] — Monitoring: when the Chief of Staff runs the weekly value check from the day M4 is live to 2026-12-31, then it records V6 in intake/value-checks.jsonl beside V1 to V5: the count of decision cards shown that week with neither mark, and the count held more than a day after their raise time, both worked out from queue/, the notes files on the main branch, the dispatch log and the cards' answer records; the operating notes give this counting rule. Each miss is reported to the owner in that week's check, naming the card.
- **AC14** [trace: V6 → AC14] — Feedback: when the owner sees a decision card with a wrong or missing mark, or misses one they expected, then the operating notes tell them to say so to the Chief of Staff in a question (decisions/questions/), and the Chief of Staff records it as a V6 miss in the next weekly check, whether or not the count in AC13 found it.
- **AC15** [trace: V6 → AC15] — Value review: when 2026-12-31 has passed, then the Chief of Staff puts V6's result (the misses, by week, with each one's card) to the owner on a card for the evidence review, where the owner rules continue, re-aim or stop; a miss before then that shows M4 is wrong is put to the owner when found, not held until then.

## Join sheets
- **J1 —** queue/ and the dispatch log → the rule (D1): for each card file `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md` (for example `queue/Q-003-failure-1.md`), its kind from `<gate>` and its raise time from its `card` entry in the dispatch log the desk already reads (plan §7). Decision card if `<gate>` is `dor-fail`, `stop` or `failure`; gate card if `dor`, `criteria` or `launch`. Awkward cases: a `dor-fail` card must not be read as a `dor` gate card by matching the prefix `dor` (it is a decision card); no `card` entry (AC7); two `card` entries for one card (the earliest counts); a raise time in the future by clock skew (treated as raised now, so held at most a day from now). The exact field names of the log's `card` entry are those of the desk's existing log reader in src/lib/ (research/P-003-sizing-findings.md §1); the Builder states them in the J1 test. A test marked `join-test: S-002/J1` covers it.
- **J2 —** the main branch's file tree and a notes file → the rule (D1): whether `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<n>-notes.md` exists on the main branch for card `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md`, and if so its recommended word: the first backticked word on the file's first line containing `Recommendation:` that is one of the card's words (the words its buttons offer), matched exactly, with any bold (`**`), italic (`*`, `_`) or heading (`#`) marks around the line ignored. Examples, for a card whose words are `drop`, `re-specify`, `resubmit`:
  - `Recommendation: \`drop\`` → `drop`
  - `**Recommendation:** \`drop\` — the scope is gone` → `drop`
  - `## Recommendation: \`re-specify\`` → `re-specify`
  - `Recommendation: not \`proceed\`; \`drop\`` → `drop` (`proceed` is not one of the card's words)
  - `Recommendation: \`Drop\`` → none (not an exact match), so no notes (AC6)
  - `Recommendation: drop` (no backticks) → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - no `Recommendation:` line → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - two `Recommendation:` lines → the first line decides; if it has no recommended word, none (the second is not read)
  - notes for `stop-2` beside a card `stop-1` → not this card's notes
  A test marked `join-test: S-002/J2` covers it.
- **J3 —** the rule (D1) → the one card list every view reads: each decision card carries one state, `held`, `shown, recommended <word>`, or `shown, no recommendation yet`, plus whether the open desk has already shown it (AC7). Gate cards, questions and pull-request cards carry no new state. Awkward cases: a card answered while held (it leaves the list as any answered card does); notes arriving in the same read as the day passing (the notes win, AC2); notes removed from the main branch after a card showed with their word (the card falls back to `shown, no recommendation yet`, never back to `held`). A test marked `join-test: S-002/J3` covers it.
- **J4 —** the card list → the views (queue, Home's count, board, card sheet in src/public/sandbox.js and its generated copies): `held` cards appear in none; `shown, recommended <word>` cards show the word in the card sheet and mark that button Recommended; `shown, no recommendation yet` cards show the mark and mark no button. Awkward cases: a card sheet opened from a direct link to a held card (it says the card is waiting for the Chief of Staff's notes, with no answer buttons); the card sheet left open while the card moves from `shown, no recommendation yet` to `shown, recommended <word>` (the mark gives way on the next read). A test marked `join-test: S-002/J4` covers it.
- **J5 —** the card list → the notification sender: a decision card is notified once, at the first read where it is not `held`; a change from `shown, no recommendation yet` to `shown, recommended <word>` sends nothing. Awkward cases: the sender reading before the desk has read the dispatch log (AC7 applies: it shows and is notified once); a card that shows and is answered between two sender runs (no notification is owed). A change to the sender's own files needs the owner's approval (plan §4). A test marked `join-test: S-002/J5` covers it.

## Risks and mitigations
- A card held for good — AC4 (a day) and AC7 (unknown raise time shows at once); V6 counts any hold over a day (AC13).
- A button marked that the Chief of Staff did not recommend — exact matching in J2 and AC6 (anything unclear counts as no notes); AC3 stops section 5 marking a decision card.
- The owner told twice — J5, one notification per card.
- A gate card changed by mistake — AC9 and J1's `dor-fail` / `dor` case.
- A card appearing and then vanishing — AC7's "once shown, not withdrawn".
- The owner leaning on the mark without reading the reasons (automation bias "cannot be prevented by training or instructions", library/facts/F-automation-complacency-01.md, B) — the card sheet still shows the card in full; the mark only names the Chief of Staff's word. Accepted, as the owner asked for the mark.
