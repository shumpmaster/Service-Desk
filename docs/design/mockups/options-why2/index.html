The why, shown differently

Your answer on the hierarchy:

"I like the tree, but I don’t like the format of the whys being embedded into everything like a step. I thought I would but I want it represented slightly differently"

All four phones have the tree you chose, with every ringed icon folding. The first shows the why as it is: a block under each event, inside the tree. In the three options the why leaves the tree and becomes part of the event instead. The reasons are still examples, on S-011 only.

Each phone opens on the timeline project. Scroll down inside it to S-011's stop and reviews.

Option A

Part of the event's line

  • The why follows "by …" on the event's last line, after "Why:", two lines at most. Tap it to read it all.
  • Always there, but it reads as part of the event, not a step under it.

Option B · recommended

A Why you tap

  • A small outlined "Why" sits on the event's first line, beside its state. Tap it and the sentence opens in a bubble pointing at the event; tap anywhere else to close it. One at a time.
  • The list stays as calm as before, and every event shows it has a reason.

Option C

A Why switch

  • A "Why" switch sits on the map, beside the milestone chip. Off, no reasons show. On, each one sits as a small note under its event, marked by a soft blue edge.
  • Good for reading the whole story at once, then turning it off.
