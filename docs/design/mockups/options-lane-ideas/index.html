Lanes, three different ideas

You asked for radically different ideas for the lanes taking too much width. First v12 as it is, with M6 open. Then three different ways to show several branches, drawn with M6's real events and v12's own icons, on a phone 393 points wide. They follow your light or dark setting.

The seven kinds, main set apart, the numbers and the agent's plan stay. Colour and the milestone shapes stay on hold. The step's actions are left out here to keep the pictures about the lanes.

2 · Sprint cards, no lines

No lines at all. Each sprint is a card that says in one line where it stands. Its icon opens or folds it, so you start from two lines, not seventeen events. Where it branched and merged is said in words.
