Home as a map: what needs you, on the map

Your idea, in your words:

"show what you have pinned at the top, the maps - but things that need me will popup in their usually color and format. But effectively be tied back to the actual cards below, of which I think need a slightly visual rework"

This page is about the pop-ups and how they tie back. The cards' rework comes next, as its own question. Each phone is live: scroll inside it, and tap a pop-up. Tapping a card still opens it, as in v13. The activity before today is an example. Your first look at the three options is kept at round1.html.

As it is

The pinned maps, as drawn in B

  • What needs you shows only as an icon on the lane: the timeline's questions and stop, the widget's failed check.
  • Tapping an icon does nothing. Needs you and the cards sit below, unconnected to the map.

Option 1 · recommended

Pop-ups tied to their rows in Needs you

  • Each thing that needs you pops up on its project's lane as its icon and tag, in the soft colour it has in the rows: "Your call" red, "3 questions" yellow, "Failed · your call" red.
  • Tapping one scrolls to its own row in Needs you and lights it. The three questions light together.
  • As you scroll down the project cards, the map lights the lane of the card you're on.
  • Needs you stays one list, in order of waiting, as in v13.

Option 2

Pop-ups tied to their project's card

  • The same pop-ups on the lanes.
  • Needs you moves into the cards: each project card carries its own waiting rows under it, so the map's lanes and the cards match one to one.
  • Tapping a pop-up scrolls to its row in its project's card and lights it. The lane of the card in view lights as you scroll.
  • There is no separate Needs you list. Everything waiting on you is on the map and in the cards.
