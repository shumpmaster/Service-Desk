# Ruling needed: Should your phone be told about decision cards from the portfolio?

Milestone: MD3
Asked in session: session_012RJPVKAoLga2RkqLYRDcBY

WHY: Once the desk reads your portfolio, a decision that spans projects can wait on the board under "Portfolio". Your phone is told about things waiting on you in a project; telling it about portfolio cards is extra work. Left unruled, the phone isn't told about them yet.
OPTIONS:
  A. Later — As its own piece of work, after the desk reads the portfolio.
  B. Now — As part of reading the portfolio.
RECOMMENDATION: A — portfolio cards will be rare at first, and it keeps the first portfolio step small.

DEFAULT: A   TIMEOUT: 48
