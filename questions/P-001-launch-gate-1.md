# Ruling needed: The Launch gate — go live on needs-you and graduate Service-Desk from the desk?

Milestone: M3C
Asked in session: session_017seyaGqUxAff6E8dKWHDCR

WHY: The Launch gate's case and the Critic's own view beside it are ready. Proceeding is going live and graduating in one act, as you chose (the gate first). Answer it in the launch sitting, in your own words to your Chief of Staff, not from the desk's answer box. A proceed is recorded only by your own commit of your graduation record, once the desk is live and naming its deploy. A kill closes the project, so you commit it yourself, with no proxy. A hold or a recycle your Chief of Staff records and you merge. No answer to this question is merged by the automatic asks-merge job. It never defaults: a proceed regenerates the desk's read token, which can't be undone. Left unanswered, nothing goes live.
OPTIONS:
  A. proceed — in a sitting of about 32 minutes, the desk's GitHub read token is regenerated (this can't be undone: the old value stops at once), the desk goes live on needs-you, Operations-Hub is archived, your phone's notifications move to the needs-you icon, and you commit your graduation record; Service-Desk then shows as Done, and still shows anything waiting on you.
  B. hold — nothing goes live; your Chief of Staff updates the case as a second case, and you read it again.
  C. recycle into Discovery, Design, Build or Launch — name the phase; nothing goes live, and the work goes back through it as its next run; the case, its reviews and the Critic's view are done again after it, about a day or more of agents' work beyond that phase's own (an estimate).
  D. kill — the project closes, as your model's kill does; nothing goes live; the preview desk keeps running as it is now, and switching it off is yours to ask for; you commit this answer yourself.
RECOMMENDATION: A — both the join-up review and the launch review passed at the reviewed commit, nothing blocking (the case, section 1); the Critic's view also recommends proceed
