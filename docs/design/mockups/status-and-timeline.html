docs/PROJECT.md, Intent · the project began 30 Sep

Worth knowing

  • Context is what was true that led to the why: a limit found in testing, a fact about the records, a review finding, a research result, an earlier ruling, or a note of yours. Each fact names its source. The agents write these lines into each sprint's record when they file it; the desk only shows them. Here they are drafted from the real question files.
  • Every why that reads "not recorded yet" is flagged in coral on purpose: a milestone with no why is the "wait, why are we doing that?" moment. The one real why shown (M2B) is your own words from the plan. Why lines for every milestone need a small change to the operating model.
  • "Not tied to a milestone" gathers the sprints that don't say which milestone they feed. Today that is most of them, including the last four. Filing sprints against milestones as they're planned closes this.
  • What we're delivering and where we started are quoted from the project's plan (docs/PROJECT.md), never written by the desk.
  • The phase dates and the 31 Discovery sprints are read from the project's merge history.
  • Text boxes will use 16 px text, so the phone stops zooming in when you tap one.
