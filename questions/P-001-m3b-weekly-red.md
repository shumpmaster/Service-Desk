# Ruling needed: After one bad week, does a weekly target stay red?

Milestone: M3B
Asked in session: session_017seyaGqUxAff6E8dKWHDCR

WHY: False alarms (V2) and upkeep (V3) are goals per week: "at most 1 a week", "at most 30 minutes a week". If one week goes over and later weeks are fine, the desk must either keep the target red for the rest of the 14 days or follow only the latest week. Left unruled, A.
OPTIONS:
  A. Stays red — any week over its goal makes the target outside; the big figure is the worst week, and each week has its own bar, so the bad week shows in red among the good ones.
  B. Follows the latest week — the mark and figure are the latest week's; earlier weeks keep their own bars.
RECOMMENDATION: A — it matches "at most 1 a week" and your Chief of Staff's "missed", so the desk and the check never disagree on it.
RISK CLASS: R1   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: V2's and V3's marks on the targets' sheet and row
DEFAULT: A   TIMEOUT: 168
