# Source standard — what counts as an independent source

*Owner ruling, 2026-10-01 (decisions/questions/Q-005-sources.md, ruling A). The Source checker
reads this file; it states a checking rule, not any answer a question hopes for (D-055).*

- **Facts about how a vendor's own product behaves** (its features, limits, settings, APIs): two
  different pages from the vendor's own documentation count as two sources. Label the entry "same
  publisher". No third-party source is required, since nobody else can document the product.
- **Claims about cost, risk, quality or comparisons** still need at least one source independent of
  the vendor.
- **Absence claims** ("not documented") are recorded as unverifiable gaps, not filed as facts.
