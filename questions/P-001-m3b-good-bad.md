# Ruling needed: Who decides whether a value is good or bad?

Milestone: M3B
Asked in session: session_017seyaGqUxAff6E8dKWHDCR

WHY: You asked for the values to "indicate good or bad". The plan has the desk never call a target met or missed itself; only your Chief of Staff's weekly check does (reading (g)). So before the first check, and between checks, the picture shows a figure with no verdict. Left unruled, the plan stands as written.
OPTIONS:
  A. The desk judges each figure as it stands — each figure is marked against its target as soon as there is one: green with a tick and "within target" when it is inside it, red with a cross and "outside target" when it isn't, grey "not measured yet" when there is no figure; during the 14 days it says "so far". Your Chief of Staff's weekly check still gives the final "met" or "missed", shown with it. Reading (g) changes.
  B. Only the weekly check judges — as planned: the figure shows plainly, and green or red appears only once your Chief of Staff's check says "met" or "missed".
RECOMMENDATION: A — you see good or bad at once, every day, and the weekly check still has the last word.
RISK CLASS: R1   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: the targets' sheet and its row
DEFAULT: B   TIMEOUT: 168
