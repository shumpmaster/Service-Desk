Ruling: design discovery answered; it shapes research Q-010 and Q-011, and S-001's design milestone
Proxy: done at the owner's request (the owner replied in the session on 2026-10-08, verbatim, to the eight shortlisted questions of the discovery map in P-001-design-discovery-notes.md)

1. When the desk is closed and something needs you, how do you want to find out? "Phone notifications"
2. One sentence you'd love the desk to say about where a project is heading: "“Design phase right now - we need to establish KPIs, create visualizations, and trial their update cadence - once this is done you’ll be able to view progress across the project” (something like this that highlights the upcoming work but brings it back to the 5000 foot view)"
3. Glance or review? "I want to glance for a few seconds, but pick up enough information from that glance to know where to press into more details. I’m not a passive builder I just don’t know where to look because I’m not an expert."
4. One combined to-do list, or waits grouped by project? "I want them bucketed by project. Because some projects I just won’t get to sporadically because I’m more heavily focused on another one."
5. An agent nearly out of context or usage, what would you do? "I would want the agent to identify a stopping point without having to compact the conversation mid action."
6. Irreversible or self-defaulting decisions versus routine PRs? "They need to be viewed differently, not more or less urgent. And the PRs themselves need to lead with a more practical explanation when proposed."
7. Calm and minimal, or rich like a dashboard? "I like a clean, modern look, that facilitates a drill down workflow. I want to naturally live at that car dashboard level - but not get hit in the face with it right off the bat."
8. "New since you last looked" markers? "Yes absolutely, I want to know all the agents that are working, what they’re working on, why they’re working on it, what has changed, etc…"

What follows from the answers:
- **Desk design** (S-001's design milestone):
  - A calm overview grouped by project, each box giving enough at a glance to know where to drill in.
  - Drill-down to the dashboard-level detail.
  - A per-project "where it's heading" line: the current phase, the upcoming work, and what it unlocks.
  - Kinds of decision told apart visually without ranking them.
  - An activity view of which agents are working, on what, why, and what changed, with "new since you last looked".
- **Reaching the owner when the desk is closed:** phone notifications, researched as Q-011.
- **Agent behaviour** (model changes, Personal-Org-Operating-Model):
  - Agents find a safe stopping point before their context window forces compaction mid-action.
  - Pull requests lead with a plain-language practical explanation.
  
  Both are queued with the discovery-step model change (track B).
