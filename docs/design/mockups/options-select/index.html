Selecting a milestone without losing your place

What you said:

"On the 1/2/3/4 section I don't like how the timeline perspective gets thrown off when you select a milestone?"

Why it happens: tapping a milestone's row zooms the branch map in to that milestone alone (since round 5). The rest of the phase leaves the map, so the map no longer matches the 1/2/3/4 row under it. Below: v29 as it is, then three ways to keep your bearings. In each phone, tap 2.3 The design fits together (scroll down a little) and watch the map.

One thing to know about A and C: the whole-phase map is the older, smaller drawing (dots, no words in the window), because the icons and words were only ever drawn for the zoomed map. If you pick A or C, I'd bring them to the whole-phase map next.

v29: the map zooms

  • Tapping a milestone swaps the map for that milestone's sprints only; the chip's cross brings the phase back.

Option A · recommended

Whole phase, milestone outlined

  • The map always shows the whole phase. The selected milestone's stretch is tinted and outlined in blue, with a chip naming it; its cross clears it.
  • The window and the centre arrow follow the scroll as before.

Option B

Zoom, with a strip to keep bearings

  • The map still zooms to the milestone, but the band shows the phase's milestones as a strip, the zoomed one ringed and Now ticked.
  • Tap another box to move the zoom there; the chip's cross brings the phase back.

Option C

No selecting

  • Tapping a milestone only folds or opens it. The map always shows the whole phase and simply follows the scroll and the centre arrow.
  • The simplest; nothing on the map says which milestone you tapped.
