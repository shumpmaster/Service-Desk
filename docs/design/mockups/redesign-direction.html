Mockup · the redesign direction · from the design audit of 19 Oct · not built

Waiting, Plan, Projects

The audit's proposed shape, drawn: the approved board as the opening screen, across all projects, and one bar of three tabs. Four text sizes and three weights, IBM Plex Sans with Plex Mono for refs, ages and times, five neutrals, vermilion only for what needs you, green and crimson only for status. The items are real ones from 19 Oct, shown as examples: ages and times come from their GitHub records, as at 12:20 on 19 Oct (Chicago time); "checked 2 min ago" and the "At work now" line are examples.

1 · Waiting, the opening screen

The board across all projects. One verb pill per card; the title is the count.

2 · The card sheet

About 55% high. A question's options are chips, the recommendation tagged and never picked for you; shown after you tap "Answer C".

3 · Plan

Work ahead: the milestones ahead in order, each with its rough finish. "Change order" brings the arrows and "Record plan".

4 · Projects

One row per project, with a glyph only when something is kicked back or CI fails. Details sit at the foot.

5 · One project's page

One sentence, the phase strip, who is at work, and the project's own milestones around the NOW line.

6 · Details, at the foot of Projects

A plain list, scrolled to: Agents and usage, Value targets, Record gaps, Notifications.

7 · Waiting, all clear

One sentence and when it was checked; the empty lanes collapse and today's Done stays. 19 Oct's real Done list.

8 · Waiting, dark

Frame 1 in the phone's dark setting.

What this draws from the audit

  • No "not recorded" lines, no standard milestones, no legends, no per-project tab bar; record ids only in mono meta lines.
  • One filled button per card, and one per sheet; every tap area at least 36 px.
  • A failed check reads differently from all clear: "Couldn't reach GitHub · last good check 13:12 · Retry", never "Nothing waiting".
