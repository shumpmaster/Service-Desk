## Question
Over the 30 days before E1 closes, how long did each step that waited on the owner wait, from when the desk's rules showed it to the owner's action on it, and what was the median?

## Why it matters
V7's target is "at most half its baseline" (plan line 128). Without this baseline V7 has no target, and the stop rule asks whether there are enough waits to measure at all (plan line 141).

## Rule (fixed before the run; S-010 AC40 uses the same rule)
Window: the 30 days ending at the moment the helper starts, in UTC. A wait counts if it starts inside the window. A wait still open at the window's end counts at its wait so far, marked open.

Each wait's start and end, read from the records of every connected project and the portfolio repository (plan line 135):

| Kind | Starts | Ends |
|---|---|---|
| Decision card (`dor-fail`, `stop`, `failure`) | when S-009's rule shows it: the earlier of its notes with a recommended word reaching the main branch and its `card` entry's time plus 24 hours (or at once if it is older than the log the desk reads); worked out by running that rule on the dispatch log and the notes, since no record holds it | the committer time of the first commit on the main branch's first-parent line that holds its answer record |
| Owner question | its file's pull request opened | the first main-line commit holding its answer record |
| Gate (`dor`, `criteria`, `launch`) | the first main-line commit holding its gate case | the first main-line commit holding its ruling record |
| Pull request that needs the owner | ready for review (or opened, if never a draft) | merged, or closed unmerged |
| Deploy awaiting the owner's review | the deployment's review requested | its review approved or rejected (the run's review history) |

"A pull request that needs the owner" is one the desk's card-making code puts on its waiting list, run on the records at the window's end with the code on the desk's main branch at that moment.

Each wait is marked as acted on by the owner or by the Chief of Staff as the owner's proxy (D-066), from who made the ending record. The two are reported apart; neither is dropped.

## Output (fixed form)
docs/handover/experiments/P-005-E1-result.json (where the desk files experiment results, docs/PROJECT.md:121): one entry per wait, `{ "project", "kind", "id", "start", "start_record", "end" | "open", "end_record", "wait_hours", "actor": "owner|proxy" }`; per kind and overall, the count and median for the owner and, apart, for the proxy; and a list of every kind the records could not time, with the reason.

## What the result decides (set before the run)
- If the owner's waits number 10 or more and their median is 1 hour or more: the baseline stands, and V7's target is half of it, put to the owner at the Design gate.
- If they number fewer than 10, or the median is under 1 hour: work stops and comes back to the owner (plan line 141; both thresholds the plan's drafts).
- If a kind cannot be timed from the records: it is left out of both E1 and V7, and the result and the plan say so (plan line 158).
- If no kind can be timed: V7 goes back to the owner as a fixed target with no baseline (plan line 158).

## Checks on the run
The Reviewer, who did not run it, re-times five waits picked at random and every open wait by hand from the records, and checks the medians. A difference fails the result, which is re-run once.

## Limits
Reads only; changes nothing in any repository. Commit times are as the committer wrote them (library/facts/F-git-dates-settable-01.md:4). Records GitHub no longer keeps cannot be timed and are listed. Cost: $0, and none of the owner's time.
