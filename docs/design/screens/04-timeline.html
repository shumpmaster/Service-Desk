Screen 4 of 7: Timeline

One line through each phase's work, in the order it happened, modelled on your Structured screenshot. Milestones head their part of the line. Reviews, failures, your decisions and stalls sit on it where they happened, and a phase worked through again is labelled as its next iteration, such as Design i2. Tap a phase at the top to move between phases, tap a milestone to open its breakdown in place, and tap any step for a plain-English summary. There are no clock times or durations: the line shows only order.

What's on this screen, and why

  • Rests on the design research (Q-013, source-checked, row numbers in brackets): One line per phase run, with the standard milestones folded away until you open them, so detail is one tap down (rows 1.4 and 1.5); a step with no record says so (2.6); milestone dots carry a key and a shape as well as colour (2.5, 4.1).
  • Newest at the top, as you asked on 13 Oct: the line reads from the bottom (oldest) up, and every step's "From the record" opens its file or pull request on GitHub.
  • Lighter, as you asked on 13 Oct: tinted icon tiles instead of solid blocks, a thin line, outline ticks and tighter rows. The colours keep their meanings, only lighter.
    • \n
  • Structured's week strip becomes the desk's four phases, then Done. Discovery, Design, Build and Launch are the desk's; Operate and Review belong to the product once it is done, so the desk doesn't show them. The gold circle is the phase on screen and names its iterations: Design shows "i1 i2" because the project came back to Design after Build. The dots under each phase show its latest run's own milestones: green when done, gold for the one under way, an empty ring when not started. Done stays dashed until you pass the Launch gate.
  • Each run is labelled "<Phase> i<n>". Where a phase ran twice, two buttons switch between its runs (Design i1, 5 to 6 Oct; Design i2, now). Each run opens with one plain sentence, bad news first.
  • Standard milestones first, then the project's own. Every run lists the model's checklist for its phase, the same for every project, beside this project's own milestones. Each shows one of five states, and only as the records show it: reached, not reached, not recorded, carried over, or not needed. Most of Service Desk's show "not recorded": it was built outside the pipeline, so no record can show them. Tap one for what it needs and why it shows as it does.
  • Structured's tall capsule marks a milestone, in plain English, with its own colour carried down the dashed line. Round pills are steps, coloured by who acted:
    • coral: you;
    • blue: an agent;
    • green: a check that passed;
    • red: a check that failed;
    • purple: a change to merge;
    • amber: a blocker.
    The circle on the right says whether each step is done, failed, waiting on you, or still to come.
  • Structured's notes in the gaps carry the story: the day changes, "Round 2 · after your re-specify" or "Second try" when a piece of research went back round (a retry inside a phase, not an iteration), and "Nothing moved for 2 h 32 min" where it stalled.
  • A milestone's breakdown opens in place. Tap a milestone and its detail opens under it on the line, the way a phase opens on the Project screen, with its bullets lined up with the icons. It holds what used to be screen 5, the breakdown and task lists: how many steps it has so far and how they went, the pieces of work under it (the second level down from the phase) and who did which steps. Everything in it is counted from the steps on the line; where a record doesn't name who did a step, it says so. Tap the milestone again to close it.
  • Confirmation on the timeline. In Design, the last step is waiting on you: PR #61, your saved screenshots. Tap it, then "I merged it". The step ticks off where it sits, and the next step lights up. That's the feedback you asked for, shown in place.
  • Real history, plain words. Every step comes from Service Desk's records from 1 to 10 October, and each popup says what happened, why, and what it means for you. Upcoming steps are greyed. The plan that failed review on 5 and 6 Oct now sits in Design i1, where the operating model puts planning; it used to sit under Build.
