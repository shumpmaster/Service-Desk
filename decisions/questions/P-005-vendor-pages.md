Ruling: A — a tool's changelog counts as one of its pages; a fact its own pages disagree on is filed as a record of what each page says; a fact on one page is filed marked "single page"; the last two are not load-bearing

Question: questions/P-005-vendor-pages.md
Milestone: Discovery-5

Question, as put: How may the library file a tool's facts from its changelog, from pages that disagree, or from a single page?
Options, as put:
  A. File all three, each marked for what it is: (1) a tool's changelog on its own site counts as one of its documentation pages for how it behaves; (2) where a tool's own pages disagree, the fact is filed as a record of what each page says, quoting each with its page and any date it gives, without stating which is current, and it is not load-bearing; (3) a fact about how a tool behaves found on only one of its pages may be filed marked "single page", and it is not load-bearing.
  B. File the changelog and disagreement case only: lines (1) and (2) of A.
  C. No change.
