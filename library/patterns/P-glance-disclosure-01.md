claim: "For a glanceable overview: put what users often need on the first screen and the rest one step down (corroborated: Nielsen 2006 'You have to disclose everything that users frequently need up front, so that they have to progress to the secondary display only on rare occasions'; Apple Widgets 'Create a layout that provides essential information at a glance and allows people to view additional details by taking a longer look'). A tap should open the matching detail directly (Apple Widgets, Live Activities). A glance surface need not show everything; prioritise the most useful (Apple Live Activities). A one-line text summary or small preview chart helps (Apple Charting data). Label the link so it sets clear expectations for the next level (Nielsen only; Budiu's information-scent page covers link-label accuracy, not the same claim, so not corroborated). Keep to at most two disclosure levels (Nielsen only, single source). Not documented: what belongs on a phone's first screen for several projects, item counts, a time budget for a glance, gauges."
