Question, as put: After one bad week, does a weekly target stay red?
Options, as put:
  A. Stays red — any week over its goal makes the target outside; the big figure is the worst week, and each week has its own bar, so the bad week shows in red among the good ones.
  B. Follows the latest week — the mark and figure are the latest week's; earlier weeks keep their own bars.
