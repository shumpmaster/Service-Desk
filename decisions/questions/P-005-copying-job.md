## How it is carried out
- The job is a change to what the projects record, so it is specified and built in the operating model (Personal-Org-Operating-Model), reviewed, and adopted by each of the four repositories as its own change; each adoption adds one workflow file and one copy branch, and the workflow file is R3 there as here.
- Its permissions: actions, checks and statuses read, contents write; no new key; the desk's key stays read-only. It keeps no logs, annotations or artifacts.
- Design sets the copies' file layout, how long they are kept, the check that a stopped job shows as stopped, and whether any repository's branch rules match the copy branch.
- The spend approved is the job's own minutes on the two private repositories, about 12 cents a month by the research's estimate; a larger cost goes back to the owner.
- The owner may also raise the private repositories' retention to 400 days in their settings; not done by this ruling.
