A lighter background

What you said: “It looks great so far, maybe just lighten the background up a tad?” (round 9, answer 1). v46 as it is comes first, then four ways to lighten it, the last a step further for range, each on home and on a project’s page, live: scroll and tap inside the phones. The recommended one has a blue edge. Only the background changes, in light; cards, rows, words, status colours and the one blue are untouched, and dark stays as it is. A lighter ground only raises the contrast of the words on it.

The background, a tad lighter

In v46: In v46, in light, the ground is a pale grey (#f2f3f7) under three soft colour fields, blue at the top left, lavender at the right and a touch of mint at the foot. v42 made the fields stronger (blue 22%, lavender 21%, mint 15%) so the glass had colour to catch. Dark is not changed by any option.Touches: Round 8, answers 15 and 16 (a soft colour field, option A) and answer 25 (more glass, which strengthened the fields). Every option keeps the field; none changes the status colours or the one blue.
Recommended: C. A little of each keeps the colour you asked for in round 8 and the glass still has something to catch, while the whole screen reads a step brighter. A alone keeps the colour as strong as now on a whiter base; B alone keeps the grey and fades the colour.
