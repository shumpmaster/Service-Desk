Screen 2 of 7: Project

Tap a project card on Home to get here. Modelled on your Lifesum screenshot: a big "Current status" title, coloured group headings, and one rounded row per phase, each with a status word and a status circle. Tap a phase to see its milestones in plain English.

What's on this screen, and why

  • Lifesum's layout, used for phases. Lifesum's big "Current status" title becomes yours. Its coloured caps headings group the phases into Here now, Done and Still to come. Each phase gets one rounded row: an icon, its name in caps, a status word, and a status circle (a filled tick, a gold ring, or an empty ring), so the state never depends on colour alone.
  • Two levels, no more. Tap a phase and its milestones open underneath in plain English, each with its status and one short reason. The phase that's under way opens by default. Going deeper, into each milestone's steps, is the timeline (screen 4).
  • The line under the title says what's happening in one sentence. Directly under it, two buttons jump to what needs you (the queue, screen 6) and who has the ball (the team, screen 3), so they're never more than one tap away.
  • Order is the operating model's, not the order things happened. Build shows as Done even though Design is still under way, because Build really did finish first.
  • A blocker shows where it bites. Launch carries "must pass first: the scrubbing proof", in coral, so you can see what's holding it before it starts.
  • Every item is real Service Desk data. The ⓘ explains the six phases; underlined words explain themselves.
