A fixed centre the list scrolls past

The second half of your answer:

"And can we establish a fixed center, that the scroll moves around? My example would be an arrow fixed in the middle of the screen that is always identifying the phase or milestone or task or action that is in line with it. And along with it an arrow pops up on the horizontal scrolling branch timeline to match"

The centre is the middle of the list's window, between the floating map and the tab bar. Whatever row is level with it is the one marked: a phase heading, a milestone, a sprint, an event, a plan or one of its actions (the smallest one the line crosses). On the map, a small blue arrow points down at the matching event: the event itself, a sprint's or milestone's first event, or the event a plan follows. The arrow moves as you scroll, and the map's window keeps following as before.
