The branch map fits the milestone you pick

The second half of your answer:

"Additionally, can we make it so whenever a milestone is selected, the branch map fits to the events in that milestone and expands the dots to our icons with descriptions if we can fit them?"

In each option, tapping a milestone's row selects it, and the map shows only that milestone's sprints with every event as its icon. Planned events sit at the right as open rings. A small chip on the map ("2.7 M6") says which milestone is shown; its cross, or folding the milestone, brings back the whole phase. The page opens with M6, the milestone in progress, already selected. M6 has 20 events, so they can't all carry words on a phone at once: the options differ in how they make room.

Each phone opens on the timeline project. Scroll inside it to move the window, tap another milestone (Design-5 has sprints too), and tap the chip's cross.
