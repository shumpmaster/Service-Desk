How the timeline opens

You said the page should always open on the step we are on, with its sprints, tasks and the rest open, and the other phases fixed, only scrolling when a phase is expanded. Today, in v12, the page opens partway down on Now. The Build phase and its gate sit above, Discovery below, and everything scrolls together (the first picture).

Then two ways to do it. Both use what you have chosen so far: the floating branch map, the tinted gauges on phases, milestones and sprints, and the marks and tags on events and steps. Scroll and tap inside each phone. Percentages marked “example” are made up.

Now

v12 as it opens

Option

A · The other phases pinned above and below

The page opens on the current step with everything in it open. Later phases sit as slim bars pinned under the map, earlier ones pinned at the bottom, each with its gauge. Only the current phase scrolls between them. Tap a pinned phase and it opens in place; then the page scrolls.
