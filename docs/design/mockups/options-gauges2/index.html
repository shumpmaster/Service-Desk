What a gauge counts, and when it changes colour

Two questions, each drawn on the same real slice of the timeline: the Design phase, its milestone M6, and M6's two sprints (S-010, superseded, and S-011, stopped after three failed plan reviews). The figures come from the records: S-011 had 12 events, 9 of which happened, and its spec listed 38 acceptance criteria, none of which could pass. Today only the phase's figure follows a rule; the rest are examples.

A ring's track now takes the soft colour of its state, so a ring at 0 still shows it; the phase's own colour is the dot by its name. The shapes stay as you chose them in v28 (rings for milestones, ten squares for sprints, the strip for the phase). Only what they count and their colour are in question.
