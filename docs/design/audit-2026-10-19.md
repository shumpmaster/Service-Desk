# Design audit of the trial view, 2026-10-19

*By the design audit agent, for the owner's request of 2026-10-19 (docs/design/references/owner-request-2026-10-19-design-steward.md). Judged against docs/PROJECT.md Part 1 (intent, V1 to V5), the Researcher's 15 principles (research-2026-10-19-modern-mobile-design.md, mostly provisional sources) and board mockup v5. Evidence: the trial view at `/?sandbox`, captured at 414×896 and 360×740, with text and computed-style dumps per screen. Fixture content ("A made-up…" items, "Doesn't match a reviewed pull request: don't approve", "Loading: 49 answered questions…") is not counted against the design; the structure around it is. The screenshots render in DejaVu Sans because the capture ran on Linux. On an iPhone `system-ui` is SF Pro, so the type looks heavier here than it will on the phone, but the 600 to 800 weights are in the CSS either way.*

## 1. Verdict in five lines

1. The desk shows you its database, not your to-do list. One project's Status page has 34 "not recorded" lines, 18 rows of standard milestones you never act on and 11 tiles for agents, all of them "Idle".
2. What waits on you sits four steps deep: Home card, then the orange button, then Queue, then the sheet. No row shows the one verb you need. The board you approved fixes this and should be the opening screen.
3. Each project gets its own four-tab app plus a hidden More detail drawer, so you move around by project instead of by what you need to do. That holds even for a project that tracks none of it: Personal-Org-Operating-Model has four tabs of "not recorded".
4. It feels heavy because everything is bold, boxed and coloured: 9 text sizes, 27 colours, legends to decode them, cards inside cards, and a colour per phase that tells you nothing about any decision.
5. It doesn't look AI-generated. It looks generated by the spec: every acceptance criterion became a visible line. The fix is a design brief that says what must not be shown, and a rebuild around three places: Waiting, Plan and Projects.

## 4. The bloat list, by impact

1. **"Not recorded" everywhere. Cut.** It appears 34 times on Status (27 of them "why: not recorded"), 21 on Timeline ("WHY not recorded yet · for the agents to add" ×15), 17 on the Chief of Staff page ("tokens not recorded · tool uses not recorded · run time not recorded"), on 5 of 7 Queue rows ("Unblocks: milestone not recorded"), and in "Its approver: not recorded", "wait not recorded", "Your last action: not available yet", the VERDICTS TO WRITE panel and "(operating model v2.5.1)" on every Personal-Org-Operating-Model screen. A missing field gets no line. The gaps become one row under Details, "Record gaps · N", for the Chief of Staff to work through, not you.
2. **Standard milestones. Stop showing them.** Launch-1 to 5, Build-1 to 5 and Design-1 to 6 (plus Design i1's two) add 18 rows of "Still to come" or "Not recorded" to Status, and Timeline adds Discovery's, none of which you act on. Show only the project's own milestones (M1 to M3C, MD1 to MD3).
3. **The five stacked phase rows** (on Home's card and Status) **and the runs.** "RUN 2 · Build i2 · still to come", "finished, then gone back over", "Run 2 began · why it went back: not recorded". Merge them into one line, "Design, second time through · next: The chosen design running on the real back end", plus a 4-segment strip with words.
4. **Legends. Cut all three:** Status's RUNS and MILESTONES (10 entries), Timeline's STATUS ICONS (9) and Team's (5). Each glyph already sits beside a word. The heading "THE PHASES, READ FROM THE BOTTOM UP" is an instruction that admits the layout reads backwards. Order the list top-down instead.
5. **The 12 text boxes in the response sheet. Merge them.** Show the 11 questions as rows of tappable options with the recommendation tagged "Rec.". Keep one optional "Anything else" box. The ruling file textarea moves behind "See the file it writes". Cut "(labelled, never picked for you)" ×11 and the spec citations in "WHY IT IS HERE" (S-001, AC54).
6. **The 4-tab bar per project** (STATUS, TIMELINE, TEAM, QUEUE), plus the home square, plus "Timeline ›" on every project screen, including Timeline itself. **Replace** with one app-level bar (§5).
7. **Team's 11 tiles. Cut** to "At work now: <role> on <milestone> · <time>" and "11 idle" in a sheet. The running logs (30 rows on You, 18 on Chief of Staff) move to Details › Agents. Stop showing session file paths and "line 18".
8. **Footers and tips. Cut or show once.** The notification tip, which is on Home and again in More detail; "new-since markers off on this device"; the median answer time on Home (it moves to Value targets); "GitHub Actions use: not set up yet (Setup N12)". On the board: "Your Done marks are kept on this phone…" (show once on first drag) and "Being recorded by the automatic job: 1" (move into the Done card's own state).
9. **The 9 font sizes and 27 colours. Replace with tokens.** Sizes in use: 10, 11.84, 12.48, 14.4, 15.04, 16, 16.85, 21.6 and 23.2 px across 16 size and weight pairs. Colours: 27 in light, 36 with dark, including a Gantt blue (#0b57d0) and red (#b3261e) that appear only on Agents, and per-phase magenta, olive, brown and teal. Bold is the body text: 12.48 px at weight 600 is the most common style on every screen, so nothing stands out.
10. **The More detail fold that wraps. Cut.** Its summary label breaks to "MOR / E", its content runs under the tab bar, and it hides three whole screens. Replace it with a plain Details list.
11. **The half-width project card. Cut.** It is 300 of 414 px wide, the description fades out mid-word ("project the agents ru"), and its grid and "Latest" toggles are unlabelled. The board replaces it. Projects become full-width rows.
12. **The orange summary "3 decisions · 1 gate ruling · 2 to merge · 1 deploy to approve". Cut.** It repeats on Home and Status and wraps to 5 lines at 360 px. The board is that breakdown.
13. **Record IDs in titles. Move to the sheet** (P-001-design-lab-open, P-951 stop-1, N-open, M3C, Launch-5, run 40). Show them once, in mono, in the sheet's meta line.
14. **Timeline's chrome. Cut:** the TECHNICAL/CONTEXT/WHY chips (unexplained filters), the "Not tied to a milestone" blocks (38, 14, 12 and 7 sprints, which is agents' filing work), and the "WHAT WE'RE DELIVERING" quote box at the top of every visit (move it to the foot). The page is 4,700 px, or 5.2 phone screens.
15. **The Sandbox pill. Shrink it.** A 30 px dashed pill heads every screen. Make it a small neutral "Trial" tag beside the title. It earns that much, since you must know you're not on production, and no more.
16. **Work ahead's repeats. Cut** the "ESTIMATE … · your Chief of Staff, 18 Oct" attribution on every card (say it once at the top), "No changes yet" ×2, "No plan recorded yet", and the note about the 16 Oct pick. Milestones 1 and 2 ("waiting on your acceptance") are asks, so they become board cards: "Accept: What a good desk looks like is known".
17. **Queue's empty groups. Cut.** Personal-Org-Operating-Model shows 5 boxes reading "NONE" under "Nothing waits on you". Show the sentence alone.
18. **Rewording.** "Your merges?" is a stub with no answer; cut it. The button "Team: Waiting on you, the oldest for 3 d 0 h ›" leads to Team but talks about the queue; cut it. On Time asked, show "proxy" only when it's true.

**Records the desk can simply stop showing** (not show better): standard milestones; empty "why" fields; sprints not tied to a milestone; run and iteration numbers (Design i2) beyond "second time through"; idle roles' "last:" lines; session file paths and line numbers; helper sessions' token rows on role pages (they belong in Agents); Personal-Org-Operating-Model's v2.5.1 reduced-view fields; your own 30-row running log (GitHub already holds it); "Done today (0)"; "CI on main: passing" (show CI only when it fails, exceptions first); the GitHub Actions setup line.

## 5. The proposed shape

**Opening screen: the board (v5), across all projects.** Its title is the count, "5 waiting on you · 1 kicked back", with "Trial" beside it and "checked 2 min ago" in meta. The lanes run left to right: Waiting on you, Kicked back, Done. Under the lanes there is nothing else.

**Navigation: one bottom bar, three labelled tabs.** Labels are 11 px at weight 600, quieter than the content. A badge shows on Waiting only.

| Tab | Holds | Reached from it |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting** | The board | Tap a card to open its sheet; drag it to Done |
| **Plan** | Work ahead across projects: milestones ahead in your order, rough finish per row, ▲▼ or drag to reorder, one "Record plan" button | Tap a milestone for a sheet: done when, why (only if recorded), sprints, estimate |
| **Projects** | One row per project: name, phase word, next milestone, and a glyph only if CI fails or something is kicked back | Tap a project for its page (below) |

**The project page** (one tap down from Projects) has three blocks, top-down:
- the status sentence and the 4-segment phase strip;
- "At work now", one line, which opens the roles in a sheet;
- Milestones, its own only, in time order, with the "NOW · 19 OCT" divider; reached milestones collapse to "3 reached ▸".

**The Details corner**, at the foot of Projects, is a plain list: Agents and usage (sessions, timelines, context-window figures), Value targets (V1 to V5, including Time asked), Record gaps, Notifications setup.

**Type scale: four sizes, two weights for text.**

| Token | Size / weight | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| title | 20 px / 700 | Screen title (the count line), sheet title |
| body | 15 px / 400 | Sheet text, the project sentence |
| item | 13 px / 600 | Card and row titles |
| meta | 11 px / 400 or 600 | Project, ref, age, lane headers, tab labels |

Nothing goes below 11 px. That keeps your "small because it's close to my face" and the floor, and it replaces v5's 10 px.

**Colour roles.** There are five neutrals: page, surface, line, ink and ink-2. There is one accent, and it means only "needs you" and your action. There are two status colours: green for confirmed or passing, and crimson for failing or blocked (used only on project rows). Kicked back uses the accent plus a ↩ glyph and a reason, because it also needs you. Phases get no colour.

**Card anatomy (lane card):**
- title, item size, up to 3 lines;
- meta line: `Service-Desk · #135 · 3 d`, in mono for ref and age;
- the reason, only when kicked back, on a 6% accent tint;
- one full-width verb pill: Answer ↗, Merge ↗, Verdict ↗ or Approve ↗.

The card has a fill and no border, no left rule and no shadow. Tap targets are at least 36 px.

**Row anatomy (Plan, Projects, Milestones):**
- left: glyph plus word;
- title, with the meta line under it;
- right: a single right-aligned value (rough finish, or phase).

Rows are separated by hairlines.

**Sheet anatomy.**
- It opens at medium height, about 55%, with a grabber.
- Title, then a meta line (project · ref · waiting 3 d), then 1 to 3 lines of what you're deciding.
- For multi-question asks: one row per question with option chips, the recommendation tagged.
- One accent button, "Answer on GitHub ↗". A secondary row holds Done ✓ and Close.
- "More ▸" opens the full text, the file it writes and the link to GitHub.
- Drag the sheet up for the rest.

**Empty state.** The title reads "Nothing waiting on you". The meta reads "checked 2 min ago · 2 projects". Today's Done cards stay, and the empty lanes collapse. Failure looks different: "Couldn't reach GitHub · last good check 13:12 · Retry", and it never says "nothing waiting".

**What makes it distinctive rather than a kit or a Linear clone.** Linear is a list of issues with status icons in indigo. This desk is a **dispatch rail**:
- tickets move left to right;
- each carries one verb;
- a "Kicked back" lane exists because the desk checks your Done against GitHub, a behaviour no generic kit has;
- times and refs are in mono, like a ticket stub;
- one hot accent, no gradients, no avatars, no shadows;
- labels in your own plain voice ("Merge", "Kicked back", "checked 2 min ago").

Behaviour stays standard: tab bar, sheet, list.

**Deliberate choices of type and accent.**
- **Type: IBM Plex Sans (400/600/700) with IBM Plex Mono for refs, ages and times.** It is engineered rather than friendly, its tabular figures keep "3 d 0 h" aligned, and its mono gives refs a ticket-stub texture. It is not Inter, the face of the AI default kit. Cost: two to three font files from Google Fonts (OFL).
- **Accent: keep signal vermilion, #D23C0F in light and #FF6A3D in dark.** You have already learned it as "needs you", and it reads at a glance on a phone held in passing, like hi-vis. Every other colour gives way to it.

## 6. A design brief draft, for a future Design reviewer

Every rule below can be checked on a screenshot at 414×896 or in the CSS and computed-style dump.

1. **One job per screen.** Every screen's title says its job in six words or fewer, and nothing above the title but the "Trial" tag.
2. **Waiting comes first.** On the opening screen, the top 400 CSS px holds the waiting count and the first waiting item with its verb.
3. **No empty fields.** No field reading "not recorded", "not available", "none" or "no … yet" renders in Waiting, Plan, Projects or a project page unless you must act on it. Only Details › Record gaps may contain them. Check: a grep of the rendered text finds zero.
4. **At most 4 text sizes and 3 weights** across all screens, and no text under 11 px. Check: the capture script's FONT inventory.
5. **Colour from tokens only.** At most 5 neutrals, 1 accent and 2 status colours, all as custom properties on `:root`, with no literal colours outside it. No screen shows more than 10 distinct computed colours per theme.
6. **The accent marks only "needs you" and the primary action.** It appears on no heading, phase or decoration.
7. **One filled button per card, and at most one per sheet or screen.**
8. **No legends.** No screen carries a legend or key. Every status glyph has a word in the same row.
9. **No nested boxes.** Uniform items are rows or lane cards with a fill or a hairline, never border, left rule and shadow together, and never a card inside a card.
10. **One app-level navigation bar** of at most 3 labelled tabs. Its label weight is no heavier than the content's. There is no per-project tab bar and no link to the screen you're on.
11. **Details open in a sheet** at 60% height or less. Before any scrolling, the sheet shows the title, what's being decided and the primary action.
12. **At most one free-text box per sheet.** Choices are tappable options.
13. **Record IDs** (P-, Q-, PR #, M-codes, run numbers) appear only in mono meta lines, never in a title.
14. **Quiet is calm.** An empty view shows one sentence plus "checked N min ago". A failure uses different wording and never says "nothing waiting".
15. **Pages stay short.** No main-path screen is longer than 2 phone screens (1,800 CSS px) with fixture data; longer content goes behind "Show all". A tip shows once and can be dismissed, and no footnote stays in the main path.

## 7. What to keep

- **The prefilled-link route.** "Open GitHub with this written", together with the ruling preview and the inline check ("No answer yet: line 1 names no number"), is the desk's core verb and it works. Keep it, behind "More".
- **The honest count line.** "7 waiting on you in all · last read 13:12" is plain, true and time-stamped, so it serves V1 and the trust that quiet is real.
- **Vermilion means "needs you"**, with a diamond and a word, used the same way on every screen. Keep the meaning; spend it more sparingly.
- **Plain-English milestones with "Done when"**, such as "The desk live at the needs-you address · Done when the owner's needs-you address opens the desk on production". Written for you, not for agents.
- **The "NOW · 19 OCT · ahead above · done below" divider.** A strong anchor; keep it and flip the order to top-down.
- **Rough finish ranges with "An expectation, never a deadline."** Honest estimating, exactly M3A's job.
- **Waiting age on each item** ("waiting 3 d 0 h"). It is V5 made visible.
- **"Being asked in your session: answer there."** It stops you answering twice. Keep it as a card state on the board.
- **Board v5's mechanics:** Kicked back, Undo, "confirming with GitHub" in the row, one verb per card, the half-height sheet.
- **Dark mode** holds up (01-home-dark), and the labelled tab bar with a count badge is the right pattern, just one level up.

## 8. Open choices for you

1. **One board or a board per project?** (still open in P-001-board-scope)
   - A: one board, all projects mixed, project name on each card.
   - B: lanes split into project sections.
   - C: one board with a project filter at the top, default "All".
   - *Recommended: A*, adding C's filter only beyond three projects. You think in "what waits on me", not "which repo".
2. **Navigation.**
   - A: three tabs, Waiting · Plan · Projects.
   - B: one scrolling screen: board, then "Next up" (the next 3 milestones), then the project rows, with Details at the foot.
   - *Recommended: A*: the lanes need the full height and drag, and Plan has its own job. B is fine if you'd rather have no chrome at all.
3. **Type and accent.**
   - A: IBM Plex Sans and Plex Mono, with vermilion.
   - B: the phone's own SF Pro and SF Mono, with vermilion (looks native, no font download, less of its own character).
   - C: Plex with a new accent, such as a deep signal blue, letting vermilion retire.
   - *Recommended: A*: it is a deliberate, non-default choice that answers "not an AI doppelganger", and it keeps the colour you already read as "needs you".
