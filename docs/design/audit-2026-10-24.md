**Design audit of the whole desk, 2026-10-24: catching what matters at a glance (asked for by the owner, outside the usual schedule)**

You already know where everything is. What's missing is that the most important thing on each screen doesn't stand out more than the rest. Three things are nearly invisible at a glance today:
- **A project that has stopped moving.** Home can say "Nothing needs you" with a tick. The only sign that a project hasn't moved for 8 days is a pale grey line at the very bottom of its tile.
- **A project the desk can't read.** Its tile says "Can't read" in the same pale grey as "Nothing waiting". When you're signed out, Home still says "Nothing new since yesterday".
- **Your own number.** "9 need you" is in smaller, lighter type than the word "Home" above it. The time when a question will settle itself is the last few words of a sentence.

On Waiting, two empty columns take almost two-thirds of the phone's width, so the column that matters is about 117 px wide and long titles get cut off. Below are 11 small changes, most useful first. Each one says why. Most reuse the sizes and colours the desk already has; none adds a colour. A few change something the approved mockup drew, so they are yours to choose. Nothing in this audit changed the desk.

## Ranked proposals

**1. A stopped project is hidden under a calm Home (the most important gap)**
- **Where:** a Home tile, "No movement for 8 days · last: PR #77 merged, 16 Oct", under the briefing "Nothing needs you".
- **Now:**
  - The line is 12 px/400 in grey (`--ink2`, #5c5f66 on #fbfbfb). It is the last line of the tile, at y=512 (360 px).
  - It is the same colour and size as "No agent run recorded since 8 Oct" and "no summary in this project's README" above it.
  - Above the tile, the briefing shows "Nothing needs you" at 15 px/500 with a tick, and the tile's corner shows "Nothing waiting" in grey.
  - Everything around it reads as calm.
  - Styles: sandbox.css:199 and :799; sandbox.js:515-524 and :871; feed.js:869-897.
  - Captures: `stall/stall-home-360-light.png` and `-dark.png`.
- **Proposed:**
  - The line takes the tile's ink line role (12 px/500, `--ink`) with an icon from the set already used (the clock).
  - It moves up to sit directly under the tile's name row, where the eye already goes for "N wait on you".
  - No vermilion: this gap is the agents' to fill.
- **Rationale:** You asked for this signal "so a quiet desk can't hide a stopped project". Today the desk does exactly that: the tick and "Nothing needs you" win, and the only warning is the faintest line on the screen. This is the one case where "quiet" is misleading, and it is the purpose of this ruling. Ink plus an icon is the smallest step that makes it show without colour, and vermilion stays only on what needs you. The cost is one more line in ink on a calm tile. It appears only after 3 days with no movement and nothing waiting, so it is rare.
- **Serves:** P-001-trial-view-notes-2 note 34 and its follow-up answer ("a quiet desk can't hide a stopped project"); part A items 4 and 14.
- **Kind:** a fix within your rulings. S-001 AC71's "in slate" would need the Definer.

**4. Waiting: the column that matters gets about a third of the width**
