claim: "WCAG 2.2 Understanding pages state: SC 1.4.1 (Level A) 'Color is not used as the only visual means of conveying information, indicating an action, prompting a response, or distinguishing a visual element.'; SC 1.4.3 (AA) text contrast at least 4.5:1, large-scale text at least 3:1, large scale being at least 18 point or 14 point bold; SC 1.4.11 (AA) at least 3:1 against adjacent colour(s) for UI components and graphical objects (examples: 'Status icons on an application's dashboard (without associated text) have a 3:1 minimum contrast ratio'; 'A graph uses a light background and ensures that the colors for each line have a 3:1 contrast ratio against the background.'); SC 2.5.8 (AA) target at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels, exceptions: spacing, equivalent, inline, user agent control, essential; SC 2.5.5 (AAA) at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels, exceptions: equivalent, inline, user agent control, essential; SC 2.3.3 (AAA) 'Motion animation triggered by interaction can be disabled, unless the animation is essential to the functionality or the information being conveyed.' (example respects prefers-reduced-motion); SC 2.2.2 (A) auto-updating information that starts automatically and is shown in parallel with other content needs a mechanism to pause, stop, hide or control frequency 'unless the auto-updating is part of an activity where it is essential' (intent lists weather, news, stock price updates); SC 4.1.3 (AA) status messages can be programmatically determined through role or properties so assistive technology can present them without receiving focus. Not documented: whether a screen whose whole purpose is live status counts as 'essential' under 2.2.2; gauges and progress indicators are not named under 1.4.11."
