# Ruling needed: Should finished and failed marks be green and red, on the mark only?

Milestone: none (a design follow-up after M3D; the planner gives it a milestone when it is built)
Asked in session: session_017seyaGqUxAff6E8dKWHDCR

WHY: You asked for a limited palette for what is going well and what isn't. The desk already has a green (--ok) and a red (--bad) but almost never uses them: reached milestones, merged sprints and failed checks are all grey or black. This changes your earlier rulings that statuses show by icon, not colour (trial-view notes-2 note 23 and note 28's pick, notes-1 "no colour", S-001 AC48, P-001-timeline-labels' "colour is left for what needs the owner"). Proposed with it: the stall line stays ink with a clock, a milestone reached in a run that was later gone back over stays grey (history), and every absence ("Nothing needs you") stays grey. Left unruled, it stays as built.
OPTIONS:
  A. Green and red marks — A reached or passed mark (✓) turns green and a failed or not-reached mark (✕) red, on the 14–16 px mark only, never on words, rows or borders; vermilion stays what needs you, gold stays next to reach; no third colour. The red tile border on an unreadable project goes.
  B. A, with a deeper red — As A, and red is retuned (#a8123a light, #ff5c7a dark) to sit further from vermilion now that it appears more often.
  C. As built — Marks stay grey and black.
RECOMMENDATION: A — the two colours already exist and pass contrast; marks-only keeps the desk calm. B only if the red reads too close to vermilion on your phone.
RISK CLASS: R0   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: Status marks across every screen
DEFAULT: C   TIMEOUT: 168
