# Ruling needed: how may the library file a tool's facts from its changelog, from pages that disagree, or from a single page?

Milestone: Discovery-5

WHY: The research on how events are classified has stopped a second time. Most of its findings are filed, nine of them since the last stop, but three small facts about two tools, Linear and incident.io, are held, and your source rule does not say how to file any of them. One is that Linear now gives duplicate issues a status of their own: Linear's changelog says so, and one of its guide pages describes the older behaviour; the rule does not say whether a changelog is one of a tool's pages, nor what to do when a tool's own pages disagree. The other two each appear on one page of the tool's documentation, and the rule needs two; the checker asked the Researcher to find a second page or drop them, and the Researcher kept them on hold instead. The Researcher says none of the three is needed to answer the research question, but it will not drop them, and the research cannot finish while they are held. Until you rule, sending it back is expected to stop again, and Discovery's research milestone is not reached.

OPTIONS:
  A. File all three, each marked for what it is — three lines are added to the source rule the checker reads, a governance change you approve, merged as its own pull request; then the card's `re-specify` sends the research back. The lines: (1) a tool's changelog on its own site counts as one of its documentation pages for how it behaves; (2) where a tool's own pages disagree, an entry may record what each page says, quoting each with its page and any date it gives, without stating which is current, and it is not load-bearing; (3) a fact about how a tool behaves that is found on only one of its pages may be filed marked "single page", and it is not load-bearing. Not load-bearing means no plan decision may rest on it. The three held facts can then be filed under those marks. Cost: the rule widens by three lines for all later research, and the library holds facts that cannot carry a decision. It rules out leaving a tool's documented behaviour out of the library because its documentation is thin or inconsistent.
  B. File the changelog and disagreement case only — lines (1) and (2) of A are added, a governance change you approve; then `re-specify`. The Linear duplicate fact can be filed as a recorded disagreement. The two single-page facts stay held unless the Researcher drops them, which it has so far declined to do, so the research may stop again on them. Cost: the rule widens by two lines, and a third stop card is possible. It rules out filing a fact found on only one of a tool's pages.
  C. No change — no rule changes; the card's `re-specify` sends the research back at once. All three facts stay held unless the Researcher drops them, and nothing it receives asks it to drop the duplicate fact, so the research is likely to stop again. Cost: one more round of the loop, likely wasted, and a third stop card. It rules out filing any of the three.
RECOMMENDATION: A — it is the only option under which every held fact has a way to be filed, so the research is expected to finish, and marking each as not load-bearing keeps any plan decision from resting on thin or inconsistent documentation.
