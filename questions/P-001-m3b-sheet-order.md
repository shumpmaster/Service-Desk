# Ruling needed: On the targets' sheet, quote first or measure first?

Milestone: M3B
Asked in session: session_017seyaGqUxAff6E8dKWHDCR

WHY: The picture leads each target with its full quoted line (up to three lines), then the measure in smaller text under it. With five targets, the quotes fill the top of the sheet and the measure, which is what you open it to see, is easy to miss (the Design reviewer's note 2). Left unruled, it is built as drawn.
OPTIONS:
  A. As drawn — each target's quoted line, then its measure under it.
  B. Measure first — each target's name and its measure first ("V1 Missed decisions · 0 missed · On track"), its full quoted line one tap away.
RECOMMENDATION: B — the sheet answers "are we meeting it?" at a glance, and the target's words stay one tap away.
