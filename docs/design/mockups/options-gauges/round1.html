Circle icons with percent gauges

You asked for the circle icons with percent gauges, in red, yellow and green. Today, in v12, a milestone is a small circle: filled when reached, hollow when planned, a square for your own milestones. Phases have no icon, and sprints and steps have none on the line.

Then three ways to draw the gauge, on the Design phase. Each one uses the same colours: green on track, yellow waiting on you or at risk, red stopped or blocked, and no colour for not started. Red keeps its meaning of a problem. The percentages marked “example” are made up: what a gauge counts is the next question.
