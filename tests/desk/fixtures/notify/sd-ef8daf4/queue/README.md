# The owner's queue (S-012)

Card stubs, `<item>-<gate>-<card>.md`, written by the Orchestrator in the decision-card form. The
Chief of Staff adds its part in `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<card>-notes.md`.

`queue/merge/<item>-<step>.md` holds the merge gate's notes (S-013 AC5): why an item was not merged
at a step. They are notes, not cards; a failure that needs the owner also raises a card here.
