Home as a map: three ways

You asked what it would take to make the first page more like the horizontal timeline we built. I read that as the home page, and as the branch map that floats at the top of a project in v13. Each option is drawn on the real v13 home page with its real projects and what waits on you. The activity before today is an example.

The soft status colours are the only colour: red for failed or stopped, yellow for waiting on you, green for done. Now stays blue. Each picture is shown in light and dark.

Reference

The horizontal timeline: v13's branch map in a project

Inside a project, the whole phase floats at the top, oldest on the left, main along the bottom. The events in view show their icons and the rest are dots.

As it is

v13's home page today

Needs you comes first, with five rows. Under it, Projects is a column of six cards, each saying in one sentence what is happening. Nothing on the page shows time.

Option A

A small map in each project card

  • The card's sentence becomes a thin strip of the last two weeks: main, its branches, events as dots, Now in blue at the right end.
  • Whatever waits on you shows as its icon in soft red or yellow, with a tag under the strip ("Stopped · your call").
  • Changes the least. Five strips stacked can feel busy, and the sentence that says what happened is gone.
Option B · recommended

One map for the whole desk, floating at the top

  • Each project is one thin lane on a shared time axis, from two weeks ago to now. Time stretches toward now, so today's events have room.
  • Whatever waits on you sits on its lane as its icon: the timeline's stop and questions, the widget's failed readiness check. Everything else is a dot.
  • It floats like the project's map, so it stays as you scroll down to Needs you and the cards. The cards drop their sentence, since the map now says what moved.
  • In one look: which projects moved, which are quiet, and where you are needed. Home works like a project page, with a map on top and a list below.
Option C

A row of phase gauges in each card

  • The card's sentence becomes the project's phases as gauges, the current one raised, using the same gauges as the project page.
  • Shows where each project stands. It is the calmest option, but it has no time axis and says nothing about what happened lately.
  • Only the timeline and the widget have plans in this mockup. The other three projects' phases are examples and are marked as such.
