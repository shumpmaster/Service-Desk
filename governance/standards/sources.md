# Source standard — what counts as an independent source

*Owner ruling A, 2026-10-01 (decisions/questions/Q-005-sources.md), widened by owner ruling A,
2026-10-03 (decisions/questions/Q-006-vendor-sources.md), and by owner ruling A, 2026-11-12
(decisions/questions/P-005-spec-sources.md). The Source checker reads this file; it
states a checking rule, not any answer a question hopes for (D-055). It says only what the owner
ruled; anything wider needs its own ruling.*

- For facts about how a vendor's own product behaves (its limits and behaviour), two different
  pages of that vendor's documentation count as two sources, labelled "same publisher".
- For what a published specification itself requires, one quoted passage of that specification is
  enough; where its pages state the requirement at different strengths, the entry quotes the one it
  rests on and notes the others. A specification's text is never evidence of what others do in practice.
- Claims about cost, risk or quality still need an independent source.
