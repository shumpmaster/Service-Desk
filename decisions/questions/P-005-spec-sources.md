Ruling: A — a published specification's own quoted text is enough to source what that specification requires, and is never evidence of what others do in practice

Question: questions/P-005-spec-sources.md
Milestone: Discovery-5

Question, as put: Can a published specification's own text be the source for what that specification requires?
Options, as put:
  A. Yes, one quoted passage is enough — a specification's text is the source for what that specification requires, and is never evidence of what others do in practice.
  B. Yes, but only with two of its pages, labelled "same publisher", as for a vendor's own documentation.
  C. No: a specification's text needs two independent publishers for a load-bearing claim not about a vendor's own product.
