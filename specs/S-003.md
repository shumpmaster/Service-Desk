## Acceptance criteria (scenarios; for the owner's approval on the scenario card, not yet approved)
Each criterion is a plain scenario traced to V6: *decision cards shown to the owner with neither the Chief of Staff's recommended button marked nor the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, 0, from the day M4 is live to 2026-12-31*. A V6 miss is also any decision card held off the queue more than a day after it was raised (plan §6).

"The records" below means queue/, the notes files and the dispatch log as they stand on the main branch; nothing else (no memory of earlier reads, nothing kept on a device, nothing about how a read went) decides a decision card's state or which word it shows. A **read** is the desk's or the sender's reading of those records; a read that did not get queue/, the notes or the dispatch log has not got the records, and gives no decision card a state (AC16). A card's **age** at a moment is that moment minus its raise time as recorded in its `card` entry, never reset or adjusted; "raised less than a day ago" means an age under 24 hours, including a negative age (a recorded raise time later than the moment, J1), and "a day ago or more" means an age of 24 hours or more.

### Holding and showing (the rule, D1, and what the owner sees, D2)
- **AC1** [trace: V6 → AC1] — When a decision card is in queue/ and its notes are not on the main branch, and it was raised less than a day ago, then the desk holds it: it is not on the owner's queue, not in Home's count of what needs the owner, not on the board, no view offers its answer buttons (a direct link or a notice to it opens what the desk opens today for a card not on its list, AC15), and no notification is sent for it.
- **AC2** [trace: V6 → AC2] — When a held decision card's notes reach the main branch with a recommended word, then on the open desk's next read the card shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board; its card sheet says the Chief of Staff recommends that word, and that word's button, and only that one, is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent at the sender's next run (AC14).
- **AC3** [trace: V6 → AC3] — When a decision card's card sheet shows, then its own section 5 marks no button, whatever word it names: on a decision card only the notes' recommended word marks a button. (Gate cards keep today's behaviour, AC9.)
- **AC4** [trace: V6 → AC4] — When a decision card was raised a day (24 hours) ago or more by its `card` entry in the dispatch log and still has no notes on the main branch, then it shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board, its card sheet carries the mark "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet", and no button is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent at the sender's next run (AC14). If the desk is open as the day passes, the card shows on the desk's next read, without a reload.
- **AC5** [trace: V6 → AC5] — When a card shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark later gets notes with a recommended word on the main branch, then on the desk's next read the mark is gone and the card shows the notes' recommended word with that word's button marked Recommended, as in AC2. Notification: as AC14 says.
- **AC6** [trace: V6 → AC6] — When a decision card's notes are on the main branch but have no recommended word (no `Recommendation:` line as J2 defines it; no backticked word on that line; or no backticked word on it that is one of the card's words), then the desk treats the card as having no notes: held under AC1 within its first day, shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark under AC4 after it, and never with a button marked.
- **AC7** [trace: V6 → AC7] — When the dispatch log on the main branch, as the desk's existing log reader reads it, has no `card` entry for a decision card (for example a card older than the part of the log that reader reads), then the card's raise time is not recorded and the card shows at once: as in AC4, with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, or as in AC2 if its notes with a recommended word are on the main branch. (A read that did not get the log at all is not this case: AC16.)
- **AC8** [trace: V6 → AC8] — When notes for a decision card exist only on a branch other than the main branch (for example in an open pull request), then the desk treats the card as having no notes.

### Unchanged
- **AC9** [trace: V6 → AC9] — When a card is a gate card (`dor`, `criteria` or `launch`), an owner question, or a pull-request card, then it shows, counts and notifies exactly as before this fix, with its Recommended mark from its gate case as today, and the desk does not look for notes for it.
- **AC10** [trace: V6 → AC10] — When the desk reads notes, then it reads them through the repository reads it already makes, writes nothing to any repository, and sends nothing to any new outside service; the desk's spend stays $0 (plan §1 Out, §5).

### The views and the sender agree, from the records alone
- **AC11** [trace: V6 → AC11] — When the queue, Home's count, the board, the card sheet (including the hand-written one in src/public/sandbox.js) and the notification sender are given the same records and the same moment, then each decision card's state, held, shown with a recommended word (and which word), or shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, is the same in all of them, because each takes the card list the one rule makes from those records and that moment and from nothing else. The same records and moment always give the same state, whatever was read before: a desk open for hours, a desk just opened, and the sender all agree. A read that did not get the records gives no state at all (AC16), never a different one. (Whether a notice is sent is not a card's state; AC14 and AC15 cover it.)
- **AC16** [trace: V6 → AC16] — When a read of the desk or of the sender does not get the dispatch log (or queue/, or the notes files), then that read gives no decision card a state: from it no decision card is shown on the queue, counted in Home, put on the board or offered with answer buttons, and the sender sends no notice for a decision card on that run; instead the queue and Home show the line "Decision cards not shown: the dispatch log could not be read" (naming what could not be read), until a read gets the records, when the rule applies as in AC1 to AC8 with each card's real age. Gate cards, questions and pull-request cards are not affected (AC9). *This departs from the plan's §1 ("or the log not yet read ... shows at once") to follow the owner's later ruling (decisions/P-003/stop-5.md); it needs the owner's ruling before freeze, open question 1.*

### Notifications

## Join sheets
- **J1 —** queue/ and the dispatch log on the main branch → the rule (D1): for each card file `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md` (for example `queue/Q-003-failure-1.md`), its kind from `<gate>` and its raise time from its `card` entry in the dispatch log the desk already reads (plan §7). Decision card if `<gate>` is `dor-fail`, `stop` or `failure`; gate card if `dor`, `criteria` or `launch`. Awkward cases: a `dor-fail` card must not be read as a `dor` gate card by matching the prefix `dor` (it is a decision card); no `card` entry in the log as read (AC7: shown at once); the log not got by the read (AC16: no decision card gets a state); two `card` entries for one card (the earliest counts); a recorded raise time later than the moment of the read, from clock skew (age is always measured from the recorded time and never reset, so a negative age counts as less than a day: the card is held until the recorded time plus 24 hours, then shown; for example, recorded 2026-11-10 10:00 and read at 09:58 that day → held; read at 2026-11-11 09:59 → held; read at 2026-11-11 10:00 → shown; the same records and moment give the same answer everywhere, AC11); a recorded raise time far in the future, from a malformed entry, follows the same rule, so it is held until that time plus 24 hours (see Risks). The exact field names of the log's `card` entry are those of the desk's existing log reader in src/lib/ (research/P-003-sizing-findings.md §1); the Builder states them in the J1 test. A test marked `join-test: S-003/J1` covers it.
- **J2 —** the main branch's file tree and a notes file → the rule (D1): whether `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<n>-notes.md` exists on the main branch for card `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md`, and if so its recommended word. The notes' `Recommendation:` line is the first line of the file that, once any leading heading marks (`#`, then spaces) and any bold or italic marks (`**`, `*`, `_`) at its start are removed, begins with `Recommendation:`; a line that only mentions `Recommendation:` later in its text is not it. The recommended word is the first backticked word on that line that is one of the card's words (the words its buttons offer), matched exactly. Examples, for a card whose words are `drop`, `re-specify`, `resubmit`:
  - `Recommendation: \`drop\`` → `drop`
  - `**Recommendation:** \`drop\` — the scope is gone` → `drop`
  - `_Recommendation:_ \`resubmit\`` → `resubmit`
  - `## Recommendation: \`re-specify\`` → `re-specify`
  - `Recommendation: not \`proceed\`; \`drop\`` → `drop` (`proceed` is not one of the card's words)
  - `Recommendation: \`Drop\`` → none (not an exact match), so no notes (AC6)
  - `Recommendation: drop` (no backticks) → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - an earlier prose line `The Recommendation: line below says \`resubmit\`` followed by `Recommendation: \`drop\`` → `drop` (the prose line does not begin with `Recommendation:`)
  - no line beginning with `Recommendation:` → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - two lines beginning with `Recommendation:` → the first decides; if it has no recommended word, none (the second is not read)
  - notes for `stop-2` beside a card `stop-1` → not this card's notes
  A test marked `join-test: S-003/J2` covers it.
- **J3 —** the rule (D1) → the one card list every view reads: each decision card carries one state, `held`, `shown, recommended <word>`, or `shown, no recommendation yet`, worked out from the records and the moment alone (AC11); nothing about earlier reads is carried. A read that did not get the records gives the list no decision cards and one flag, `decision cards not read: <what could not be read>` (AC16). Gate cards, questions and pull-request cards carry no new state. Awkward cases: a card answered while held (it leaves the list as any answered card does); notes arriving in the same read as the day passing (the notes win, AC2); notes removed from the main branch after a card showed with their word (the state is worked out afresh: `shown, no recommendation yet` if it was raised a day ago or more or its raise time is not recorded, `held` if it was raised less than a day ago); a card file on the main branch before its `card` entry is (shown at once, AC7; if the entry then arrives with a raise time less than a day before the read, it is `held` until that time plus 24 hours, as the records say). Those last two are the only ways a shown card goes back to held. A test marked `join-test: S-003/J3` covers it.
- **J4 —** the card list → the views (queue, Home's count, board, card sheet in src/public/sandbox.js and its generated copies): `held` cards appear in none, and no view offers their answer buttons; `shown, recommended <word>` cards show the word in the card sheet and mark that button Recommended; `shown, no recommendation yet` cards show the mark and mark no button; the `decision cards not read` flag shows its line on the queue and on Home (AC16). Awkward cases: a card sheet opened from a direct link to a held card, or from a notice sent before the card went back to held (it opens what the desk opens today for a card not on its list, AC15); the card sheet left open while the card moves from `shown, no recommendation yet` to `shown, recommended <word>` (the mark gives way on the next read). A test marked `join-test: S-003/J4` covers it.
- **J5 —** the card list → the notification sender: the sender takes the same card list (src/notify/collect.mjs already runs the desk's card-making code outside the browser, docs/PROJECT.md:588), so a held decision card is not on the list it notifies from, and a shown one is. Handed over: the card list at that run (each card's id, kind and state, and the AC16 flag if the read did not get the records). Nothing in the sender's own files changes; its existing way of not repeating a notice applies to decision cards as to any card (AC14). Awkward cases: the sender's read not getting the log (AC16: no notice for any decision card on that run; gate cards as today); a card that shows and is answered between two sender runs (no notification is owed); a notice already sent for a card that goes back to `held` (nothing withdrawn, nothing sent while held, AC15). If meeting AC1, AC2 or AC14 turns out to need a change to src/notify/**, the build stops and comes back to the owner (plan §4); the spec is then revised, not built around. A test marked `join-test: S-003/J5` covers it.

## Risks and mitigations
- A card held for good — AC4 (a day) and AC7 (no recorded raise time: shown at once); J1 measures age from the recorded raise time and never resets it, so clock skew delays a card only by the skew. A malformed raise time far in the future would hold a card until that time plus a day; it would count as a V6 miss, which AC13 lets the Chief of Staff's value check find from the records. Accepted, since the plan defines "raised" by the log entry; open question 3.
- A card hidden while the dispatch log cannot be read — AC16 shows the line "Decision cards not shown: the dispatch log could not be read" on the queue and Home, so it is never silent; a card held this way more than a day after it was raised is a V6 miss. Open question 1.
- A button marked that the Chief of Staff did not recommend — exact matching in J2 and AC6 (anything unclear counts as no notes); J2's line rule stops a prose mention deciding the word; AC3 stops section 5 marking a decision card.
- The owner told twice — no notice while held (AC1); for a word arriving later, or a card that goes back to held and shows again, the sender behaves as today (AC14); open question 2.
- A notice for a card that then goes back to held, opening to "gone" — rare (J3: only notes removed within a card's first day, or a `card` entry reaching the main branch after its card); the card shows again within a day of its raise time; accepted, AC15.
- A gate card changed by mistake — AC9 and J1's `dor-fail` / `dor` case.
- The views or the sender disagreeing — AC11: one rule, on the records and the moment alone; AC16: a read without the records gives no state rather than a different one.
- The build passing the stop rule — the restated size is near it on tests; the Builder reports lines and tests against the stop rule, and the stop rule applies unchanged.
- The owner leaning on the mark without reading the reasons (automation bias "cannot be prevented by training or instructions", library/facts/F-automation-complacency-01.md, B) — the card sheet still shows the card in full; the mark only names the Chief of Staff's word. Accepted, as the owner asked for the mark.

## Open questions for the owner

The plan's section 1 says a card whose raise time is not recorded because "the log not yet read" shows at once. 2. Not holding the criteria up. AC14: this spec leaves the notification sender as it is. If today's sender tells you again when a card's recommendation arrives after it showed without one, or when a card shows a second time, should a later change stop that? That would change the sender's own files, which needs your approval, and would be a new spec.
3. Not holding the criteria up. J1: a raise time far in the future in the dispatch log (a malformed entry) holds the card until that time plus a day, as the plan's meaning of "raised" gives. It would show up as a miss in the Chief of Staff's value check. Is that acceptable, or do you want a later change for it?
