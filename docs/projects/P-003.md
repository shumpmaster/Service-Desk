## 1. Scope
*What is in, what is explicitly out, and where it must work (target devices or places).*

A **decision card** is a card under queue/ that is not a gate card: a `dor-fail`, `stop` or `failure` card. A **gate card** is a card for a phase gate (`dor`, `criteria` or `launch`), whose recommendation comes from its gate case. A decision card's **notes** are the Chief of Staff's notes file for it, `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<n>-notes.md`, and the notes' **recommended word** is the first backticked word on the notes' `Recommendation:` line that is one of the card's words, whatever bold or heading marks surround the line.

In:
- A decision card whose notes are not on the main branch is held off the owner's queue, off Home's count of what needs the owner, and out of the desk's notifications.
- Once its notes are on the main branch, the card shows with the notes' recommended word and that word's button marked Recommended; its notification is sent when it shows.
- If no notes have arrived a day after the card was raised (its `card` entry in the dispatch log), the card shows anyway, marked "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet", with no button marked; if notes arrive later, the mark gives way to their recommendation.
- Notes with no recommended word count as no notes. A decision card whose raise time is not recorded (older than the log the desk reads, or the log not yet read) shows at once with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, so no card is ever held for good.

Out:
- Gate cards, questions and pull-request cards: unchanged.
- How cards and notes are written, the Orchestrator, and the operating model: unchanged. The desk reads one new kind of record, the notes files, and still writes nothing.

Where it must work: on the desk, on the owner's phone and computer, in the browsers the desk already supports, through its preview site and then its live address; nothing else.

If the desk holds a decision card until its notes are in and then shows their word with its button marked, the owner sees the recommendation where they answer, and value target V6 is met.

# Part 2 — The intent

## Intent
The owner's purpose, preferences and trade-offs for this project, and what they would hate. Drafted by
the Chief of Staff from conversations, portfolio defaults and past rulings; confirmed on the
move-to-Design card.

- **Purpose.** The owner answers a decision card knowing what the Chief of Staff recommends, on the card itself. Their words: "Proceed with the fix"; "Well then we need to make sure the cards don't show up until then."; "Show it after a day, flagged (Recommended)" (decisions/questions/P-001-card-recommendation.md).
