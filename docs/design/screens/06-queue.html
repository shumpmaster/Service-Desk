Screens 6 and 7: Queue and response card

Everything waiting on you in one project, grouped by the kind of action it needs, modelled on your monday.com screenshot. Tap an item and the response card slides up, modelled on your Linear screenshot: each answer is spelled out, the recommendation is labelled but never preselected, and it takes two taps through GitHub. Switch between today, a busy moment on 8 October, and the Design gate of 5 October.

What's on these screens, and why

  • The queue is monday.com's board, with one group per kind of action: Decisions, Gate rulings, Verdicts to write, Merges and Deploys. Each group has its title and count, and its rows have a coloured edge. A group with nothing in it shrinks to a single line, so a quiet project stays short. Everything here belongs to this project only.
  • Each row answers what it is, how long it has waited, and whether you can act on it yet. An item that depends on another says so ("after PR #54 and your verdict") and can't be opened until it's ready. The one to do first is marked.
  • The response card is Linear's sheet: grouped rows with one answer per chip.
    • Pick a chip and the card spells out, in plain words, what happens next with that answer.
    • The Chief of Staff's recommendation is labelled, never preselected.
    • Your verdicts get no recommendation at all: that word is yours alone.
  • A gate ruling's card opens with one plain sentence, bad news first, then links the one-page case and the Critic's independent view, and gives the user representative's line. You are Service Desk's only user, so that line reads "not needed". Its four answers are proceed, hold, recycle and kill; hold and recycle need a one-line reason, and the older words (build, re-aim, stop) still work.
  • Two taps. The card opens GitHub with your answer already written, and you tap Commit there. The desk holds no key that can write.
  • Confirmation, monday-style: a small toast at the bottom says what happened next and offers "See it on the timeline", where the step ticks off (screen 4). Meanwhile, the row moves to a "Done today" fold at the bottom of the queue.
  • Real items only.
    • "Right now" shows today's one item, PR #61.
    • "8 Oct, 17:46" replays a busy moment: the Q-012 decision had waited 24 minutes, PRs #54 and #55 were ready to merge, and PR #52 was waiting on the Reviewer before your verdict.
    • The merge timings come from the records; that all four were open together at 17:46 is reconstructed from them.
    • "5 Oct, the Design gate" replays Service Desk's one real gate ruling, before you answered it at 17:14. How long it had waited isn't recorded, so the row doesn't say.
