Rulings:
1. **Agent panel.** One panel across projects that lights up where an agent is working or has just finished; tap an agent to see its work (record, outcome, usage). It also answers "who has the ball": an agent working, queued for the next Orchestrator run (and when that is due), checks running, or waiting on the owner. No invented figures or scores.
2. **Timeline.** One timeline per project, in order, without a time scale: work, reviews, failures, re-issues and blockers, rolling up to milestones and then to phases.
3. **Phases** (fixed for every project, a model change): renamed to Discovery, Design, Build, Launch, Operate, Review, mapped onto the model's existing stages.
4. **Milestones** (per project, a model change): each project's plan names its milestones, each led by a plain-English name, then what done means and its phase; every item is tagged to a milestone. This is level two of the breakdown ("N+2").
5. **Chief of Staff sessions are recorded** (a model change): the Chief of Staff role exists on every project, so its sessions and the subagents it starts are logged like pipeline sessions and appear on the agent panel.
6. **The owner's actions** (from the lab discussion, accepted): a queue of the owner's actions across projects, in the order to act, each with why it is his and what it waits on, acting through prefilled GitHub links (the desk holds no write credential), and confirmation that the action took effect.
