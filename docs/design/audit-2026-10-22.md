# Design audit of the desk, 2026-10-22

The new desk does the job you set it, so nothing here stands in the way of accepting MD3. Two things were left out. The 5-second glance test was not run, as you ruled. There is no made-up Members Letter project to test with yet, so the desk was audited on the five test projects, the six portfolio states and a test-only deploy card. Nothing failed outright. Three things fall short in a small way, and each is a quick fix:
- When one project can't be read, its own Waiting view says "Can't read" without saying since when.
- A deploy's sheet repeats itself and ends on a link called "Its status file".
- A project page's list of what waits shows each card's whole title, so a long one runs to 15 lines on a small phone.

Five things already follow your rulings, so they are choices for you rather than fixes. The biggest is that the same "not yet" reason repeats on every row: on Plan, every one of 21 rows says it has no estimate. You would decide whether those reasons show once per group. Nothing in this audit changes the desk, and every choice can be changed back.

## 1. Verdict in five lines

1. The desk now opens on what needs you. "6 need you" sits at the top of Home and is counted the way the board counts. Waiting is one tap away, with one verb per card.
2. It is light where the 19 Oct audit found it heavy:
   - **Type:** 6 sizes (24, 18, 15, 13, 12, 11 px) and 3 weights, against 9 sizes and weights up to 900.
   - **Colour:** 18 colours per theme, against 27.
   - **Typeface:** one typeface.
   - **Accent:** vermilion only on what waits on you.
   - Nothing is under 4.5:1 and nothing scrolls sideways, in light or dark.
3. What is left of the old "database" feel is repetition rather than clutter. The same "not yet" reason appears once per row: 21 times on Plan, 15 on the Timeline, 5 on Team. The rulings make every gap say who fills it, so how often it says so is your choice.
4. The main-path screens are long. With three projects, Plan is 4.0 phone screens at 360 px and the Timeline 4.4. With five, Waiting is 7.4 and Plan 6.8. Most of Plan's length is that repetition.
5. Three small defects and three small wording or drawing points remain, none of them blocking: an undated "Can't read" in one view, the deploy sheet's wording, and long titles on the page's waits row; then "Your merges?", "Cost: –" and a faint seam on Plan's bubbles.

## 4. The bloat list, by impact

1. **The same "not yet" reason on every row.** These all meet AC69 and the settled point that unknowns are said in words, so this is choice 1, not a fix.
   - **Plan:** each of 21 rows says "Estimate: not made yet" and then "Rough finish: not available: … has no estimate yet".
   - **Timeline:** "Why not recorded yet · for the agents to add" 15 times.
   - **Home:** "why it went back: not recorded" 3 times.
   - **Team:** "not recorded in what the desk reads" on 5 of Service-Desk's 11 tiles.
2. **Fix: an unreadable project's own Waiting view doesn't say since when.** Its lanes read "Can't read" under a subtitle still saying "checking…". Home says it properly: "Can't read Personal-Org-Operating-Model since 16:00". This is part A item 4, first noted at stage 1a and unchanged at main.
3. **Long main-path screens.**
   - With three projects at 360 px: the Timeline is 3,236 px (4.4 phone screens), Plan 2,966 (4.0), Home 1,994 (2.7).
   - With md-extremes' five projects: Waiting is 5,510 (7.4), Plan 5,062 (6.8), Home 3,215 (4.3).
   - Most of Plan's length is item 1.
4. **The copy route stacks every answer file above the choices.** A three-option question shows three monospace answer files, each with its own Copy, before A, B and C. The same files are already in "The whole question · and the file each answer writes". It only appears when an answer passes 6,000 characters or a project takes no prefilled content. That is choice 2, with the planner since stage 8.
5. **Fix: the deploy sheet's wording.**
   - "desk-deploy: waiting for approval run 40" appears under "What it is" and again under "Whether it matches a reviewed pull request".
   - "Why it is here" reads "run 40; its approver: not recorded".
   - The last link says "Its status file" for what is a run's page.
   - Item 19; seen with a test fixture's deploy.
6. **Fix: a project page's waits row lists whole card titles.** For Eta in md-extremes at 360, it runs to 15 lines before the milestones. AC83 asks for "the waiting items' short names".
7. **Cut: "Your merges?" on Personal-Org-Operating-Model's page.** It is a question with no answer. It sits beside "Your last action: not recorded (operating model v2.5.1 records rulings in the ledger)". The 19 Oct audit's item 18 named it too.
8. **Team's key and idle tiles.** A five-glyph key ("working · just finished · idle · waiting on you · not recorded"), when each glyph already has its word, and 10 of Service-Desk's 11 tiles reading "Idle". The screen is as you ruled it, so this is choice 3.
9. **Small points:**

## 5. What to keep

- **Home's briefing:** "6 need you · Waiting ›", with the oldest age and "one question settles itself 15 Oct, 03:20", counted as the board counts. It is V1 at a glance.
- **The board's mechanics:** one board grouped by project, three lanes at once, one verb per card, "Kicked back" when GitHub doesn't confirm, Done for 24 hours with Undo, and a gate's choices as buttons.
- **"Since you last looked"**, four lines then "more", with "Mark as seen". It tells you what changed without a list to keep up with.
- **The type and colour system:**
  - IBM Plex Sans and Mono only;
  - 6 sizes and 3 weights;
  - 18 colours per theme, every one a token;
  - vermilion only on the ◆, Waiting's badge and the kinds of card that wait on you;
  - nothing under 4.5:1 in light or dark.
- **Dark mode**, which follows the phone. Forced dark and the phone's dark draw the same.
- **Honest status lines:** "checked 2 s ago", "Can't read … since 16:00", "Not read yet: your portfolio isn't connected to the desk". "Quiet" is never shown while something is unread.
- **The prefilled answer route:**
  - one box per question;
  - the recommendation labelled and never picked;
  - "Can't be undone" and "settles itself" told apart by icon and word;
  - the whole record one tap away on every sheet that has one, the hold and merge sheets now included.
- **A minute's refresh keeps your screen:** an open sheet, your typed words and the scroll all stay as you left them.
- **Plain-English milestones with "Done when"**, the Now line, and "Rough finish … an expectation, never a deadline".
- **The three states told apart:** "not used", "not recorded yet" with its dashed "?", and "no record for this in the model" with its slashed circle.
- **The running log's lanes:** three greys and three line styles, with curves and dots that follow their lane.
- **Old addresses still work.** An old Home Screen icon or notification, with `?sandbox`, opens the same screen at `/`. A notification tap opens its card's sheet with "Opened from a notification".

## 6. Open choices for you

1. **The same "not yet" reason on every row** (Plan, Timeline, Home, Team).
   - A: say it once per group or phase, as you ruled for standard milestones (P-001-why-line). For example, Plan's group head would read "6 milestones · no estimates yet", and each row would show its rough finish only once there is one.
   - B: keep it on every row, as built.
   - *Recommended: A.* It removes about half of Plan's length and 15 lines from the Timeline, and every gap still says who fills it.
2. **Where the copy route sits** when an answer is too long to prefill.
   - A: a "Copy" inside each choice's box, with the text in the existing "and the file each answer writes" fold, as the v2.5 sheets do.
   - B: the texts below the choices.
   - C: above the choices, as built.
   - *Recommended: A.* The decision stays first, and the text you paste is one tap down.
3. **The Team screen**, which is as you ruled it (P-001-team-screen, A).
   - A: keep it as built.
   - B: drop its key, since each glyph already has its word, and fold idle members into one line, "10 idle ›".
   - *Recommended: B.* What changes is who is working; the idle tiles mostly say "not recorded".
4. **A full reload of the page.** Your rule for a minute's refresh is met. A full reload, which iOS does to Home Screen apps after a while in the background, keeps the open sheet but loses your typed words and the scroll.
   - A: keep typed words and scroll across a reload too.
   - B: as built.
   - *Recommended: A.* A half-written answer shouldn't vanish because the phone closed the app.
5. **The portfolio rows' marks.** Frame H4 puts each row's state icon in a left column; the desk puts it just before the words it marks. This has been open since stage 6a.
   - A: as built.
   - B: a left column, as H4 draws it.
   - *Recommended: A.* Each mark sits beside the one part it describes, and the Scorecard has more than one.
