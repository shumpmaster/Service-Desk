Why things happen, and how they feed into one another

The second half of your last round 4 point:

"the one thing I always struggle understanding is how actions task events feed into one another. So when I’m perusing the plan I will want to see the context behind how or why things are happening the way they are? If that makes sense?"

What an event needs now shows under it (v17), and the plan's actions say which event needs them. These options add the other direction: why each thing that happened, happened. The records don't say why, so every reason below is an example I wrote from the records, and the links follow the same example requirements. They're shown on S-011, the spec that has stopped.
