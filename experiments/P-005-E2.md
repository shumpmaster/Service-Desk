## Question
For each of the key's eleven kinds and each kind of step that waits on the owner, does some record, readable with the desk's read key, carry its kind, state, who and time, and its project, piece of work and milestone? What cannot be rebuilt from the records alone?

## The piece of work (chosen by rule, before any of its records are read)
The most recently finished project item in Service-Desk that has, between them, at least one gate, one decision card, one research item, one pull request merged and one production deploy. If none has all five, the one with the most, and each missing kind is checked on the next most recent item that has it. The helper names the item, and how the rule picked it, before reading further.

## Method
1. Rebuild the item end to end from its records alone, as S-010 J1's event records, with the read key's permissions (Contents, Metadata, Pull requests and Actions read today, plus Commit statuses and Deployments read once the owner adds them; plan line 187).
2. For each event, mark each J1 field `carried` (the record that holds it), `reconstructed` (the records that support it) or `not recorded`.
3. Also record, for each connected repository: its Actions retention setting; whether its oldest workflow run and status are still kept, and whether any made before 2026-10-01 are gone (plan line 182); whether commit times keep their original offset (plan line 185); whether a commit status reads on the private operating-model repository once the permission is added (plan line 187); and whether check results carry anything that runs and statuses miss (plan line 187).

## Output (fixed form)
docs/handover/experiments/P-005-E2-result.md: the item and how it was picked; one table with a row per kind (eleven, plus each waiting kind: card, question, gate, pull request, deploy) and a column per J1 field, each cell `carried: <record>`, `reconstructed: <records>` or `not recorded`; and the per-repository answers of step 3.

## What the result decides (set before the run)
- A kind with every field carried or reconstructed: J1 uses those sources.
- A field `not recorded` for a kind: shown as "not recorded" on the timeline, and put to the owner for a record change or acceptance (M6, plan line 72).
- A waiting step, or one of the eleven kinds, with no record of its time and who: work stops and comes back to the owner (plan line 141).

## Checks on the run
The Reviewer, who did not run it, checks every `carried` and `reconstructed` cell for five events picked at random, and every `not recorded` cell, against the records. A difference fails the result, which is re-run once.

## Limits
Reads only. One item is a sample, not a proof for every item; kinds checked on a second item are marked so. Cost: $0, and none of the owner's time beyond adding the two read permissions, which the plan already asks of the owner.
