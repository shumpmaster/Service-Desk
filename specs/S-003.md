# Spec S-003 — Every decision card shows the Chief of Staff's recommendation, or says it has none

status: draft   frozen_at: —   workstream: P-003 (the card fix)
work-type: fix   design_id: P-003   lineage: [S-001, S-002]
mechanism: One rule in the desk's shared card-making code (src/lib/) works out each decision card's state only from the records on the main branch (queue/, the notes files, the dispatch log) and the current time: held until its notes with a recommended word are there, then shown with that word marked, or shown after a day marked "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet"; every view and the notification sender run that one rule, so they agree.
problem: The owner answers decision cards on the desk, but the desk marks a button Recommended only from the card's own section 5 and never reads the Chief of Staff's notes, so twice the owner answered `re-specify` where the notes recommended `drop`.

*Supersedes specs/S-002.md, by the owner's ruling decisions/P-003/stop-5.md, "re-specify": "Keep the spec to the desk's code: move my weekly value check, my reports and the end-of-year card out to the plan and the Launch gate. ## The defect
Owner's ruling record: decisions/questions/P-001-card-recommendation.md ("Proceed with the fix"; "Well then we need to make sure the cards don't show up until then."; "Show it after a day, flagged (Recommended)"; then "A: own addition now (Recommended)"), quoted in the plan's Part 2 and header. The record itself is not in this definer's pack; this spec takes its words from the plan's quotation of it.

What goes wrong today: a decision card (`dor-fail`, `stop` or `failure`) shows on the desk as soon as it is in queue/, and its Recommended mark comes only from its own section 5. The Chief of Staff's recommendation lives in a separate notes file, `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<n>-notes.md`, which the desk never reads (plan §2, citing research/P-003-sizing-findings.md §2). Seen twice: decisions/Q-019/stop-1.md and decisions/Q-017/stop-2.md were answered `re-specify` where the notes recommended `drop` (plan §2, citing the sizing findings §4).

## Acceptance criteria (scenarios; owner-approved)
Each criterion is a plain scenario traced to V6: *decision cards shown to the owner with neither the Chief of Staff's recommended button marked nor the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, 0, from the day M4 is live to 2026-12-31*. A V6 miss is also any decision card held off the queue more than a day after it was raised (plan §6).

"The records" below means queue/, the notes files and the dispatch log as they stand on the main branch at the read; nothing else (no memory of earlier reads, nothing kept on a device) decides whether a decision card is held or shown, or which word it shows.

### Holding and showing (the rule, D1, and what the owner sees, D2)
- **AC1** [trace: V6 → AC1] — When a decision card is in queue/ and its notes are not on the main branch, and it was raised less than a day ago, then the desk holds it: it is not on the owner's queue, not in Home's count of what needs the owner, not on the board, has no card sheet reachable from those views, and no notification is sent for it.
- **AC2** [trace: V6 → AC2] — When a held decision card's notes reach the main branch with a recommended word, then on the open desk's next read the card shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board; its card sheet says the Chief of Staff recommends that word, and that word's button, and only that one, is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent then.
- **AC3** [trace: V6 → AC3] — When a decision card's card sheet shows, then its own section 5 marks no button, whatever word it names: on a decision card only the notes' recommended word marks a button. (Gate cards keep today's behaviour, AC9.)
- **AC4** [trace: V6 → AC4] — When a decision card was raised a day (24 hours) ago or more by its `card` entry in the dispatch log and still has no notes on the main branch, then it shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board, its card sheet carries the mark "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet", and no button is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent then. If the desk is open as the day passes, the card shows on the desk's next read, without a reload.
- **AC5** [trace: V6 → AC5] — When a card shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark later gets notes with a recommended word on the main branch, then on the desk's next read the mark is gone and the card shows the notes' recommended word with that word's button marked Recommended, as in AC2. No second notification is sent for it.
- **AC6** [trace: V6 → AC6] — When a decision card's notes are on the main branch but have no recommended word (no `Recommendation:` line; no backticked word on that line; or no backticked word on it that is one of the card's words), then the desk treats the card as having no notes: held under AC1 within its first day, shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark under AC4 after it, and never with a button marked.
- **AC7** [trace: V6 → AC7] — When a decision card's raise time is not recorded (no `card` entry for it in the part of the dispatch log the desk reads, or the log not read in that read), then the card shows at once, as in AC4, with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, or as in AC2 if its notes with a recommended word are already on the main branch.
- **AC8** [trace: V6 → AC8] — When notes for a decision card exist only on a branch other than the main branch (for example in an open pull request), then the desk treats the card as having no notes.

### Unchanged
- **AC9** [trace: V6 → AC9] — When a card is a gate card (`dor`, `criteria` or `launch`), an owner question, or a pull-request card, then it shows, counts and notifies exactly as before this fix, with its Recommended mark from its gate case as today, and the desk does not look for notes for it.
- **AC10** [trace: V6 → AC10] — When the desk reads notes, then it reads them through the repository reads it already makes, writes nothing to any repository, and sends nothing to any new outside service; the desk's spend stays $0 (plan §1 Out, §5).

### The views and the sender agree, from the records alone
- **AC11** [trace: V6 → AC11] — When the queue, Home's count, the board, the card sheet (including the hand-written one in src/public/sandbox.js) and the notification sender are given the same records and the same moment, then each decision card's state, held, shown with a recommended word (and which word), or shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, is the same in all of them, because each runs the one rule on those records and that moment and on nothing else. The same records and moment always give the same state, whatever was read before: a desk open for hours, a desk just opened, and the sender all agree. Each decision card's notification is sent at most once, at the first sender run at which the rule shows it.

### After graduation: running, monitoring, feedback and value review
*The desk has graduated (docs/PROJECT.md §3, Launch-5). By the owner's ruling (decisions/P-003/stop-5.md) this spec keeps to the desk's code: the Chief of Staff's weekly value check for V6, its reports of misses, the end-of-year value review card, the feedback route, and the operating notes on M4 are moved to the plan and the Launch gate, outside this spec. What stays here is what the desk's code must do so that those can be run.*
- **AC12** [trace: V6 → AC12] — Running: when M4 is live, then it needs no new setting, credential, schedule or step from the owner or any agent; the desk reads the notes on the reads it already makes, on its existing schedule.
- **AC13** [trace: V6 → AC13] — Monitoring: when anyone later runs the one rule on the records as they stood at a past moment (the main branch at a commit, and that moment's time), then it gives the state each decision card had on the desk at that moment, so V6's two counts (cards shown with neither mark; cards held more than a day after being raised) can be worked out from the records alone, with no log kept by the desk or the browser. A test runs the rule on sample records at chosen moments and checks the states.

## Join sheets
- **J1 —** queue/ and the dispatch log on the main branch → the rule (D1): for each card file `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md` (for example `queue/Q-003-failure-1.md`), its kind from `<gate>` and its raise time from its `card` entry in the dispatch log the desk already reads (plan §7). Decision card if `<gate>` is `dor-fail`, `stop` or `failure`; gate card if `dor`, `criteria` or `launch`. Awkward cases: a `dor-fail` card must not be read as a `dor` gate card by matching the prefix `dor` (it is a decision card); no `card` entry, or the log not read (AC7: shown at once); two `card` entries for one card (the earliest counts); a raise time later than the current time by clock skew (treated as raised at the current time of that read, so held while it stays within a day of the recorded time; the same records and moment still give the same answer everywhere, AC11). The exact field names of the log's `card` entry are those of the desk's existing log reader in src/lib/ (research/P-003-sizing-findings.md §1); the Builder states them in the J1 test. A test marked `join-test: S-003/J1` covers it.
- **J2 —** the main branch's file tree and a notes file → the rule (D1): whether `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<n>-notes.md` exists on the main branch for card `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md`, and if so its recommended word: the first backticked word on the file's first line containing `Recommendation:` that is one of the card's words (the words its buttons offer), matched exactly, with any bold (`**`), italic (`*`, `_`) or heading (`#`) marks around the line ignored. Examples, for a card whose words are `drop`, `re-specify`, `resubmit`:
  - `Recommendation: \`drop\`` → `drop`
  - `**Recommendation:** \`drop\` — the scope is gone` → `drop`
  - `## Recommendation: \`re-specify\`` → `re-specify`
  - `Recommendation: not \`proceed\`; \`drop\`` → `drop` (`proceed` is not one of the card's words)
  - `Recommendation: \`Drop\`` → none (not an exact match), so no notes (AC6)
  - `Recommendation: drop` (no backticks) → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - no `Recommendation:` line → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - two `Recommendation:` lines → the first line decides; if it has no recommended word, none (the second is not read)
  - notes for `stop-2` beside a card `stop-1` → not this card's notes
  A test marked `join-test: S-003/J2` covers it.
- **J3 —** the rule (D1) → the one card list every view reads: each decision card carries one state, `held`, `shown, recommended <word>`, or `shown, no recommendation yet`, worked out from the records and the moment of the read alone (AC11); nothing about earlier reads is carried. Gate cards, questions and pull-request cards carry no new state. Awkward cases: a card answered while held (it leaves the list as any answered card does); notes arriving in the same read as the day passing (the notes win, AC2); notes removed from the main branch after a card showed with their word (the state is worked out afresh: `shown, no recommendation yet` if it was raised a day ago or more or its raise time is not recorded, `held` if it was raised less than a day ago); a card shown at once because the log was not read, and then found in the next read's log raised less than a day ago with no notes (it is `held` again; see open question 3). A test marked `join-test: S-003/J3` covers it.
- **J4 —** the card list → the views (queue, Home's count, board, card sheet in src/public/sandbox.js and its generated copies): `held` cards appear in none; `shown, recommended <word>` cards show the word in the card sheet and mark that button Recommended; `shown, no recommendation yet` cards show the mark and mark no button. Awkward cases: a card sheet opened from a direct link to a held card (it says the card is waiting for the Chief of Staff's notes, with no answer buttons); the card sheet left open while the card moves from `shown, no recommendation yet` to `shown, recommended <word>` (the mark gives way on the next read). A test marked `join-test: S-003/J4` covers it.
- **J5 —** the card list → the notification sender: the sender runs the same rule on the records (src/notify/collect.mjs already runs the desk's card-making code outside the browser), and a decision card is notified once, at the first sender run where it is not `held`; a change from `shown, no recommendation yet` to `shown, recommended <word>` sends nothing, and a card that goes back to `held` and shows again is not notified a second time. What the sender keeps to avoid repeats is about what it has sent, never an input to whether a card is held or shown. Awkward cases: the sender's read of the log failing (AC7 applies: it shows and is notified once); a card that shows and is answered between two sender runs (no notification is owed). A change to the sender's own files needs the owner's approval (plan §4). A test marked `join-test: S-003/J5` covers it.

## Risks and mitigations
- A card held for good — AC4 (a day) and AC7 (unrecorded raise time shows at once).
- A button marked that the Chief of Staff did not recommend — exact matching in J2 and AC6 (anything unclear counts as no notes); AC3 stops section 5 marking a decision card.
- The owner told twice — J5, one notification per card, even if the card goes back to held and shows again.
- A gate card changed by mistake — AC9 and J1's `dor-fail` / `dor` case.
- The views or the sender disagreeing — AC11: one rule, on the records and the moment alone; nothing kept from earlier reads.
- A card appearing and then vanishing — S-002's "once shown, not withdrawn" rule is removed by the owner's ruling, since it rested on what one desk had seen. It can now happen only if notes are removed from the main branch within a card's first day, or if a read without the log showed a card at once (J3). Accepted as following from the records; open question 3 asks the owner about the second case.
- The owner leaning on the mark without reading the reasons (automation bias "cannot be prevented by training or instructions", library/facts/F-automation-complacency-01.md, B) — the card sheet still shows the card in full; the mark only names the Chief of Staff's word. Accepted, as the owner asked for the mark.

## Open questions for the owner
1. AC5: when a card shown as "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" later gets its notes, this spec sends no second notification (so the owner is not told twice). Should it instead send one saying the recommendation has arrived?
2. J2: a recommended word must match the card's word exactly (`Drop` does not mark `drop`); a near miss counts as no notes. Is that strictness what you want?
3. AC7 and J3: the plan shows a card at once when the dispatch log was not read. Under your ruling that only the records decide, such a card can go back to held at the next read if the log then shows it was raised less than a day ago with no notes, and the sender, if its log read failed, may notify a card the desk is holding. Keep the plan's rule as written (never hidden, at the cost of a card sometimes vanishing), or should a failed log read hold nothing back and change nothing until the log is read? The second would need a change to the plan's section 1, which only you can rule.
