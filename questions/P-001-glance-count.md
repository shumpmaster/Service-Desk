# Ruling needed: Should Home's "N need you" be the biggest text on Home, with a due time on its own line?

Milestone: none (a design follow-up after M3D; the planner gives it a milestone when it is built)
Asked in session: session_017seyaGqUxAff6E8dKWHDCR

WHY: The count of what needs you is 15 px, smaller than the word "Home" above it, and "one question settles itself Thu 03:20" is the last clause of a small grey sentence. It bends frame H1's single 15 px briefing line (P-001-redesign-direction-v2). Left unruled, it stays as built.
OPTIONS:
  A. Bigger count, due line — The count takes the sheet-title size (18 px, bold); a question settling itself or a choice that can't be undone gets its own line with a clock or lock icon, as on the Waiting cards. About 8 px taller.
  B. As built — The count stays 15 px and the due time stays in the sentence.
RECOMMENDATION: A — the count is the reason the desk exists, and a due time is the only deadline in the briefing.
