# Ruling needed: Three tabs, or one scrolling screen?

Milestone: MD2
Asked in session: session_012RJPVKAoLga2RkqLYRDcBY

WHY: The mockup uses a bottom bar of three tabs: Waiting, Plan and Projects. A single scrolling screen, with the board on top and plan and projects below, is the other common pattern. Left unruled, the mockup's three tabs stand.
OPTIONS:
  A. Three tabs — Waiting, Plan and Projects at the bottom, one tap each; Waiting opens first.
  B. One scrolling screen — The board at the top, then your plan, then your projects, all on one page.
RECOMMENDATION: A — the board gets the whole screen, and the plan and projects are one tap away, never pushed down by a long board.
RISK CLASS: R0   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: how the page's parts are reached
DEFAULT: A   TIMEOUT: 48
