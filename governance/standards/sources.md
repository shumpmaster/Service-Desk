# Source standard — what counts as an independent source

*Owner ruling A, 2026-10-01 (decisions/questions/Q-005-sources.md). The Source checker reads this
file; it states a checking rule, not any answer a question hopes for (D-055). It says only what the
owner ruled; anything wider needs its own ruling.*

- For facts about how the product behaves, two different **Anthropic** pages count as two sources,
  labelled "same publisher".
- Claims about cost, risk or quality still need an independent source.
