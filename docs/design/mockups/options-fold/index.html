Icons that open and fold

You said the icons on the timeline should expand or collapse sections. First v12 as it is: a phase folds from the chevron at the right of its heading, a milestone opens when you tap its row, a step folds from its own caret, and a sprint cannot fold. The icons do nothing.

Then three ways the icons could do it. Each is drawn with M6's real events in the branch-map layout you chose, and you can tap the icons. Phase, milestone, sprint and step each get an icon; an event's icon stays its kind.

Now

v12 as it is

Option

A · A count on the icon

Every level has an icon, and the icon opens or folds what is under it. A folded icon carries a small count of what is inside. Tapping the words opens the detail, as today.
