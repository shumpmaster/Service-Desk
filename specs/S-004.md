## The defect
Owner's ruling record: decisions/questions/P-001-card-recommendation.md ("Proceed with the fix"; "Well then we need to make sure the cards don't show up until then."; "Show it after a day, flagged (Recommended)"; then "A: own addition now (Recommended)"), quoted in the plan's Part 2 and header. The record is not in this definer's pack, so this spec takes its words from the plan's quotation; S-003 records that its plan review read the record at source and found the quotations match (specs/S-003.md, "The defect", citing reviews/S-003/plan.md, which is not in this pack either).

What goes wrong today: a decision card (`dor-fail`, `stop` or `failure`) shows on the desk as soon as it is in queue/, and its Recommended mark comes only from its own section 5. The Chief of Staff's recommendation lives in a separate notes file, `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<n>-notes.md`, which the desk never reads (plan §2, citing research/P-003-sizing-findings.md §2). Seen twice: decisions/Q-019/stop-1.md and decisions/Q-017/stop-2.md were answered `re-specify` where the notes recommended `drop` (plan §2, citing the sizing findings §4).

## Acceptance criteria (scenarios; for the owner's approval on the scenario card, not yet approved)
Each criterion is a plain scenario traced to V6: *decision cards shown to the owner with neither the Chief of Staff's recommended button marked nor the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, 0, from the day M4 is live to 2026-12-31*. A V6 miss is also any decision card held off the queue more than a day after it was raised; a card not shown because a read could not get the records is not a miss (plan §6).

"The records" below means queue/, the notes files and the dispatch log as they stand on the main branch; nothing else (no memory of earlier reads, nothing kept on a device) decides a decision card's state or which word it shows. A **read** is the desk's or the sender's reading of those records. A card's **age** at a moment is that moment minus its raise time as recorded in its `card` entry, never reset or adjusted; "raised less than a day ago" means an age under 24 hours, including a negative age (a recorded raise time later than the moment, J1), and "a day ago or more" means an age of 24 hours or more.

A **baseline run** is a run of the desk's code, or of the notification sender, as it stands at the build's **base commit** (the main-branch commit the build starts from, before any change for this fix), on inputs a test sets; its outputs are recorded in a file under tests/desk/, committed on the build branch before any change under src/, with the base commit named in the file. "As today" and "as before this fix" below (AC9, AC14, AC15, AC16, AC17) mean: equal, for the same inputs, to that recorded output, and the test for the criterion compares against it. What is known of today's behaviour second-hand is said where it is used, but the recorded output, not that account, is what the check holds the build to.

### Holding and showing (the rule, D1, and what the owner sees, D2)
- **AC1** [trace: V6 → AC1] — When a decision card is in queue/ and its notes are not on the main branch, and it was raised less than a day ago, then the desk holds it: it is not on the owner's queue, not in Home's count of what needs the owner, not on the board, no view offers its answer buttons (a direct link or a notice to it opens what the desk opens today for a card not on its list, AC15), and no notification is sent for it.
- **AC2** [trace: V6 → AC2] — When a held decision card's notes reach the main branch with a recommended word, then on the open desk's next read the card shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board; its card sheet says the Chief of Staff recommends that word, and that word's button, and only that one, is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent at the sender's next run (AC14).
- **AC3** [trace: V6 → AC3] — When a decision card's card sheet shows, then its own section 5 marks no button, whatever word it names: on a decision card only the notes' recommended word marks a button. (Gate cards keep today's behaviour, AC9.)
- **AC4** [trace: V6 → AC4] — When a decision card was raised a day (24 hours) ago or more by its `card` entry in the dispatch log and still has no notes on the main branch, then it shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board, its card sheet carries the mark "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet", and no button is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent at the sender's next run (AC14). If the desk is open as the day passes, the card shows on the desk's next read, without a reload.
- **AC5** [trace: V6 → AC5] — When a card shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark later gets notes with a recommended word on the main branch, then on the desk's next read the mark is gone and the card shows the notes' recommended word with that word's button marked Recommended, as in AC2. Notification: as AC14 says.
- **AC6** [trace: V6 → AC6] — When a decision card's notes are on the main branch but have no recommended word (no `Recommendation:` line as J2 defines it; no backticked word on that line; or no backticked word on it that is one of the card's words), then the desk treats the card as having no notes: held under AC1 within its first day, shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark under AC4 after it, and never with a button marked.
- **AC7** [trace: V6 → AC7] — When a read gets the dispatch log on the main branch, as the desk's existing log reader reads it, and the log has no `card` entry for a decision card (for example a card older than the part of the log that reader reads), then the card's raise time is not recorded and the card shows at once: as in AC4, with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, or as in AC2 if its notes with a recommended word are on the main branch. (A read that did not get the log at all is not this case: AC17.)
- **AC8** [trace: V6 → AC8] — When notes for a decision card exist only on a branch other than the main branch (for example in an open pull request), then the desk treats the card as having no notes.

### When a read cannot get the records (plan §1, fifth and sixth bullets)
- **AC16** [trace: V6 → AC16] — When a read of the desk or of the sender cannot get queue/, or cannot get a notes file the rule needs (one that is on the main branch for a decision card in queue/), then from that read no decision card is shown on the queue, counted in Home, put on the board or offered with answer buttons, and the sender sends no notice for any decision card on that run. Instead the queue and Home show the line "Decision cards not shown: queue/ could not be read" or "Decision cards not shown: the Chief of Staff's notes could not be read" (both lines if both failed). When a later read gets the records, each card is held or shown by AC1 to AC8 and AC17, with its real age. Gate cards, questions and pull-request cards behave as they do today when a read fails (AC9): what the desk and the sender give for them is equal to a baseline run with the same records and the same read failing.
- **AC17** [trace: V6 → AC17] — When a read gets queue/ and the notes but cannot get the dispatch log, then each decision card whose notes with a recommended word are on the main branch is shown, counted in Home, put on the board, offered with answer buttons and notified exactly as in AC2, as when the log is read; every other decision card in queue/ is not shown, counted, put on the board or offered with answer buttons from that read, and the sender sends no notice for it on that run. While at least one decision card is not shown for this reason, the queue and Home show the line "Decision cards without Chief of Staff notes not shown: the dispatch log could not be read". When a later read gets the log, each card is held or shown by AC1 to AC8 with its real age. Gate cards, questions and pull-request cards behave as today (AC9): what the desk and the sender give for them is equal to a baseline run with the same records and the log read failing.

### Unchanged
- **AC9** [trace: V6 → AC9] — When a card is a gate card (`dor`, `criteria` or `launch`), an owner question, or a pull-request card, then it shows, counts and notifies exactly as before this fix, with its Recommended mark from its gate case as today, and the desk does not look for notes for it. A test runs the desk's card-making code and the sender on sample records holding each of these kinds, with every read getting the records and with each of the three reads failing in turn (queue/, a notes file, the dispatch log), and checks that what they give for these cards (on the list or not, Home's count, the marked button, any line shown, any notice) is equal to a baseline run on the same records and failures.
- **AC10** [trace: V6 → AC10] — When the desk reads notes, then it reads them through the repository reads it already makes, writes nothing to any repository, and sends nothing to any new outside service; the desk's spend stays $0 (plan §1 Out, §5).

### The views and the sender agree, from the records alone
- **AC11** [trace: V6 → AC11] — When the queue, Home's count, the board, the card sheet (including the hand-written one in src/public/sandbox.js) and the notification sender are given the same records and the same moment, then each decision card's state, held, shown with a recommended word (and which word), or shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, is the same in all of them, because each takes the card list the one rule makes from those records and that moment and from nothing else. The same records and moment always give the same state, whatever was read before: a desk open for hours, a desk just opened, and the sender all agree. A read that did not get some of the records gives no state to the decision cards it cannot decide (AC16, AC17) and, to the ones it can (AC17), the state a full read gives; never a different one. (Whether a notice is sent is not a card's state; AC14 and AC15 cover it.) The moment is always given to the rule, never read from a clock inside it, since the desk and the sender never run at the same instant: a test hands the views' card-making path and the sender's the same sample records and one injected moment, at several moments (before, at and after a card's raise time plus 24 hours), and checks that every decision card's state is the same in all of them.

- **AC12** [trace: V6 → AC12] — Running: when M4 is live, then it needs no new setting, credential, schedule or step from the owner or any agent; the desk reads the notes on the reads it already makes, on its existing schedule.
- **AC13** [trace: V6 → AC13] — Monitoring (the plan's "monitoring criterion", §6): when anyone runs the one rule on the records as they stood on the main branch at a past commit and on a moment they choose, then it gives, for each decision card in queue/ at that commit, its id, its raise time as recorded (or "not recorded") and the state it had on the desk at that moment, reading nothing else (no clock of its own, no network, nothing stored), so V6's two counts can be worked out from the records alone: cards shown with neither mark, and cards still `held` at their raise time plus 24 hours (J6). It runs on records from before M4 was live as well, so the Chief of Staff can run the count once on past records before the Launch gate (plan §6). A test runs the rule on sample records at chosen moments, including a card with a raise time far in the future and a card with no recorded raise time, and checks the ids, raise times and states. What this count can and cannot see, said openly: it measures V6's second half (a card held more than a day after it was raised) from the records. V6's first half (a card shown with neither mark) it cannot find on the live desk: every shown state the rule gives carries one of the two marks, so a view that drops the mark, or shows a card the rule did not list, leaves no sign in the records. That half is guarded by the tests of J4 and AC11, before the fix goes live, not measured after; this is carried to the Launch gate as a risk (Risks; open question 4).
- **AC18** [trace: V6 → AC18] — Operating notes: when M4 is handed over, then the handover package's operating notes (the Builder's, plan §4) say, in plain words, what a held decision card is and when it shows; what the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark means and that it gives way when notes arrive; what each of the three "not shown" lines (AC16, AC17) means and that the cards return by themselves once the records are read; how to run the rule on the records at a commit and a moment to get V6's two counts (AC13, J6), and that the count can find a held-too-long card but not a view showing a card with neither mark (AC13); and how to roll the fix back (redeploy the previous production deployment of the desk, then revert the merge).
- **AC19** [trace: V6 → AC19] — Feedback and value review: when the Chief of Staff files the owner's feedback on the fix as a question, or files the review card putting V6's result to the owner after 2026-12-31, then the desk shows it by the rules for its kind and needs no change for it: a question as today (AC9); a card that is a decision card waits for its notes, or for a day, like any decision card (AC1 to AC8); a gate card as today (AC9).

### Notifications
- **AC14** [trace: V6 → AC14] — When the notification sender runs, then it takes the card list the one rule makes and sends for decision cards as it sends today for any card on that list, with no change to the sender's own files (src/notify/**): no notice for a held card (AC1) or for a card not shown because a read could not get the records (AC16, AC17), and a notice at the first sender run at which the card is shown (AC2, AC4, AC7, AC17). Two cases keep the sender's behaviour of today unchanged: a shown card whose state changes from `shown, no recommendation yet` to `shown, recommended <word>` (AC5) is treated as today's sender treats a card that stays on its list with changed content, and a card that leaves the list (back to held, J3, or not shown on a failed read, AC16, AC17) and later shows again is treated as today's sender treats a card that leaves its list and comes back. A test runs the sender over a chosen sequence of records and moments, including both cases, and checks: no notice while held or not shown; one at first showing; and for the two cases, notices equal to a baseline run of the sender (src/notify/** at the base commit) handed the same sequence of card lists at the point where it takes the list from the card-making code (J5). How today's sender avoids repeating a notice is not known to this definer; the criterion holds whatever it is, since it is checked against the recorded run.
- **AC15** [trace: V6 → AC15] — When a notice has been sent for a decision card and the card then leaves the list (back to held, J3, or not shown on a failed read, AC16, AC17), then this fix withdraws nothing and the sender sends nothing for the card while it is off the list; opening that notice, or a direct link to the card, while it is off the list opens what the desk opens today for a card not on its list, with no answer buttons, and adds no new card-sheet state; once the card shows again, the notice or link opens it as shown, as it does for any card on the list. "What the desk opens today" is checked against a baseline run: a test opens a notice and a direct link for a card id not on the list at the base commit, records what the desk shows, and checks that a held or not-shown decision card's notice and link show the same after the fix. ## Join sheets
- **J1 —** queue/ and the dispatch log on the main branch → the rule (D1): for each card file `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md` (for example `queue/Q-003-failure-1.md`), its kind from `<gate>` and its raise time from its `card` entry in the dispatch log the desk already reads (plan §7). Decision card if `<gate>` is `dor-fail`, `stop` or `failure`; gate card if `dor`, `criteria` or `launch`. Awkward cases: a `dor-fail` card must not be read as a `dor` gate card by matching the prefix `dor` (it is a decision card); the log got but with no `card` entry for the card (AC7: shown at once); the log not got by the read (AC17: shown only if its notes with a recommended word are on the main branch); queue/ not got (AC16: no decision card gets a state); two or more `card` entries for one card (the pick is the rule's own, made in src/lib/ and not left to the reader: the rule takes the earliest raise time among the entries the existing log reader returns for the card, whatever order it returns them in; if the reader returns only one of them, that one is the raise time, and the same pick is what AC13 and J6 report, so the desk, the sender and the count agree; the J1 test includes a log with two entries for one card and states which of them the reader returns); a recorded raise time later than the moment of the read, from clock skew (age is always measured from the recorded time and never reset, so a negative age counts as less than a day: the card is held until the recorded time plus 24 hours, then shown; for example, recorded 2026-11-10 10:00 and read at 09:58 that day → held; read at 2026-11-11 09:59 → held; read at 2026-11-11 10:00 → shown; the same records and moment give the same answer everywhere, AC11); a recorded raise time far in the future, from a malformed entry, follows the same rule, so it is held until that time plus 24 hours (see Risks). The exact field names of the log's `card` entry are those of the desk's existing log reader in src/lib/ (research/P-003-sizing-findings.md §1); the Builder states them in the J1 test. A test marked `join-test: S-004/J1` covers it.
- **J2 —** the main branch's file tree and a notes file → the rule (D1): whether `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<n>-notes.md` exists on the main branch for card `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md`, and if so its recommended word. The notes' `Recommendation:` line is the first line of the file that, once any leading heading marks (`#`, then spaces) and any bold or italic marks (`**`, `*`, `_`) at its start are removed, begins with `Recommendation:`; a line that only mentions `Recommendation:` later in its text is not it. The recommended word is the first backticked word on that line that is one of the card's words (the words its buttons offer), matched exactly. Examples, for a card whose words are `drop`, `re-specify`, `resubmit`:
  - `Recommendation: \`drop\`` → `drop`
  - `**Recommendation:** \`drop\` — the scope is gone` → `drop`
  - `_Recommendation:_ \`resubmit\`` → `resubmit`
  - `## Recommendation: \`re-specify\`` → `re-specify`
  - `Recommendation: not \`proceed\`; \`drop\`` → `drop` (`proceed` is not one of the card's words)
  - `Recommendation: \`Drop\`` → none (not an exact match), so no notes (AC6)
  - `Recommendation: drop` (no backticks) → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - an earlier prose line `The Recommendation: line below says \`resubmit\`` followed by `Recommendation: \`drop\`` → `drop` (the prose line does not begin with `Recommendation:`)
  - no line beginning with `Recommendation:` → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - two lines beginning with `Recommendation:` → the first decides; if it has no recommended word, none (the second is not read)
  - notes for `stop-2` beside a card `stop-1` → not this card's notes
  - a notes file listed in the tree whose content the read could not get → not "no notes": the read has not got the notes (AC16)
  A test marked `join-test: S-004/J2` covers it.
- **J3 —** the rule (D1) → the one card list every view reads: each decision card the read can decide carries one state, `held`, `shown, recommended <word>`, or `shown, no recommendation yet`, worked out from the records and the moment alone (AC11); nothing about earlier reads is carried. A read that did not get queue/ or the notes gives the list no decision cards and the flag `decision cards not read: <queue/ | notes>` (either or both, AC16). A read that got queue/ and the notes but not the dispatch log gives the list only the decision cards whose notes with a recommended word are on the main branch, each `shown, recommended <word>`, and, if any decision card was left out, the flag `decision cards without notes not read: dispatch log` (AC17). Gate cards, questions and pull-request cards carry no new state. Awkward cases: a card answered while held (it leaves the list as any answered card does); notes arriving in the same read as the day passing (the notes win, AC2); notes removed from the main branch after a card showed with their word (the state is worked out afresh: `shown, no recommendation yet` if it was raised a day ago or more or its raise time is not recorded, `held` if it was raised less than a day ago); a card file on the main branch before its `card` entry is (shown at once, AC7; if the entry then arrives with a raise time less than a day before the read, it is `held` until that time plus 24 hours, as the records say). Those last two are the only ways a shown card goes back to held; a failed read (AC16, AC17) takes a card off the list without giving it a state. A test marked `join-test: S-004/J3` covers it.
- **J4 —** the card list → the views (queue, Home's count, board, card sheet in src/public/sandbox.js and its generated copies): `held` cards appear in none, and no view offers their answer buttons; `shown, recommended <word>` cards show the word in the card sheet and mark that button Recommended; `shown, no recommendation yet` cards show the mark and mark no button; the `decision cards not read` flag shows its line or lines on the queue and on Home (AC16), and the `decision cards without notes not read` flag shows its line there (AC17), each with the exact words those criteria give. Awkward cases: a card sheet opened from a direct link to a held card, or from a notice sent before the card left the list (it opens what the desk opens today for a card not on its list, AC15); the card sheet left open while the card moves from `shown, no recommendation yet` to `shown, recommended <word>` (the mark gives way on the next read); a card sheet left open when a read fails (it shows what the desk shows today for a card not on its list, AC15). A test marked `join-test: S-004/J4` covers it.
- **J5 —** the card list → the notification sender: the sender takes the same card list (src/notify/collect.mjs already runs the desk's card-making code outside the browser, docs/PROJECT.md:588), so a held decision card is not on the list it notifies from, and a shown one is. Handed over: the card list at that run (each card's id, kind and state, and the flags of AC16 and AC17 if the read did not get the records). Nothing in the sender's own files changes; its existing way of not repeating a notice applies to decision cards as to any card (AC14). Awkward cases: the sender's read not getting queue/ or the notes (AC16: no notice for any decision card on that run; gate cards as today); the sender's read not getting the log (AC17: notices as usual for cards whose notes with a recommended word are on the main branch, none for the rest); a card that shows and is answered between two sender runs (no notification is owed); a notice already sent for a card that leaves the list (nothing withdrawn, nothing sent while it is off the list, AC15). If meeting AC1, AC2, AC14, AC16 or AC17 turns out to need a change to src/notify/**, the build stops and comes back to the owner (plan §4); the spec is then revised, not built around. A test marked `join-test: S-004/J5` covers it.
- **J6 —** the rule (D1) → the Chief of Staff's V6 count (AC13): handed over, for one commit of the main branch and one moment, a list with one entry per decision card in queue/ at that commit: its id (`<item>-<gate>-<n>`), its raise time as recorded in the dispatch log (ISO 8601, or `not recorded`), and its state (`held`, `shown, recommended <word>` or `shown, no recommendation yet`); if the records at that commit cannot be got, the AC16 or AC17 flag instead, as on the desk. How the count uses it, set down in the operating notes (AC18): a card is a held-too-long miss if, run at the commit current at its raise time plus 24 hours and at that moment, its state is `held`; a card is a no-mark miss if it is shown with neither mark; every shown state the rule gives carries one of the two marks, so a count from the rule finds a no-mark miss only as a shown card whose state carries neither, which is 0 unless the rule is broken. A view that shows a card off the list, or drops its mark, is not seen by this count; J4's and AC11's tests guard against that before launch, and V6's first half is not measured after it (AC13, Risks, open question 4). Awkward cases: a card answered before its raise time plus 24 hours (not a miss, whatever its state was); a raise time far in the future (held-too-long miss once its raise time plus 24 hours is past, as Risks says; until then the count cannot place it); a card with no recorded raise time (shown at once, never a held-too-long miss); a commit at which the log cannot be read (the AC17 flag: a failed read, not a miss, plan §6). A test marked `join-test: S-004/J6` covers it.

## Risks and mitigations
- A card held for good — AC4 (a day) and AC7 (no recorded raise time: shown at once); J1 measures age from the recorded raise time and never resets it, so clock skew delays a card only by the skew. A malformed raise time far in the future would hold a card until that time plus a day; it counts as a V6 miss, which AC13 and J6 let the Chief of Staff's count find from the records. Accepted, since the plan defines "raised" by the log entry; open question 2.
- A card hidden while a record cannot be read — AC16 and AC17 show a line on the queue and Home naming what could not be read, so it is never silent; cards with a recommendation still show while only the log is missing (AC17); the cards return on the first read that gets the records. Not a V6 miss (plan §6), so a long outage would not show in V6; the line is its only sign, and AC18 tells the operating team what it means.
- One notes file that cannot be fetched hides every decision card (AC16, J2's last case), as the plan's "cannot get ... the notes" reads; this is wider than it need be, but it keeps one rule and is said on the line. Open question 3.
- A button marked that the Chief of Staff did not recommend — exact matching in J2 and AC6 (anything unclear counts as no notes); J2's line rule stops a prose mention deciding the word; AC3 stops section 5 marking a decision card.
- The owner told twice — no notice while held or not shown (AC1, AC16, AC17); for a word arriving later, or a card that leaves the list and shows again, the sender behaves as today (AC14); open question 1.
- A notice for a card that then leaves the list, opening to "gone" — rare (J3: only notes removed within a card's first day, a `card` entry reaching the main branch after its card, or a failed read); the card shows again by the rules; accepted, AC15.
- A gate card changed by mistake — AC9 and J1's `dor-fail` / `dor` case.
- V6's first half not measured on the live desk — the count from the rule (AC13, J6) cannot see a view that drops the mark or shows a card the rule did not list, since that leaves no record; it measures only the held-too-long half. Mitigation: J4's and AC11's tests check every view against the rule before launch, and the operating notes (AC18) say the count covers only that half. Carried to the Launch gate as a risk; open question 4.
- A baseline recorded from the wrong code — the baseline runs (AC9, AC14 to AC17) are only a fair check if they come from the base commit; the file names the base commit and is committed before any change under src/, so the Reviewer can see both on the branch.
- The views or the sender disagreeing — AC11: one rule, on the records and the moment alone; a partial read gives no state, or the same state, never a different one (AC16, AC17).
- The build passing the stop rule — the restated size is near it on tests; the Builder reports lines and tests against the stop rule, and the stop rule applies unchanged.
- The owner leaning on the mark without reading the reasons (automation bias "cannot be prevented by training or instructions", library/facts/F-automation-complacency-01.md, B) — the card sheet still shows the card in full; the mark only names the Chief of Staff's word. Accepted, as the owner asked for the mark.
