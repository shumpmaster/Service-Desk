### Holding and showing (the rule, D1, and what the owner sees, D2)
- **AC1** [trace: V6 → AC1] — When a decision card is in queue/, its notes are not on the main branch and it was raised less than a day ago, then the desk holds it. It is not on the owner's queue, not in Home's count of what needs the owner and not on the board. No view offers its answer buttons: a direct link or a notice to it opens what the desk opens today for a card not on its list (AC15). No notification is sent for it.
- **AC2** [trace: V6 → AC2] — When a held decision card's notes reach the main branch with a recommended word, then on the open desk's next read the card shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board. Its card sheet says the Chief of Staff recommends that word, and that word's button, and only that one, is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent at the sender's next run (AC14).
- **AC3** [trace: V6 → AC3] — When a decision card's card sheet shows, then its own section 5 marks no button, whatever word it names. On a decision card only the notes' recommended word marks a button. (Gate cards keep today's behaviour, AC9.)
- **AC4** [trace: V6 → AC4] — When a decision card was raised a day (24 hours) ago or more by its `card` entry and still has no notes on the main branch, then it shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board. Its card sheet carries the mark "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet", and no button is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent at the sender's next run (AC14). If the desk is open and visible as the day passes, the card shows on the desk's next scheduled read, without a reload: today's desk reads again every 60 seconds while it is visible (reviews/S-009/src-reading.md:104-106), and AC10 keeps that schedule. If the desk is hidden then, the card shows on its next read once it is visible again.
- **AC5** [trace: V6 → AC5] — When a card shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark later gets notes with a recommended word on the main branch, then on the desk's next read the mark is gone and the card shows the notes' word with that word's button marked Recommended, as in AC2. For its notification, AC14 applies.
- **AC6** [trace: V6 → AC6] — When a decision card's notes are on the main branch but have no recommended word (no `Recommendation:` line as J2 defines it, no backticked word on that line, or no backticked word on it that is one of the card's words), then the desk treats the card as having no notes. It is held under AC1 within its first day, shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark under AC4 after that, and never shown with a button marked.
- **AC7** [trace: V6 → AC7] — When a read gets the dispatch log and a decision card's raise time is older than the part of the log the desk's existing log reader reads, then the card shows at once. It shows as in AC4, with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, or as in AC2 if its notes with a recommended word are on the main branch. A new card's `card` entry reaches the main branch with its card file (decisions/P-003/stop-7.md), so a card in queue/ whose entry is not in the part of the log that was read is a card raised before that part. The rule takes a missing entry, in a log it got, as this case and no other. (A read that did not get the log at all is not this case: AC17.)
- **AC8** [trace: V6 → AC8] — When notes for a decision card exist only on a branch other than the main branch (for example in an open pull request), then the desk treats the card as having no notes.

### The views and the sender agree, from the records alone
- **AC11** [trace: V6 → AC11] — When the queue, Home's count, the board, the card sheet (including the hand-written one in src/public/sandbox.js) and the notification sender are given the same records and the same moment, then each decision card has the same state in all of them: held, shown with a recommended word (and the same word), or shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark. Each takes the card list the one rule makes from those records and that moment, and from nothing else. The same records and moment always give the same state, whatever was read before, so a desk open for hours, a desk just opened and the sender all agree. The weekly V6 count, its reports of misses, the questions that carry the owner's feedback and the review card after 2026-12-31 are the Chief of Staff's (plan §4, §6), and by the owner's ruling (decisions/P-003/stop-5.md) they are not built in this spec. - **AC13** [trace: V6 → AC13] — Monitoring (the plan's "monitoring criterion", §6): when anyone runs the one rule on the records as they stood on the main branch at a past commit, at a moment they choose, then it gives, for each decision card in queue/ at that commit, its id, its raise time as recorded (or "not recorded") and the state the rule gives it at that moment. If those records cannot be got, it gives the AC16 or AC17 flag instead. It reads nothing else: no clock of its own, no network, nothing stored. It also runs on records from before M4 was live, so the Chief of Staff can run the count once on past records before the Launch gate (plan §6). Run at the check moments J6 names, it gives V6's held-too-long count and, beside it, a count of cards held a day after they reached the main branch. J6 checks each card at every moment from its due time onward at which its state can change, so a card that is `held` at any of those moments is found. A test runs the rule at J6's check moments on sample records holding three cards, works out J6's two figures from the states the rule gives, and checks them, and the id, raise time and state the rule gives each card:
  - (a) a card whose `card` entry reaches the main branch with its card file but, from clock skew, records a raise time almost a day later than the card's arrival on the main branch (J6's first worked example: arrival 2026-11-10 10:00, recorded 2026-11-11 09:00): reported as held a day after reaching the main branch, and not a V6 miss;
  - (b) a card raised and shown on time: neither figure reports it.
  - (c) a card raised before the part of the log that is read: its raise time is reported `not recorded`, it is shown at once, and neither figure reports it.

  What these counts can and cannot see. By the plan's meaning of "raised" (§8, the card's `card` entry), the V6 held-too-long figure can be other than 0 only if the rule's code on the main branch, at some commit after M4, holds a card past its recorded day. The rule as M4 builds it never does that (AC4, AC7), so that figure guards against a later change to the rule. The second figure finds holds that a skewed or malformed raise time causes (case (a)). By the plan's words it is not part of V6 (open question 2). Neither count finds V6's first half, a card shown with neither mark. Every shown state the rule gives carries one of the two marks, so a view that drops the mark, or shows a card the rule did not list, leaves no sign in the records. That half is guarded by the tests of J4 and AC11 before the fix goes live, not measured after it (Risks; open question 4).

### Notifications
