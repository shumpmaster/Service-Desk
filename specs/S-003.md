## The defect
Owner's ruling record: decisions/questions/P-001-card-recommendation.md ("Proceed with the fix"; "Well then we need to make sure the cards don't show up until then."; "Show it after a day, flagged (Recommended)"; then "A: own addition now (Recommended)"), quoted in the plan's Part 2 and header. The record is not in this definer's pack, so this spec takes its words from the plan's quotation; the plan review read the record at source and found all four quotations match it (reviews/S-003/plan.md, finding 5).

What goes wrong today: a decision card (`dor-fail`, `stop` or `failure`) shows on the desk as soon as it is in queue/, and its Recommended mark comes only from its own section 5. The Chief of Staff's recommendation lives in a separate notes file, `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<n>-notes.md`, which the desk never reads (plan §2, citing research/P-003-sizing-findings.md §2). Seen twice: decisions/Q-019/stop-1.md and decisions/Q-017/stop-2.md were answered `re-specify` where the notes recommended `drop` (plan §2, citing the sizing findings §4).

## Acceptance criteria (scenarios; owner-approved)
Each criterion is a plain scenario traced to V6: *decision cards shown to the owner with neither the Chief of Staff's recommended button marked nor the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, 0, from the day M4 is live to 2026-12-31*. A V6 miss is also any decision card held off the queue more than a day after it was raised (plan §6).

"The records" below means queue/, the notes files and the dispatch log as they stand on the main branch at the read; nothing else (no memory of earlier reads, nothing kept on a device) decides whether a decision card is held or shown, or which word it shows. A card's **age** at a moment is that moment minus its raise time as recorded in its `card` entry, never reset or adjusted; "raised less than a day ago" means an age under 24 hours, including a negative age (a recorded raise time later than the moment, J1), and "a day ago or more" means an age of 24 hours or more. What the notification sender keeps about the notices it has sent is not part of the records; it decides only whether a notice is sent (AC14), never a card's state.

### Holding and showing (the rule, D1, and what the owner sees, D2)
- **AC1** [trace: V6 → AC1] — When a decision card is in queue/ and its notes are not on the main branch, and it was raised less than a day ago, then the desk holds it: it is not on the owner's queue, not in Home's count of what needs the owner, not on the board, has no card sheet reachable from those views, and no notification is sent for it.
- **AC2** [trace: V6 → AC2] — When a held decision card's notes reach the main branch with a recommended word, then on the open desk's next read the card shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board; its card sheet says the Chief of Staff recommends that word, and that word's button, and only that one, is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent then.
- **AC3** [trace: V6 → AC3] — When a decision card's card sheet shows, then its own section 5 marks no button, whatever word it names: on a decision card only the notes' recommended word marks a button. (Gate cards keep today's behaviour, AC9.)
- **AC4** [trace: V6 → AC4] — When a decision card was raised a day (24 hours) ago or more by its `card` entry in the dispatch log and still has no notes on the main branch, then it shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board, its card sheet carries the mark "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet", and no button is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent then. If the desk is open as the day passes, the card shows on the desk's next read, without a reload.
- **AC5** [trace: V6 → AC5] — When a card shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark later gets notes with a recommended word on the main branch, then on the desk's next read the mark is gone and the card shows the notes' recommended word with that word's button marked Recommended, as in AC2. No second notification is sent for it.
- **AC6** [trace: V6 → AC6] — When a decision card's notes are on the main branch but have no recommended word (no `Recommendation:` line; no backticked word on that line; or no backticked word on it that is one of the card's words), then the desk treats the card as having no notes: held under AC1 within its first day, shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark under AC4 after it, and never with a button marked.
- **AC7** [trace: V6 → AC7] — When a decision card's raise time is not recorded (no `card` entry for it in the part of the dispatch log the desk reads, or the log not read in that read), then the card shows at once, as in AC4, with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, or as in AC2 if its notes with a recommended word are already on the main branch.
- **AC8** [trace: V6 → AC8] — When notes for a decision card exist only on a branch other than the main branch (for example in an open pull request), then the desk treats the card as having no notes.

### Unchanged
- **AC9** [trace: V6 → AC9] — When a card is a gate card (`dor`, `criteria` or `launch`), an owner question, or a pull-request card, then it shows, counts and notifies exactly as before this fix, with its Recommended mark from its gate case as today, and the desk does not look for notes for it.
- **AC10** [trace: V6 → AC10] — When the desk reads notes, then it reads them through the repository reads it already makes, writes nothing to any repository, and sends nothing to any new outside service; the desk's spend stays $0 (plan §1 Out, §5).

### The views and the sender agree, from the records alone
- **AC11** [trace: V6 → AC11] — When the queue, Home's count, the board, the card sheet (including the hand-written one in src/public/sandbox.js) and the notification sender are given the same records and the same moment, then each decision card's state, held, shown with a recommended word (and which word), or shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, is the same in all of them, because each runs the one rule on those records and that moment and on nothing else. The same records and moment always give the same state, whatever was read before: a desk open for hours, a desk just opened, and the sender all agree. (Whether a notice is sent is not a card's state; AC14 and AC15 cover it.)

### After graduation: running, monitoring, feedback and value review
*The desk has graduated (docs/PROJECT.md §3, Launch-5). By the owner's ruling (decisions/P-003/stop-5.md) this spec keeps to the desk's code: the Chief of Staff's weekly value check for V6, its reports of misses, the end-of-year value review card, the feedback route, and the operating notes on M4 are moved to the plan and the Launch gate, outside this spec. What stays here is what the desk's code must do so that those can be run.*
- **AC12** [trace: V6 → AC12] — Running: when M4 is live, then it needs no new setting, credential, schedule or step from the owner or any agent; the desk reads the notes on the reads it already makes, on its existing schedule.
- **AC13** [trace: V6 → AC13] — Monitoring: when anyone later runs the one rule on the records as they stood at a past moment (the main branch at a commit, and that moment's time), then it gives the state each decision card had on the desk at that moment, so V6's two counts (cards shown with neither mark; cards held more than a day after being raised) can be worked out from the records alone, with no log kept by the desk or the browser. A test runs the rule on sample records at chosen moments and checks the states. Notifications are outside this: V6 does not count them, and they cannot be worked out from the records, so AC14's test checks them instead.

### Notifications
- **AC14** [trace: V6 → AC14] — When the notification sender runs at a series of moments, each on the records as they stand at that moment and keeping between runs what it keeps live about the notices it has sent, then each decision card gets exactly one notice, at the first run at which the rule gives it a shown state, and no other: none while it is held, none when it moves from `shown, no recommendation yet` to `shown, recommended <word>` (AC5), and none when it goes back to held and later shows again. What the sender keeps is used only to decide whether to send; it never changes a card's state (AC11). A test runs the sender over a chosen sequence of records and moments, including a card that shows, goes back to held and shows again, and counts the notices per card.
- **AC15** [trace: V6 → AC15] — When a notice has been sent for a decision card and the card then goes back to held (J3: its notes removed from the main branch within its first day, or a read with the log finding it raised less than a day ago after a read without the log showed it), then the sender withdraws nothing and sends nothing more; opening that notice while the card is held opens the card sheet saying the card is waiting for the Chief of Staff's notes, with no answer buttons (J4), not a message that the card is gone; and once the card shows again, the same notice opens it as shown. (Today a sent notice whose card is no longer listed re-checks as "gone": reviews/S-003/plan.md, finding 2, citing research/P-003-sizing-findings.md:67 and notifyview.js:337. See open question 5.)

## Join sheets
- **J1 —** queue/ and the dispatch log on the main branch → the rule (D1): for each card file `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md` (for example `queue/Q-003-failure-1.md`), its kind from `<gate>` and its raise time from its `card` entry in the dispatch log the desk already reads (plan §7). Decision card if `<gate>` is `dor-fail`, `stop` or `failure`; gate card if `dor`, `criteria` or `launch`. Awkward cases: a `dor-fail` card must not be read as a `dor` gate card by matching the prefix `dor` (it is a decision card); no `card` entry, or the log not read (AC7: shown at once); two `card` entries for one card (the earliest counts); a recorded raise time later than the moment of the read, from clock skew (age is always measured from the recorded time and never reset, so a negative age counts as less than a day: the card is held until the recorded time plus 24 hours, then shown; for example, recorded 2026-11-10 10:00 and read at 09:58 that day → held; read at 2026-11-11 09:59 → held; read at 2026-11-11 10:00 → shown; the same records and moment give the same answer everywhere, AC11); a recorded raise time far in the future, from a malformed entry, follows the same rule, so it is held until that time plus 24 hours (open question 4). The exact field names of the log's `card` entry are those of the desk's existing log reader in src/lib/ (research/P-003-sizing-findings.md §1); the Builder states them in the J1 test. A test marked `join-test: S-003/J1` covers it.
- **J2 —** the main branch's file tree and a notes file → the rule (D1): whether `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<n>-notes.md` exists on the main branch for card `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md`, and if so its recommended word: the first backticked word on the file's first line containing `Recommendation:` that is one of the card's words (the words its buttons offer), matched exactly, with any bold (`**`), italic (`*`, `_`) or heading (`#`) marks around the line ignored. Examples, for a card whose words are `drop`, `re-specify`, `resubmit`:
  - `Recommendation: \`drop\`` → `drop`
  - `**Recommendation:** \`drop\` — the scope is gone` → `drop`
  - `## Recommendation: \`re-specify\`` → `re-specify`
  - `Recommendation: not \`proceed\`; \`drop\`` → `drop` (`proceed` is not one of the card's words)
  - `Recommendation: \`Drop\`` → none (not an exact match), so no notes (AC6)
  - `Recommendation: drop` (no backticks) → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - no `Recommendation:` line → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - two `Recommendation:` lines → the first line decides; if it has no recommended word, none (the second is not read)
  - notes for `stop-2` beside a card `stop-1` → not this card's notes
  A test marked `join-test: S-003/J2` covers it.
- **J3 —** the rule (D1) → the one card list every view reads: each decision card carries one state, `held`, `shown, recommended <word>`, or `shown, no recommendation yet`, worked out from the records and the moment of the read alone (AC11); nothing about earlier reads is carried. Gate cards, questions and pull-request cards carry no new state. Awkward cases: a card answered while held (it leaves the list as any answered card does); notes arriving in the same read as the day passing (the notes win, AC2); notes removed from the main branch after a card showed with their word (the state is worked out afresh: `shown, no recommendation yet` if it was raised a day ago or more or its raise time is not recorded, `held` if it was raised less than a day ago); a card shown at once because the log was not read, and then found in the next read's log raised less than a day ago with no notes (it is `held` again; see open question 3). A test marked `join-test: S-003/J3` covers it.
- **J4 —** the card list → the views (queue, Home's count, board, card sheet in src/public/sandbox.js and its generated copies): `held` cards appear in none; `shown, recommended <word>` cards show the word in the card sheet and mark that button Recommended; `shown, no recommendation yet` cards show the mark and mark no button. Awkward cases: a card sheet opened from a direct link to a held card, or from a notice sent before the card went back to held (AC15) (it says the card is waiting for the Chief of Staff's notes, with no answer buttons); the card sheet left open while the card moves from `shown, no recommendation yet` to `shown, recommended <word>` (the mark gives way on the next read). A test marked `join-test: S-003/J4` covers it.
- **J5 —** the card list → the notification sender: the sender runs the same rule on the records (src/notify/collect.mjs already runs the desk's card-making code outside the browser), and sends per AC14: one notice per decision card, at the first sender run where it is not `held`; a change from `shown, no recommendation yet` to `shown, recommended <word>` sends nothing, and a card that goes back to `held` and shows again is not notified a second time. Handed over: the card list (each decision card's id and state) at that run; the sender adds its own record of the card ids it has already notified, which is never an input to the rule. How the sender keeps that record today is not known to this spec (Facts, known limits): the Builder names in the J5 test what it keeps and where, and the test checks the behaviour of AC14 and AC15 whatever the mechanism. If what it keeps today cannot remember a card across a shown → held → shown cycle, the fix to it is a change to the sender's own files and needs the owner's approval (plan §4). Awkward cases: the sender's read of the log failing (AC7 applies: it shows and is notified once); a card that shows and is answered between two sender runs (no notification is owed); a notice already sent for a card that goes back to `held` (nothing withdrawn, nothing sent; opening it shows the waiting card sheet, AC15). A change to the sender's own files needs the owner's approval (plan §4). A test marked `join-test: S-003/J5` covers it.

## Risks and mitigations
- A card held for good — AC4 (a day) and AC7 (unrecorded raise time shows at once); J1 measures age from the recorded raise time and never resets it, so clock skew delays a card only by the skew. A recorded raise time far in the future would hold a card until that time plus a day; open question 4 asks the owner about it.
- A button marked that the Chief of Staff did not recommend — exact matching in J2 and AC6 (anything unclear counts as no notes); AC3 stops section 5 marking a decision card.
- The owner told twice — AC14 and J5, one notification per card, even if the card goes back to held and shows again.
- A notice sent for a card that then goes back to held, pointing at something that looks gone — AC15: nothing withdrawn or resent, and the notice opens the card sheet saying the card waits for the Chief of Staff's notes; open question 5 asks the owner whether to withdraw it instead.
- A gate card changed by mistake — AC9 and J1's `dor-fail` / `dor` case.
- The views or the sender disagreeing — AC11: one rule, on the records and the moment alone; nothing kept from earlier reads.
- A card appearing and then vanishing — S-002's "once shown, not withdrawn" rule is removed by the owner's ruling, since it rested on what one desk had seen. It can now happen only if notes are removed from the main branch within a card's first day, or if a read without the log showed a card at once (J3). Accepted as following from the records; open question 3 asks the owner about the second case.
- The owner leaning on the mark without reading the reasons (automation bias "cannot be prevented by training or instructions", library/facts/F-automation-complacency-01.md, B) — the card sheet still shows the card in full; the mark only names the Chief of Staff's word. Accepted, as the owner asked for the mark.

## Open questions for the owner
1. AC5: when a card shown as "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" later gets its notes, this spec sends no second notification (so the owner is not told twice). Should it instead send one saying the recommendation has arrived?
2. J2: a recommended word must match the card's word exactly (`Drop` does not mark `drop`); a near miss counts as no notes. Is that strictness what you want?
3. AC7 and J3: the plan shows a card at once when the dispatch log was not read. Under your ruling that only the records decide, such a card can go back to held at the next read if the log then shows it was raised less than a day ago with no notes, and the sender, if its log read failed, may notify a card the desk is holding. Keep the plan's rule as written (never hidden, at the cost of a card sometimes vanishing), or should a failed log read hold nothing back and change nothing until the log is read? The second would need a change to the plan's section 1, which only you can rule.
4. J1: a card's age is counted from the raise time written in the dispatch log, so a small clock error delays it by only that error. If the log ever carried a raise time far in the future (a malformed entry), the card would stay held until that time plus a day, longer than the day you allowed. This spec keeps the one rule, since the plan defines "raised" by the log entry. Should a raise time more than a day ahead of the current time instead count as not recorded, so the card shows at once (AC7)? That would avoid a long hold, but such a card could then go back to held as the time came within a day of the entry.
5. AC15: when a notice was already sent for a card that then goes back to held, this spec leaves the notice alone and makes it open the card sheet saying the card waits for the Chief of Staff's notes. Is that what you want, or should the sender withdraw the notice and send a new one when the card shows again (which means telling you twice)?
