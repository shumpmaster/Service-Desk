claim: "Guidance and evidence put the number of colour-coded categories people handle well at about six to seven. The EU data visualisation guide: 'Seven is a good maximum'. A 2026 IEEE TVCG study (three experiments, multi-class scatterplots, 2 to 15 classes): 'if the category number is smaller than six, people tend to perform well at all tasks'; 'When category numbers increased, performance fell', with the size of the drop depending on task and encoding. The study used scatterplots; applying it to legends or timelines is inference."
