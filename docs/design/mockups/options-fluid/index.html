Milestones as goals, not steps

Your answers on the due-date order:

"So looking at design as an example - I see 2.1/2.2/2.3… and the design milestone is all over the place. This is where my brain gets thrown off."
"Yes, maybe the issue is that I’m picturing the fluid milestones as rigid fixed steps."

A milestone is an outcome the phase must reach ("Done defined in real scenarios"), and several can be worked at once. So in both options below, nothing numbers them as steps any more: the 2.1, 2.2, 2.2.1 and 2.2.2.5 go from milestones, sprints, plans and the map, and each goes by its name only. They keep the due-date order and the drag bars from the last page. Dates and reasons are still examples. Below: that page's option A as it is, then two live phones.
