- Phones: Home (`#o=A.at=home.light`); The project (`#o=A.at=proj.light`); The stop sheet (`#o=A.at=sheet.light`)

## F. A plain / detailed switch

One switch in More. Plain applies A, B and E and hides the numbers, the session tags and the records; Detailed is v43. Tapping it reloads the phone in that mode.

- Cost: Every screen would be drawn and checked twice, and all the plain text of A, B and E is still needed. You would mostly sit in Detailed, so the phone you hand over would usually fail your test unless you switch first.
- Touches: Hides the numbers and session tags in Plain: questions 1 and 3.
- Phones: The switch in More (`#o=F.at=menu.light`); Home, plain (`#o=F.at=home.light`); The project, plain (`#o=F.at=proj.light`)

## Recommended: A + B + E + D, without C or F

A turns each headline into a sentence built from its Why, so the Why line of C would mostly say the same thing again at twice C’s height; B answers “what is this project” and “what is this piece of work”; E makes the one decision on the phone readable; D costs nothing and introduces the cast. - Cost: Height: home’s list 383 to 467 points (+84, the project lines); the 12 rows of S-011 1,224 to 1,423 (+199, about 17 a row); the two sprint headings 102 to 180 (+78). With the map card folded, as here, the stop row sits about three rows further down than in v43.

1. Codes and numbers behind the plain name, in grey? Touches: Round 8 answer 22 (the event line on home) and round 6 answers 13 and 14 (numbers that follow the order, “M6 is now 2.2”). The numbers stay; only their place and weight change. Recommended: Changed.
2. “Your words” as a plain sentence, with the key you tapped beneath? Touches: Round 8 answers 8 and 9: a decision carries your words. Here your words stay, as the key you tapped, under a plain sentence of what it means. Recommended: Changed.
3. A plainer session tag, “Conversation · 14 Nov”? Touches: Round 8 answer 6, item 9 (each tag names its session, “CoS 14 Nov”). Still one tap to the conversation. Recommended: Changed.
4. The Why sentence on the row? Touches: The Why tag that opens a bubble (v21) and round 8 answer 14 (fewer tags per row). Not recommended with A, which already builds titles from the Why. Recommended: As it is.
5. Should the page say the workers are AI? Touches: No ruling yet. Your test is a stranger handed your phone, and nothing on the page says the Chief of Staff, the orchestrator or the spec writer are AI sessions. Recommended: Changed.
