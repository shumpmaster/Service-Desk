What's on this screen, and why

  • One card per project, as in Abode. Card contents, top to bottom: its icon, its name, where it is in plain words, the phase checklist, who has the ball, and one pill for the main action.
  • The phase checklist follows your reference (saved as 08 and 09 in PR #61). It lists the desk's four phases in order, then Done: finished ones are ticked, and the one in progress has a gold edge and the word "now". Operate and Review are not on it: they are the product's own once it is done. The figure is a real count ("2 of 4"), not a percentage; phases aren't equal in size, so a percentage would overstate. Service Desk's Build is ticked while Design is under way because the project came back to Design after Build, so Design reads "Design i2", its second run. A finished project gets the DONE stamp. A project whose phases the desk doesn't know shows empty grey rows, not guesses.
  • The pill is that project's queue, summarised. It is coral when something waits on you ("2 to merge", "1 decision"). It is green with "All clear" when nothing does, and red when the desk can't read the project. Tapping it opens that project's queue (screen 6). Nothing from one project appears on another project's card.
  • Tap the card to go into the project (screen 2). Tap the ⓘ for what the project is, why it exists, what it means for you and what you can do, in plain words.
  • The coral panel at the bottom is Abode's "Start typing…", used to give your Chief of Staff direction. You pick the project first, so you never cross boundaries. It opens GitHub with your request already written, the same two taps as answering.
  • Top line: how many things wait on you in total, and when the desk last checked, so you can tell fresh from stale.
  • Every figure is real. "Right now" is today's actual state: PRs #59 and #60 are waiting for your merge. "8 Oct, 19:30" is the decision that waited 2 h 8 min. Garden planner is a made-up project, shown only to demonstrate the problem state.
