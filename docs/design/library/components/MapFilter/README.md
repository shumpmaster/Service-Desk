# MapFilter

A quick filter for the branch map, a small glass button in the map card's top corner. Asked for by the owner on 26 November 2026 (decisions/questions/P-005-design-round-8.md, answer 16: "Can you add a quick filtering option for the branch?"); option A of docs/design/mockups/options-r8-filter-home/, taken in answer 19 ("Go with the recommendations on the others").

- **The button** sits inside the card's inner box (`map-card-pad` from the corner, MapCard's rule), glass with the card's hairline edge, 30 high with a 44 hit area. With nothing picked it is the funnel alone; with a cut picked it names it in the selected blue with a cross that clears it.
- **The panel** opens under the button: six cuts with their counts, Everything, Yours, Turning points (stops, decisions, merges, gates and what waits on you), Reviews and checks, Research, Main only. Picking one closes it.
- **What is left out** goes faint on the map (14%) and quiet in the list (32%); the pointer, the arrow and the landing carry on.
- Lane names beside the button step left to keep clear of it; the right gate's caption steps down.
- **v41 (round 8, answer 23: "When things are filtered I would like the table to adjust and show me only that filtered view."):** the list shows only what a cut matches. Rows left out are hidden, not dimmed; the phase, milestone and sprint headings that hold matches stay as context, each with a small count of what is hidden under it ("9 hidden"); headings with no match fold away, and a plan's actions hide with their plan. When nothing open matches, one line says so and how to see everything. The map still fades what is left out. The row at the pointer keeps its place as rows go and come back (from the landing, "Yours" keeps Stop card 5 at the pointer); clearing with the cross brings the whole list back the same way. Hiding takes the fold's close (240) and showing its open (320), on the standard curve; reduced motion snaps. What each station is stays known across the map's redraws, so the map keeps to the phase in view. Rows shown per cut on S-011's landing: Everything 12 events, Yours 4, Turning points 4, Reviews and checks 4, Main only 1, Research none open.
- **v44 (round 8, answers 27 and 28):** the cuts read plainly (Your decisions, Big moments, Only what was merged); BranchToggle sits below them; the note says what is left out is hidden from the list and faint on the map.
