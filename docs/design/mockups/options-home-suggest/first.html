Home: three suggestions

You asked whether v14's home page really works, and I said not yet. It shows each waiting thing three times, pushes Needs you down the first screen, and its dots carry little you can act on. You asked for suggestions:

"Make some suggestions"

Each one is drawn live on the real home page, with only the desk's real records: no example activity. Phases use v13's gauge, which counts milestones reached and turns yellow when something waits on you. Tap a project to open it, and tap a waiting row to see it. Earlier pages on the home map: the map options.

As it is

v14: the desk map on top

  • The map opens large, above Needs you, and folds as you scroll.
  • Each waiting thing shows as a badge, a pop-up and a row.
  • The dots and loops before today are examples.

Option A

Needs you first, then quiet project rows

  • Needs you stays as it is, at the top.
  • Projects become plain rows, not cards: the current phase's gauge, the name with its waiting count, the one line, the phase's milestones, and when it last moved.
  • No map. Calm, and it scales to many projects.

Option B

As A, with a slim strip of what mattered

  • Each project row adds a thin two-week strip under it.
  • The strip shows only the events that matter: merges as dark dots, stops and gates in red, questions in yellow, now in blue. Real events only.
  • It keeps a little of the map's sense of time without a separate map.

Option C · recommended

What waits, grouped by project

  • One sentence first: "5 things wait on you, in 2 projects. 3 projects are quiet."
  • Each project with something waiting is one group: the project row, with its waiting rows under it. Each thing is shown once.
  • The quiet projects fold into one row. Tap it to see them.
  • It answers "what needs me, and where" in one look, and stays short however many projects there are.
