What an event needs

The first half of your last point:

"some events ultimately are requirements for other events. I want to see that somehow."

The desk's records don't yet say which event needs which, so every requirement below is an example, and so are three planned events added to the top of S-011 (Spec revised, round 4; Plan review, round 4; S-011 frozen). They are there because a requirement matters most before an event happens. Marks keep the soft colours: grey check met, yellow waits on you, red blocked, an empty ring not yet.

Each phone opens on the timeline project, on S-011. Scroll inside it and tap. Tapping a requirement takes you to that event, opening what holds it. Why things happen, the second half of your point, comes next, on its own.

Option A

A Needs line on every event

  • Under each event: "Needs", then each requirement as a small chip with its mark. "Unlocks" follows where a later event needs this one.
  • Always in view, so you read it as you scroll, but it adds a line or two to nearly every event, done ones included.

Option B

Trace on tap

  • An event with a requirement wears a small link tag, tinted and counted when something is unmet ("Needs 3").
  • Tap the event: what it needs and what it unlocks stay lit, joined to it by a thin line, the rest fades, and a card lists what is missing. Tap again, or the cross, to stop.
