Question (questions/Q-006-vendor-sources.md, now closed): do two Cloudflare pages count as two
sources for Cloudflare's own limits?

A, as the card put it: any vendor, its own product. For facts about how a vendor's own product
behaves (its limits and behaviour), two different pages of that vendor's documentation count as two
sources, labelled "same publisher". Claims about cost, risk or quality still need an independent
source. This widens ruling A on Q-005 (Anthropic pages only) to every vendor.
