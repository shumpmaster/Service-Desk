# Ruling needed: how the desk's timeline work starts

Milestone: none (no project item or milestone exists for the timeline yet; this question decides how it starts)

WHY: You want the desk's "Waiting on you" side redrawn as one timeline of everything the agent team does, read like a CI, starting from the basis you locked on 9 November. The desk has graduated, so new work comes back only when you raise it, and the Chief of Staff proposes the route for you to approve. The model allows one route for an addition like this: a new Discovery, scaled to the work. If you don't answer within 72 hours, nothing starts: the timeline waits (C) until you choose.
OPTIONS:
  A. A new Discovery now, with a plan of its own — it becomes its own project item (P-005). The basis is its starting brief, not a finished design, and changes only on your word; Discovery and Design still run in full. Cost: more paperwork than B (a project request and a plan document of its own), and your attention through Discovery and a Design gate while the card fix and the widget are also in progress. It rules out waiting for the card fix.
  B. A new Discovery now, as later work in the desk's first plan — its milestones are added to that plan, marked for this item. Less paperwork. Cost: two of the timeline's starting checks, what success looks like and what is in and out of scope, would show as not recorded, because the timeline would share the first plan's brief instead of having its own. The model meant this route for a fix or a small addition, and this is a redesign of a whole screen. It rules out judging the timeline on its own value target and scope, unless you later rule to move its milestones into a plan of its own.
  C. Hold it until the card fix passes its Build gate — so only one new piece of your desk work is in Discovery or Design at a time, besides the widget. Nothing is spent now; the timeline waits for as long as that gate takes. It rules out working on the timeline in the meantime. After that gate the route comes back to you to choose; nothing starts by itself.
RECOMMENDATION: A — the basis is detailed enough to make a short Discovery quick, and a plan of its own lets the work be judged on its own value target and scope, as the card fix already is.
