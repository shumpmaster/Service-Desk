## Acceptance criteria (scenarios; for the owner's approval on the scenario card, not yet approved)
Each criterion is a plain scenario traced to V6: *decision cards shown to the owner with neither the Chief of Staff's recommended button marked nor the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, 0, from the day M4 is live to 2026-12-31*. A V6 miss is also any decision card held off the queue more than a day after it was raised; a card not shown because a read could not get the records is not a miss (plan §6).

"The records" below means queue/, the notes files and the dispatch log as they stand on the main branch; nothing else (no memory of earlier reads, nothing kept on a device) decides a decision card's state or which word it shows. A **read** is the desk's or the sender's reading of those records. A card's **age** at a moment is that moment minus its raise time as recorded in its `card` entry, never reset or adjusted; "raised less than a day ago" means an age under 24 hours, including a negative age (a recorded raise time later than the moment, J1), and "a day ago or more" means an age of 24 hours or more.

A **baseline run** is a run of the desk's code, or of the notification sender, as it stands at the build's **base commit** (the main-branch commit the build starts from, before any change for this fix), on inputs a test sets; its outputs are recorded in a file under tests/desk/, committed on the build branch before any change under src/, with the base commit named in the file. "As today" and "as before this fix" below (AC9, AC14, AC15, AC16, AC17) mean: equal, for the same inputs, to that recorded output, and the test for the criterion compares against it. What is known of today's behaviour second-hand is said where it is used, but the recorded output, not that account, is what the check holds the build to.

### Holding and showing (the rule, D1, and what the owner sees, D2)
- **AC1** [trace: V6 → AC1] — When a decision card is in queue/ and its notes are not on the main branch, and it was raised less than a day ago, then the desk holds it: it is not on the owner's queue, not in Home's count of what needs the owner, not on the board, no view offers its answer buttons (a direct link or a notice to it opens what the desk opens today for a card not on its list, AC15), and no notification is sent for it.
- **AC2** [trace: V6 → AC2] — When a held decision card's notes reach the main branch with a recommended word, then on the open desk's next read the card shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board; its card sheet says the Chief of Staff recommends that word, and that word's button, and only that one, is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent at the sender's next run (AC14).
- **AC3** [trace: V6 → AC3] — When a decision card's card sheet shows, then its own section 5 marks no button, whatever word it names: on a decision card only the notes' recommended word marks a button. (Gate cards keep today's behaviour, AC9.)
- **AC4** [trace: V6 → AC4] — When a decision card was raised a day (24 hours) ago or more by its `card` entry in the dispatch log and still has no notes on the main branch, then it shows on the queue, in Home's count and on the board, its card sheet carries the mark "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet", and no button is marked Recommended. Its notification is sent at the sender's next run (AC14). If the desk is open as the day passes, the card shows on the desk's next read, without a reload.
- **AC5** [trace: V6 → AC5] — When a card shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark later gets notes with a recommended word on the main branch, then on the desk's next read the mark is gone and the card shows the notes' recommended word with that word's button marked Recommended, as in AC2. Notification: as AC14 says.
- **AC6** [trace: V6 → AC6] — When a decision card's notes are on the main branch but have no recommended word (no `Recommendation:` line as J2 defines it; no backticked word on that line; or no backticked word on it that is one of the card's words), then the desk treats the card as having no notes: held under AC1 within its first day, shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark under AC4 after it, and never with a button marked.
- **AC7** [trace: V6 → AC7] — When a read gets the dispatch log on the main branch, as the desk's existing log reader reads it, and the log has no `card` entry for a decision card (for example a card older than the part of the log that reader reads), then the card's raise time is not recorded and the card shows at once: as in AC4, with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, or as in AC2 if its notes with a recommended word are on the main branch. (A read that did not get the log at all is not this case: AC17.)
- **AC8** [trace: V6 → AC8] — When notes for a decision card exist only on a branch other than the main branch (for example in an open pull request), then the desk treats the card as having no notes.

### Unchanged
- **AC9** [trace: V6 → AC9] — When a card is a gate card (`dor`, `criteria` or `launch`), an owner question, or a pull-request card, then it shows, counts and notifies exactly as before this fix, with its Recommended mark from its gate case as today, and the desk does not look for notes for it. A test runs the desk's card-making code and the sender on sample records holding each of these kinds, with every read getting the records and with each of the three reads failing in turn (queue/, a notes file, the dispatch log), and checks that what they give for these cards (on the list or not, Home's count, the marked button, any line shown, any notice) is equal to a baseline run on the same records and failures.
- **AC10** [trace: V6 → AC10] — When the desk reads notes, then it reads them on the read path it already uses, from the repositories it already reads, with the access it already has and on the schedule it already keeps. Whether a notes file exists comes from the file tree it already reads. The one new kind of read is the content of a notes file: one read for each decision card in queue/ whose notes file is in that tree. The sizing findings name this a new record kind with a new read per card with notes (research/P-003-sizing-findings.md:28-29, as the plan review read it). It writes nothing to any repository, uses no new credential and sends nothing to any new outside service; the desk's spend stays $0 (plan §1 Out, §5). A new read of this kind does not breach this criterion. It is breached by a read of a repository the desk does not already read, a new credential, a write, or a call to a new service.

### The views and the sender agree, from the records alone
- **AC11** [trace: V6 → AC11] — When the queue, Home's count, the board, the card sheet (including the hand-written one in src/public/sandbox.js) and the notification sender are given the same records and the same moment, then each decision card's state, held, shown with a recommended word (and which word), or shown with the "No Chief of Staff recommendation yet" mark, is the same in all of them, because each takes the card list the one rule makes from those records and that moment and from nothing else. The same records and moment always give the same state, whatever was read before: a desk open for hours, a desk just opened, and the sender all agree. A read that did not get some of the records gives no state to the decision cards it cannot decide (AC16, AC17) and, to the ones it can (AC17), the state a full read gives; never a different one. (Whether a notice is sent is not a card's state; AC14 and AC15 cover it.) The moment is always given to the rule, never read from a clock inside it, since the desk and the sender never run at the same instant: a test hands the views' card-making path and the sender's the same sample records and one injected moment, at several moments (before, at and after a card's raise time plus 24 hours), and checks that every decision card's state is the same in all of them.

### After graduation: running and monitoring
*The desk has graduated (docs/PROJECT.md:127, Launch-5). The weekly V6 count, its reports of misses, the questions that carry the owner's feedback and the review card after 2026-12-31 are the Chief of Staff's (plan §4, §6), and by the owner's ruling (decisions/P-003/stop-5.md) are not built in this spec. What is here, and in AC18 and AC19 below, is what the desk's code and the Builder's handover notes must give so that they can be run.*
- **AC12** [trace: V6 → AC12] — Running: when M4 is live, then it needs no new setting, credential, schedule or step from the owner or any agent. The notes reads of AC10 run on the desk's existing reads and schedule, so they start and stop with them.
- **AC13** [trace: V6 → AC13] — Monitoring (the plan's "monitoring criterion", §6): when anyone runs the one rule on the records as they stood on the main branch at a past commit, at a moment they choose, then it gives, for each decision card in queue/ at that commit, its id, its raise time as recorded (or "not recorded") and the state the rule gives it at that moment, or the AC16 or AC17 flag if those records cannot be got. It reads nothing else: no clock of its own, no network, nothing stored. It runs on records from before M4 was live as well, so the Chief of Staff can run the count once on past records before the Launch gate (plan §6). Run at the check moments J6 names, this gives V6's held-too-long count and, beside it, a count of cards held a day after they reached the main branch. Neither count is taken only at the moment the rule turns a card to shown. J6 checks each card at every moment from its due time onward at which its state can change, so a card found `held` at any of them is found. A test runs the rule at J6's check moments on sample records holding three cards, then works out J6's two figures from the states the rule gives, and checks them:
  - (a) a card whose `card` entry records a raise time a day after its card file reached the main branch: reported as held a day after reaching the main branch, and not a V6 miss;
  - (b) a card whose card file reaches the main branch twelve hours before its `card` entry, with the raise time recorded at the later moment: shown at once (AC7), then `held` once the entry arrives; reported as held a day after reaching the main branch, and not a V6 miss;
  - (c) a card raised and shown on time: neither figure reports it.
  The test also checks the ids, raise times and states the rule gives, including for a card with no recorded raise time (reported `not recorded`, shown).

  What these counts can and cannot see, said openly. By the plan's meaning of "raised" (§8, the card's `card` entry), the V6 held-too-long figure is other than 0 only if the rule's code on the main branch, at some commit after M4, holds a card past its recorded day. The rule as M4 builds it never does that, by AC4 and AC7. So that figure guards against a later change to the rule, not against the records. The second figure is the one that finds holds the records cause (cases (a) and (b)). By the plan's words it is not part of V6 (open question 2). V6's first half, a card shown with neither mark, is found by neither count. Every shown state the rule gives carries one of the two marks, so a view that drops the mark, or shows a card the rule did not list, leaves no sign in the records. That half is guarded by the tests of J4 and AC11 before the fix goes live, not measured after. This is carried to the Launch gate as a risk (Risks; open question 4).

## Join sheets
- **J1 —** queue/ and the dispatch log on the main branch → the rule (D1): for each card file `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md` (for example `queue/Q-003-failure-1.md`), its kind comes from `<gate>` and its raise time from its `card` entry in the dispatch log the desk already reads (plan §7). It is a decision card if `<gate>` is `dor-fail`, `stop` or `failure`, and a gate card if `dor`, `criteria` or `launch`. The exact field names of the log's `card` entry are those of the desk's existing log reader in src/lib/ (research/P-003-sizing-findings.md §1); the Builder states them in the J1 test. Awkward cases:
  - A `dor-fail` card must not be read as a `dor` gate card by matching the prefix `dor`: it is a decision card.
  - The log is got but has no `card` entry for the card: shown at once (AC7).
  - The read does not get the log: shown only if its notes with a recommended word are on the main branch (AC17).
  - queue/ is not got: no decision card gets a state (AC16).
  - Two or more `card` entries for one card. The pick is the rule's own, made in src/lib/ and not left to the reader: the rule takes the earliest raise time among the entries the existing log reader returns for the card, whatever order it returns them in. If the reader returns only one of them, that one is the raise time. AC13 and J6 report the same pick, so the desk, the sender and the count agree. The J1 test includes a log with two entries for one card and states which of them the reader returns. The plan does not settle this pick (open question 6).
  - A recorded raise time later than the moment of the read, from clock skew. Age is always measured from the recorded time and never reset, so a negative age counts as less than a day: the card is held until the recorded time plus 24 hours, then shown. For example, recorded 2026-11-10 10:00: read at 09:58 that day → held; read at 2026-11-11 09:59 → held; read at 2026-11-11 10:00 → shown. The same records and moment give the same answer everywhere (AC11).
  - A recorded raise time far in the future, from a malformed entry, follows the same rule, so the card is held until that time plus 24 hours (see Risks).
  A test marked `join-test: S-004/J1` covers it.
- **J2 —** the main branch's file tree and a notes file → the rule (D1): whether `decisions/<item>/<gate>-<n>-notes.md` exists on the main branch for card `queue/<item>-<gate>-<n>.md`, and if so its recommended word. The notes' `Recommendation:` line is the first line of the file that begins with `Recommendation:` once any leading heading marks (`#`, then spaces) and any bold or italic marks (`**`, `*`, `_`) at its start are removed. A line that only mentions `Recommendation:` later in its text is not it. The recommended word is the first backticked word on that line that is one of the card's words (the words its buttons offer), matched exactly. Examples, for a card whose words are `drop`, `re-specify`, `resubmit`:
  - `Recommendation: \`drop\`` → `drop`
  - `**Recommendation:** \`drop\` — the scope is gone` → `drop`
  - `_Recommendation:_ \`resubmit\`` → `resubmit`
  - `## Recommendation: \`re-specify\`` → `re-specify`
  - `Recommendation: not \`proceed\`; \`drop\`` → `drop` (`proceed` is not one of the card's words)
  - `Recommendation: \`Drop\`` → none (not an exact match), so no notes (AC6)
  - `Recommendation: drop` (no backticks) → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - an earlier prose line `The Recommendation: line below says \`resubmit\`` followed by `Recommendation: \`drop\`` → `drop` (the prose line does not begin with `Recommendation:`)
  - no line beginning with `Recommendation:` → none, so no notes (AC6)
  - two lines beginning with `Recommendation:` → the first decides; if it has no recommended word, none (the second is not read)
  - notes for `stop-2` beside a card `stop-1` → not this card's notes
  - a notes file listed in the tree whose content the read could not get → not "no notes": the read has not got the notes (AC16)
  A test marked `join-test: S-004/J2` covers it.
- **J3 —** the rule (D1) → the one card list every view reads. Each decision card the read can decide carries one state, `held`, `shown, recommended <word>` or `shown, no recommendation yet`, worked out from the records and the moment alone (AC11); nothing about earlier reads is carried.
  - A read that did not get queue/ or the notes gives the list no decision cards and the flag `decision cards not read: <queue/ | notes>` (either or both, AC16).
  - A read that got queue/ and the notes but not the dispatch log gives the list only the decision cards whose notes with a recommended word are on the main branch, each `shown, recommended <word>`. If any decision card was left out, it adds the flag `decision cards without notes not read: dispatch log` (AC17).
  - Gate cards, questions and pull-request cards carry no new state.
  Awkward cases:
  - A card answered while held: it leaves the list, as any answered card does.
  - Notes arriving in the same read as the day passing: the notes win (AC2).
  - Notes removed from the main branch after a card showed with their word: the state is worked out afresh. It is `shown, no recommendation yet` if the card was raised a day ago or more or its raise time is not recorded, and `held` if it was raised less than a day ago.
  - A card file on the main branch before its `card` entry is: shown at once (AC7). If the entry then arrives with a raise time less than a day before the read, the card is `held` until that time plus 24 hours, as the records say.
  These last two are the only ways a shown card goes back to held. A failed read (AC16, AC17) takes a card off the list without giving it a state. A test marked `join-test: S-004/J3` covers it.
- **J4 —** the card list → the views (queue, Home's count, board, card sheet in src/public/sandbox.js and its generated copies):
  - `held` cards appear in none, and no view offers their answer buttons.
  - `shown, recommended <word>` cards show the word in the card sheet and mark that button Recommended.
  - `shown, no recommendation yet` cards show the mark and mark no button.
  - The `decision cards not read` flag shows its line or lines on the queue and on Home (AC16), and the `decision cards without notes not read` flag shows its line there (AC17), each with the exact words those criteria give.
  Awkward cases:
  - A card sheet opened from a direct link to a held card, or from a notice sent before the card left the list: it opens what the desk opens today for a card not on its list (AC15).
  - A card sheet left open while the card moves from `shown, no recommendation yet` to `shown, recommended <word>`: the mark gives way on the next read.
  - A card sheet left open when a read fails: it shows what the desk shows today for a card not on its list (AC15).
  A test marked `join-test: S-004/J4` covers it.
- **J5 —** the card list → the notification sender: the sender takes the same card list (src/notify/collect.mjs already runs the desk's card-making code outside the browser, docs/PROJECT.md:588), so a held decision card is not on the list it notifies from, and a shown one is. Handed over: the card list at that run (each card's id, kind and state, and the flags of AC16 and AC17 if the read did not get the records). Nothing in the sender's own files changes; its existing way of not repeating a notice applies to decision cards as to any card (AC14). Awkward cases:
  - The sender's read does not get queue/ or the notes: no notice for any decision card on that run; gate cards as today (AC16).
  - The sender's read does not get the log: notices as usual for cards whose notes with a recommended word are on the main branch, none for the rest (AC17).
  - A card shows and is answered between two sender runs: no notification is owed.
  - A notice was already sent for a card that leaves the list: nothing is withdrawn, and nothing is sent while it is off the list (AC15).

- **J6 —** the rule (D1) → the Chief of Staff's V6 count (AC13). Handed over, for one commit of the main branch and one moment: a list with one entry per decision card in queue/ at that commit, giving its id (`<item>-<gate>-<n>`), its raise time as recorded in the dispatch log (ISO 8601, or `not recorded`), and its state (`held`, `shown, recommended <word>` or `shown, no recommendation yet`). If the records at that commit cannot be got, the list carries the AC16 or AC17 flag instead, as on the desk.

  How the count uses it, set down in the operating notes (AC18). Terms, all taken from the main branch's history:
  - **The main line** is the main branch's first-parent history, so a commit made on a pull-request branch counts from when it reached the main branch.
  - **The commit current at a moment** is the latest commit on the main line whose committer time is at or before that moment.
  - **A card's arrival** is the committer time of the first commit on the main line whose tree holds its card file.
  - **A card's check moments, from a due time**, are the due time itself, plus the committer time of each later commit on the main line that changes queue/, any notes file, the dispatch log or the rule's code in src/lib/. They stop at the first commit whose tree no longer holds the card file. Between two such commits only time passes, and the passing of time can turn a held card to shown but never the reverse (J3), so a card `held` at any moment from the due time on is `held` at one of these moments.
  - At each check moment the rule is run on the records at the commit current then, using the rule's code as it stood at that commit. For a commit before M4 was merged, the code as M4 merged it is used.
  Figures:
  - **V6 held-too-long miss** (plan §6, with "raised" as plan §8 gives it). The due time is the recorded raise time plus 24 hours. A card is a miss if its state is `held` at any of its check moments. A card with no recorded raise time at a check moment is not `held` there (AC7).
  - **Held a day after reaching the main branch**, reported with V6 but not counted in it (open question 2). The due time is the card's arrival plus 24 hours. The card is reported if its state is `held` at any of its check moments.
  - **No-mark miss**: a card shown with neither mark. Every shown state the rule gives carries one of the two marks, so a count from the rule never finds one. A view that shows a card off the list, or drops its mark, is not seen by this count; J4's and AC11's tests guard against that before launch, and V6's first half is not measured after it (AC13, Risks, open question 4).
  Worked examples:
  - A card file arrives 2026-11-10 10:00 and its `card` entry records 2026-11-11 09:00, from skew; no notes. For V6, the due time is 2026-11-12 09:00, when the age is 24 hours, so the card is `shown, no recommendation yet`. No later commit holds it, so it is not a miss. For the second figure, the due time is 2026-11-11 10:00, when the age is 1 hour, so the card is `held` and is reported.
  - A card raised and arriving 2026-11-10 10:00, with no notes. At 2026-11-11 10:00 it is `shown, no recommendation yet`, so neither figure has a miss. If a commit on 2026-11-14 changed src/lib/ so that the rule holds the card, it would be `held` at that commit's time, which is a V6 miss.
  Awkward cases:
  - A card answered before its due time: its card file has left queue/, so it has no check moments and is not a miss.
  - A check moment at which the records cannot be got: the list carries the AC16 or AC17 flag. That is a failed read, not a miss (plan §6), and it is reported as a failed read.
  - A raise time far in the future: never a V6 miss while the rule is unchanged, since at its due time the card is shown. The second figure reports it once a day has passed since the card's arrival.
  - A card with no recorded raise time: shown at once, so never a V6 miss. The second figure reports it only if a `card` entry arrives later and holds it past a day from its arrival (J3).
  - Two `card` entries: the rule's pick (J1), the same as the desk's.
  - A committer time that is wrong, or a deploy that lags the main branch: see Risks.
  A test marked `join-test: S-004/J6` covers the handed-over list's form and the first worked example and case (c) of AC13 at their check moments; it may be the same test as AC13's.

## Risks and mitigations
- A card held for good. AC4 (a day) and AC7 (no recorded raise time: shown at once) stop this. J1 measures age from the recorded raise time and never resets it, so clock skew delays a card only by the skew. A malformed raise time far in the future would hold a card until that time plus a day. By the plan's meaning of "raised" (§8) that is not a V6 miss, so V6 does not count it. J6's second figure (held a day after reaching the main branch) finds it a day after the card reached the main branch, and the Chief of Staff can report it. Accepted, since the plan defines "raised" by the log entry; open question 2.
- The count finding nothing because it is run at the rule's own boundary. This was the second plan review's blocking finding. J6 checks every moment from the due time on at which a state can change, and it runs the rule's code as it stood on the main branch at each moment. Even so, while the rule is the one M4 merged, the V6 held-too-long figure is 0 by construction: the plan's "raised" is the field the rule itself counts from. That figure catches a later change to the rule. The second figure catches holds the records cause. AC13's test shows the second figure finding two such cards. Said openly in AC13 and AC18; open question 2.
- A card hidden while a record cannot be read. AC16 and AC17 show a line on the queue and Home naming what could not be read, so it is never silent there. Cards with a recommendation still show while only the log is missing (AC17), and the cards return on the first read that gets the records. This is not a V6 miss (plan §6), so a long outage would not show in V6; the line is its only sign, and AC18 tells the operating team what it means.
- A card silently missing from the board and from notices while a read fails. The "not shown" lines are on the queue and Home only, as the plan's §1 gives them; the board and notices carry none (join-up review). The owner would hate "a card silently missing" (plan, Intent). Mitigation: Home and the queue, where the owner looks for what needs them, carry the line. A board line could be added later without touching the sender, and a notice line would need the owner's approval for a sender change. Open question 5.
- One notes file that cannot be fetched hides every decision card (AC16, J2's last case), as the plan's "cannot get ... the notes" reads. This is wider than it need be, but it keeps one rule, and the line says so. Open question 3.
- A button marked that the Chief of Staff did not recommend. Exact matching in J2 and AC6 means anything unclear counts as no notes. J2's line rule stops a prose mention deciding the word, and AC3 stops section 5 marking a decision card.
- The owner told twice. No notice is sent while a card is held or not shown (AC1, AC16, AC17). For a word arriving later, or a card that leaves the list and shows again, the sender behaves as today (AC14); open question 1.
- A notice for a card that then leaves the list, opening to "gone". This is rare (J3: only notes removed within a card's first day, a `card` entry reaching the main branch after its card, or a failed read), and the card shows again by the rules. Accepted, AC15.
- A gate card changed by mistake. AC9 and J1's `dor-fail` / `dor` case guard against it.
- V6's first half not measured on the live desk. The count from the rule (AC13, J6) cannot see a view that drops the mark or shows a card the rule did not list, since that leaves no record. Mitigation: J4's and AC11's tests check every view against the rule before launch, and the operating notes (AC18) say what the counts cover. Carried to the Launch gate as a risk; open question 4.
- J6 trusts committer times and reads the main branch's code rather than the live deployment. A wrong commit clock shifts the second figure by the error. A deploy that lags the main branch, or a rollback (AC18), means the live desk may run other code than J6 reads. The operating notes say so. The live deploy is the owner's ruling, so the Chief of Staff knows when it lags.
- A baseline recorded from the wrong code. The baseline runs (AC9, AC14 to AC17) are only a fair check if they come from the base commit. The file names the base commit and is committed before any change under src/, so the Reviewer can see both on the branch.
- The views or the sender disagreeing. AC11 makes this one rule, on the records and the moment alone; a partial read gives no state, or the same state, never a different one (AC16, AC17).
- The build passing the stop rule. The restated size is near it on tests. The Builder reports lines and tests against the stop rule, and the stop rule applies unchanged.
- The owner leaning on the mark without reading the reasons (automation bias "cannot be prevented by training or instructions", library/facts/F-automation-complacency-01.md, B). The card sheet still shows the card in full, and the mark only names the Chief of Staff's word. Accepted, as the owner asked for the mark.
