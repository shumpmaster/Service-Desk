2. The empty and can’t-read states

In v48: no empty or can’t-read state is drawn. Home always has things waiting; a project is shown as read, never as being read or unreadable; the only error states are the Late and Stopped banners behind More’s “preview the feed”; and every visit shows “new since you last looked” marks, with no first visit.Touches: The design pack’s checklist 4, Quiet is honest: “does the screen never say “Quiet” while a project is unread or can’t be read, showing “Checking…” or “Can’t read since HH:MM” instead?” (S-001 AC4, AC5; PROJECT.md intent, “Missed decisions”). R8 answers 24 and 26: home is a priority list of your actions. Checklist 5: an unknown is shown in words, never as a value or zero.The records: The words of these states, the 9:14 am time and the calm and first-open homes are examples: no record carries a read failure, a last-looked time or a welcome. Recommended: One way each; none had a real choice. Home with nothing waiting keeps its shape and says so in one calm line. Checking dims what was last read under a shimmer instead of blanking it. Can’t read says since when, what it means for you (something waiting might not show) and the two things to do, on home and on the page, and the project leaves the quiet fold.
