- 1.4.3 (AA): text at least 4.5:1; large text at least 3:1, large = at least 18 point or 14 point bold.
- 1.4.11 (AA): UI components and graphical objects at least 3:1 against adjacent colours; graph lines are an example.
- 1.4.1 (A): "Color is not used as the only visual means of conveying information, indicating an action, prompting a response, or distinguishing a visual element."
