# Ruling needed: Which days are the first 14 days after launch?

Milestone: M3B
Asked in session: session_017seyaGqUxAff6E8dKWHDCR

WHY: Three of your targets run "over the first 14 days after launch, 2026-11-25", and upkeep "at most 30 minutes a week after launch, 2026-11-25". The project document also says the date is "V4's date plus 14 days". For an 11 November launch that can be read three ways, and the sheet shows the window's days and "day 3 of 14" right under those lines, so it should use one. It decides whether cards raised on launch day count. Left unruled, the window is the 14 days after launch day.
OPTIONS:
  A. 12 to 25 November — the 14 days after launch day, as the project document puts it; both "14 days" and "25 November" hold; cards raised on launch day, while the address is moving over, aren't counted; on launch day itself the row and the sheet say "Measured from 12 November", the response-time line shows the last 14 days' figure labelled "Launch day", and from then "day 1 of 14".
  B. 11 to 24 November — launch day and the 13 after it, exactly 14 days; launch-day cards count; the sheet's "First 14 days after launch, 11 Nov – 24 Nov" ends a day before the date in your targets.
  C. 11 to 25 November — launch day and the 14 after it, 15 days; launch-day cards count and it ends on your targets' date; the sheet says "day 3 of 15" and "Launch day and the 14 days after it", and the row "first 15 days".
RECOMMENDATION: A — it is what the project document says, keeps 14 days ending on 25 November, and doesn't half-count launch day.
RISK CLASS: R1   REVERSIBILITY: reversible   BLAST RADIUS: the days on the targets' sheet, the response-time figure for the window, and your Chief of Staff's checks
DEFAULT: A   TIMEOUT: 168
