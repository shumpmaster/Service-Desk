Taken from Linear, and kept ours

  • Taken: the white ground, thin rows and hairlines, the status glyph at the row's start, small grey section headings with a count, the large bold title with an icon pill at the right, floating rounded sheets, check-mark lists, snooze, the dark toast, the floating rounded bar.
  • Ours: Plex Sans and Plex Mono, vermilion for "needs you" only, our own glyphs (◆ needs you, ↩ kicked back, ✓ done), our words, and the bar's three tabs keep their labels, which Linear's icon-only bar drops.
  • One departure from the audit's brief: the floating bar, sheets and icon pill use a soft shadow, as Linear's do; the brief said no shadows.
